BENGALURU: Ratan Tata backed online platform for businesses to procure finished goods, Moglix, has raised Rs 28 crore from existing investors Accel partners Jungle Ventures and Seed Plus. Anurag Srivastava, founding and managing partner of Jungle Ventures will be joining the board of Moglix following this round.The company had also raised an undisclosed amount in funding from Ratan Tata in February this year and was selected for the SAP startup studio programme.Catering to the sourcing needs for Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs) in electrical products for the industry, fasteners, MRO and others, Moglix has a customer base of 20,000 and works with over 100 manufacturing houses. The company works with 1500 manufacturers and distributors on the selling side across India, China and Taiwan."We believe that technology can be a strong factor in propelling the efficiency and scale for both procurement and sales of the organizations. We are working with many of these companies to fast forward them to the digital age where technology can enable them to do tasks 3-5X faster," said Rahul Garg, CEo of Moglix.The company will utilize the current round of funding for expansion in Southern and Eastern zones.
