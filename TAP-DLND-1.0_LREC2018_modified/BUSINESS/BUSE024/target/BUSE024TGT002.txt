There’s no denying the fact that viable businesses, irrespective of their size, need funds to grow. However, most often lack of requisite collateral for loans become their stumbling blocks. Today NASSCOM Change Makers Summit has joined forces with Centre for Entrepreneurial Excellence (CEE) to bring to you women’s efforts & struggles in the startup ecosystem and providing them access to knowledge sessions, funding options, access to angel investor networks and accelerator and incubation support. The program along with CEE has also partnered with SonderConnect, and Run Skills Development Program to select 12 startups across India with women at the helm to get deep mentoring from the country’s foremost experts.

Ravi, Gururaj, NASSCOM said, “CEE, started by one of NASSCOM’s mentor and angel investor, Dr. Som Singh is playing an instrumental role in empowering entrepreneurs through fund support and training and we at NASSCOM are excited about this partnership. Together we can change the eco-system to be more sustainable.”

One of the esteemed speakers at the Change Makers Summit was Dr. Som Singh – Entrepreneur, investor & mentor who threw light on how women led tech startups can attain access to various Govt. schemes and uncollateralized funding options, get the right mentoring and actionable coaching. She said, “If you are a woman entrepreneur and thinking about the possibilities of getting loan without collateral, I say of course you can! There are several Govt. Yojanas/schemes and funds from where you can avail uncollateralized loans.”

Since its inception 3 years ago, Dr. Som has been an integral part of NASSCOM 10K as a mentor, investor and advisor. While as an investor, Dr. Som has invested in TookiTaaki and Explara which were both 10K accelerated companies, as a marketing advisor, she has also been the business strategist for most 10K warehouse startups. Apart from being a frequent presenter at NPC over the years, she not only conducts workshops at the warehouse, but is also intensely involved in the selection of startups and assists them in getting government funds through CEE to help businesses across various verticals scale and enhance their potential.

A few key highlights of the event were live Shark tank with selected start-ups pitching to a panel of angel investors and live pitch to Govt. bodies, incubators and accelerators in India.

Some of the funds that Dr. Som spoke about from where startups can avail un-collateralized loans are:

Start-up India plan – A government of India initiative exclusively for startups. The portal encompasses information with regards to various benefits being offered like funding and credit guarantee support, tax exemptions on capital gains for 3 year and on investments over fair market value.
SIDBI Growth Capital and Equity Assistance – The funds can be used for marketing, brand building, creation of distribution network, tech know how, R&D and software purchase.
SIDBI Revolving Fund for Technology Innovation (SRIJAN Scheme) – This plan provides financial assistance to MSMEs towards development, up scaling etc.
Lean Manufacturing Scheme for MSMEs – This plan helps to enhance the manufacturing competitiveness of micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs) by applying lean techniques to identify and eliminate waste in the manufacturing process.
Credit Linked Capital Subsidy Scheme (CLCSS) – for technology up-gradation of Micro and Small Enterprises for purchase of Plant & Machinery.
The overall goal of the summit is to support the community of women entrepreneurs currently working in the technology sector and help pave the way for those who want to enter the business. The objective of this event was to highlight efforts of women who have not let their hard work go futile and chased their dreams.

About CEE:

Centre for Entrepreneurial Excellence (CEE) was started with the fundamental idea of bridging the gap between entrepreneurs and policy makers. We are on our way to building a technology platform where key entrepreneurs can voice their opinion on a certain policy or requirements and it’s our aim to ensure they are being heard! BE HEARD is the mantra of this foundation. We are here to build awareness amongst entrepreneurs on ways they can leverage different policies, ask for grants and use funds that are available through government channels like DST, MSME, TIDE to name a few. We are committed to build an ecosystem that thrives through public good.
