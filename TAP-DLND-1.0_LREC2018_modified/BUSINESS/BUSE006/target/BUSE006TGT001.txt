New Delhi, Oct 3 (IANS) Casio India on Monday launched two lamp-free projectors with TCO certification that reduces the power consumption by up to 50 per cent.

TCO certification -- administered by TCO Development owned by the Swedish Confederation of Professional Employees -- keeps user friendliness, safety and products' eco-friendliness in concern.

Part of 'Advanced Series of Laser and LED Hybrid' projectors, the XJ-F200WN is priced at Rs 85,995 and the XJ-F210WN costs Rs 89,995.

"Casio's lamp-free projectors have already set the benchmark for environmental compatibility, cost effectiveness and quality standards among the education and business community of the country," said Kulbhushan Seth, Vice President, Casio India, in a statement.

The series comes with long life, high durability, and low-energy consumption while having a life of approximately 20,000 hours, ensuring no lamp replacement.
