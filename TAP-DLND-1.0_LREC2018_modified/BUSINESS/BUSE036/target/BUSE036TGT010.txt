Snapchat CEO Evan Spiegel believes his app isn’t for ‘poor countries like India’ 309 GQ Staff Apr 15, 2017 
Just a month ago, Evan Spiegel was the toast of the town. The 26-year-old billionaire who founded Snapchat took his company public. The following week, he walked home with a bonus of some $800 million. Everyone, including his fiance, supermodel Miranda Kerr, was celebrating. Cut to present when Evan Spiegel has earned the ire of Indians who have just learnt what he has to say about them. 
Thanks to a lawsuit that has been filed by former employee, Anthony Pompaliano, we now know that the Snapchat founder has no plans of expanding his business in India because he considers it to be a poor country. “This app is only for rich people. I don’t want to expand into poor countries like India and Spain,” Spiegel reportedly told Pampliano. According to the lawsuit, the Snpchat CEO believed that the app was only for ‘rich people’. 
The much-controversial statement was made in 2015 but has come into the public eye thanks to the Pampliano, who has sued Snapchat for having presented inflated metrics about user data to the investors when it was going public. 
Obviously that hasn’t stopped Indians from being outraged. And it wasn’t long before #uninstalsnapchat became a trending topic on Twitter
