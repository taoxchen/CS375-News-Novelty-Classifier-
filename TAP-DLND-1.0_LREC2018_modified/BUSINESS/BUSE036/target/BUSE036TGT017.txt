Many youngsters in India have downloaded Snapchat on their smartphones and the installation of the app is growing at a decent pace day by day. Despite this, the CEO of this app feels that India is ‘too poor’ for the company to consider expanding user base. Evan Spiegel, the CEO, made this point during a meeting to discuss the growth of the app’s user base in 2015, reported by Variety. 
An employee of Snapchat raised concern about app’s slow growth in Indian market which is growing digitally strong, the CEO cut the employee in between saying, “the app is for the rich.” He further added that” I don’t want to expand Snapchat in India and Spain.” As per some reports, Snapchat has around 4 million users in India. This was an unverified report of 2016, and since then the number must have increased and after making this comment he left the place. 
Anthony Pompliano, the employee, who has filed a lawsuit against the app has accused the company of misleading its investors and providing overrated statistics about user data. 
The employee Anthony Pompliano, who is currently engaged in a lawsuit against Snapchat after he accused the company of misleading investors by providing inflated statistics about user data, said Spiegel stormed out of the meeting after making this comment.
