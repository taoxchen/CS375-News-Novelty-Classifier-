Snapchat CEO has miffed a lot of Indians who are a part of the app. And that includes a lot of Bollywood celebs too, who have been using the platform. Evan Spiegel, founder of the photosharing app has come under scrutiny for calling India a ‘poor country’. We contacted Neha Dhupia and asked her to comment on the same (as she is an avid Snapchat user). The diva EXCLUSIVELY told us that she refrains from using the app, unless the CEO apologizes for his derogatory comment. Neha said, “I know about this comment and I only realised it after I posted my last Snap story. So I think I’m going to refrain from using the app for a while, till he apologizes.” Looks like she’s boycotting the app just like many other Indians. 
According to a report by Variety, the comment was made by the CEO of Snapchat, Evan Spiegel, allegedly during a meeting to discuss the growth of the app’s user base in 2015. A former employee filed a suit against Spiegel for making such a statement and various other allegations have also been slapped at the CEO. Due to a certain dip in the Indian market, the employee had raised the concern to Evan. However, he cut him in the middle of his talk and said, “This app is only for rich people,” Spiegel said, Variety quoted the employee’s revelation. “I don’t want to expand into poor countries like India and Spain.”
