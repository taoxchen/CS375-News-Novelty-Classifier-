Britain is to follow the US and ban large electronic items from cabin baggage on flights from some destinations, according to news reports. Sky News said its sources claimed the ban would apply to 'most' electronic devices. The Telegraph also claimed its sources had confirmed that Britain would follow the US lead and ban electronic devices in hand-luggage on flights from the Middle East. The US Department of Homeland Security in the US announced a ban today after revealing that extremists are seeking 'innovative methods' to bring down planes. The ban by the US will affect flights by nine airlines from eight countries including Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Turkey and Egypt. The Telegraph said it had been told that UK security services have seen the same intelligence as their US counterparts and are expected to introduce a similar ban, possibly as soon as today. Discussions have been taking place in Whitehall over the past few weeks over how the ban will work and among Cabinet ministers, it said.
