
According to American media sources the US is set to ban electronic devices such as laptops and tablets from cabin baggage on flights from some Middle Eastern and North African countries. At present, it is unclear which airlines will be affected. US media reports say the move is led by intelligence obtained overseas. It will reportedly include all ‘large’ electronic devices such as cameras, electronic games, laptops and tablets, possibly everything larger than a mobile ‘phone. The US Department of Homeland Security (DHS) is expected to will make an announcement on Tuesday March 21.
