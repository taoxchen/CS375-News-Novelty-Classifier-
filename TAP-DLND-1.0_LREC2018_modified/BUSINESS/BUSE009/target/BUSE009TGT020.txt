
U.S. officials are barring passengers flying from certain airports in the Middle East and Africa from carrying on electronic devices larger than smartphones, according to multiple news reports Tuesday. Officials told CNN, Reuters[1] and Fox News that the indefinite restriction is based on an unspecified terrorist threat and will take effect Tuesday. CNN reported[2] that U.S. carriers will not be affected by the ban, which affects non-stop flights bound for the U.S. from certain countries.
