U.S. officials are barring passengers flying from certain airports in the Middle East and Africa from carrying on electronic devices larger than smartphones, according to multiple news reports Tuesday. Officials told CNN, Reuters and Fox News that the indefinite restriction is based on an unspecified terrorist threat and will take effect Tuesday. CNN reported that U.S. carriers will not be affected by the ban, which affects non-stop flights bound for the U.S. from certain countries. Fox News reported the rules apply to 13 foreign airports, and that the alert was caused by an increased volume of chatter by terrorist organizations. Royal Jordanian Airlines issued a statement Tuesday that devices such as tablets, laptops, DVD players and hand-held gaming devices must be checked with luggage. Cell phones and medical devices were exempted, the airline said. The increased security measures will also revive strict restrictions against carrying liquids on planes.
