With reference to an update on Import Alert 66-40 by US FDA on the Company's API Unit at Ankleshwar, Gujarat, announced that U.S. Food and Drug Administration ('USFDA') on 30 September 2016 has posted on its website an update on the above referred matter and excluded the product Ceftriazone Sodium from Import Alert. This will enable the Company to manufacture and sell Ceftriazone API and formulation to US market.
