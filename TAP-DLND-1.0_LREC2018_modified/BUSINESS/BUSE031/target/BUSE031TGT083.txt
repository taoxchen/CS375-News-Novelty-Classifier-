
Ride sharing service Uber has lost its senior VP of engineering, Amit Singhal, after he left the position for failing to reveal a sexual harassment allegation at his former employer, Alphabet 's Google ( GOOGL ) 
Uber CEO Travis Kalanick asked Singhal to resign Monday morning, as the executive did not disclose that he left Google a year prior after executives there informed Singhal of a "credible" allegation of sexual harassment by another employee, Recode reports.
