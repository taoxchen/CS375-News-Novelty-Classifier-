Uber’s senior vice president of engineering resigned Monday after just a month on the job, following claims that he had sexually harassed an employee while at Google. Amit Singhal was hired at Google in 2000, as employee No. 176. Early in his career, he helped rewrite Google’s search ranking algorithm, a distinction that earned him a Google Fellow award in 2006. He left the company in early 2016 to focus on philanthropy.
