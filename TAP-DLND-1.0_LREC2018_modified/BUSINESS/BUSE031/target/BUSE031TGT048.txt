Uber just lost one of its key executives. Amit Singhal, Uber's senior vice president of engineering, stepped down on Monday after the ride-hailing company discovered sexual harassment allegations had been lodged against him while he was employed at Google, Recode reported. He apparently didn't disclose this past tumult to Uber when he was hired. Uber says it didn't know about the allegations against Singhal when it hired him.
