CHINESE handset vendor Xiaomi has partnered Foxconn to set up its second manufacturing unit in India at Sri City in Andhra Pradesh .
Xiaomi entered India in July 2014 and started local manufacturing a year later. The new facility will be exclusive to Xiaomi, while the older one may see Foxconn making handsets for other vendors too. “ I cannot disclose the exact capacity. But we will be able to manufacture one phone per second whenever the lines are running. The two factories combined will provide employment to about 5,000 people, 90 per cent of which are women,” said Xiaomi India Head Manu Jain.
The move will help Xiaomi meet about 95 per cent of its demand in India from these two facilities. However, it will continue to import accessories, ecosystem products and its premium product, Mi5 from China .
According to research firm IDC, Xiaomi had a 10.7 per cent share of the Indian market, putting the company just behind Samsung ( over 25 per cent share) at the end of December quarter. The smartphone market in India will grow from 110 million units per annum to 200 million plus units, in 3- 5 years, Jain added.
The company has launched a new device - Redmi 4A - that will also be manufactured at the Indian facilities.
Priced at Rs 5,999 , the model will help the company compete in the sub Rs 7,000 category.
