Xiaomi has launched their entry-level Redmi 4A smartphone in India , for a price of mere $91. 
The device comes with a 5-inch 720p display and is powered by Snapdragon 425 . The phone has 2GB of RAM and 16GB of internal storage, expandable with a microSD card. 
The Redmi 4A features 13MP main camera with f2.2 aperture and a 5MP selfie shooter. Other features include 3,120 mAh battery and 4G dual-SIM support with VoLTE. 
The phone will be available in three colors - Gold, Dark Grey, and Rose Gold.
