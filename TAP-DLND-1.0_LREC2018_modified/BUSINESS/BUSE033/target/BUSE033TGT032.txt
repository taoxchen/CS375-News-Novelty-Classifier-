Chinese smartphone maker Xiaomi on Monday launched Redmi 4A in India at at Rs 5,999. 
Xiaomi Redmi 4A, successor to the Redmi 3 is the lightest Redmi phone ever weighing just 131.5 grams. 
The Redmi4 A will go on sale Amazon on March 23 March at 12 pm. Xiaomi India managing director Manu Kumar Jain tweeted: #Redmi4A goes on sale on https://t.co/lzFXOcGyGQ and @amazonIN at 12 noon on 23 March. Mark your calendars now! @RedmiIndia @XiaomiIndia (2)
