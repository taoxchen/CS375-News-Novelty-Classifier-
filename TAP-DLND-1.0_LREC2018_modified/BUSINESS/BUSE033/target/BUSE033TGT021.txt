Xiaomi launched Redmi 4A in India at a budget price of Rs 5,999. The budget device from Xiaomi will be available in two colour variants Dark Grey and Gold. Xiaomi Redmi 4A will be an Amazon and Mi.com exclusive smartphone. Let us take a quick look at the specifications:

1. A polycarbonate body with a metal finish
2. 139.9 x 70.4 x 8.5 mm dimension and 131.5 gm weight
3. 2GB RAM+16GB inbuilt storage
4. 5-inch HD display
5. Qualcomm Snapdragon 425 quad-core processor
6. Dual SIM slot that supports 4G connectivity
7. 13MP rear camera with LED flash
8. 5MP front camera
9. 3120mAh battery that supports fast charging facility, and
10. Android 6.0 Marshmallow.

It is said that Xiaomi launched the cheapest ever smartphone in India. The phone will go on sale for Indian customers from March 23, 2017
