 Xiaomi will launch the much anticipated budget smartphone Redmi 4A in India on Monday. 
Xiaomi Redmi 4A will be a successor to the Redmi 3. The phone will be an Amazon exclusive and is expected to be priced below Rs 6,000. 
Xiaomi India managing director Manu Kumar Jain tweeted: 20 March: In addition to a new Redmi, we'll also announce a major milestone in @XiaomiIndia 's India journey. 3 days to go! #TimeToGetSmarter
