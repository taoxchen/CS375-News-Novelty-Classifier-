Xiaomi launched its all new Redmi 4A smartphone in India with a price tag of Rs 5,999. The handset has been launched at an event in New Delhi and will be sold exclusively via Mi.com and Amazon India . It will be available from 12pm on March 23 only in Dark Grey and Gold colour variants. The Rose Gold colour will be available from April 6 across the country. In term of specifications, the Android smartphone features a 5-inch HD display with resolution 720x1280 pixels. It is powered by a 1.4GHz quad-core Snapdragon 425 SoC coupled with 2GB of RAM and 16GB of inbuilt memory space, which is further expandable up to 128GB via microSD card. As a highlighting feature, the handset is blessed with an IR blaster, which allows using the smartphone as a TV remote control. The optical part of the Dual-SIM smartphone packs a 13-megapixel rear camera along with a LED flash and f/2.2 aperture. A 5-megapixel front shooter with f/2.2 aperture is also provided by the company for selfie lovers. The handset lacks the availability of a fingerprint sensor and runs Android 6.0 Marshmallow-based MIUI 8 operating system. At the event, Xiaomi along with market share has claimed itself to be the biggest smartphone brand in terms of online sales. The company has 30 percent online market share and has committed to set up one more manufacturing plant in Andhra Pradesh, India, which will be owned and operated by Foxconn. After opening the second manufacturing plant, Xiaomi will be able to manufacture 1 smartphone per second. The Xiaomi Redmi 4A made its debut in November last year in China with a price tag of CNY 499 (Rs 5,000 approx) along with Redmi 4.
