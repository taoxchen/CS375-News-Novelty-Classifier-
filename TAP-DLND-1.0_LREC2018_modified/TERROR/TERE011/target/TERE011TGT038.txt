An air strike carried out by U.S. forces on Thursday in Idlib, Syria, killed several al-Qaeda militants, U.S. Central Command said in a statement. 
“Idlib has been a significant safe haven for al-Qaeda in recent years,” the statement said, making no mention of civilian casualties.
