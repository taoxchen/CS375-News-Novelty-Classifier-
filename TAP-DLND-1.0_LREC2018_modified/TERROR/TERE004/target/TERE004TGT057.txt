The Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport is busy this time of the year with people coming into town to ski. That includes a group NBC Montana met that flew in from Fort Lauderdale. They left Florida a few hours before the deadly shooting. 
It wasn't until they landed in Montana that they found out what happened 
"We got a bunch of texts. People knew that we were flying out of Fort Lauderdale, and then my wife of course just received a phone call," said John Jeffers. 
Jeffers is surprised something like that could happen in his town. 
"To have something like that happen in Fort Lauderdale is kind of disturbing," he said. 
NBC Montana went directly to the airport director to ask how they prepare in case a shooting happened here. 
"We always run through drills where we go through similar situations. We also try to anticipate what could be happening," said Brian Sprenger. 
Sprenger says mutual aid from local law enforcement agencies would be available. 
"It's as possible here as anywhere, but we hope that we'd respond very well, but it's a difficult situation for anyone," he said. 
NBC Montana checked and there were no direct impacts on flights at Bozeman Yellowstone International Airport. 
We talked to travelers to get their take on the recent shooting. 
Sara Call told us she won't live in fear. "I'm going to be vigilant, and I'm going to report things that I think should be reported. However, I'm angry that this kind of thing happens in our country," she said. 
Jeffers says he stays alert too. 
"You're definitely a lot more aware in today's environment as opposed to in the past," he said. 
For anyone made uneasy about Friday's shooting, the airport director recommends staying aware of your surroundings and being observant.
