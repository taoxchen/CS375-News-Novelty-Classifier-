Adam Purinton reportedly shouted racial slurs at two people he thought to be from the Middle East, is accused of killing one of them. The New York Daily News reported that Adam shouted “get out of my country” before opening fire, killing one man, and injuring the other. 
The former US Navy employee has been arrested for allegedly killing an Indian and wounding two others. 
The incident took place at Austins Bar and Grill in Olathe where Adam was said to be heavily drinking, allegedly began making the comments on the two Indians. In the incident, the 51-year-old also injured another man who tried to interfere. The third man is identified as Ian Grillot. 
Adam raised questions over their presence in the States and even asked how they are better than him. Fearing the situation might get worse, the Bar management had apparently asked Adam to leave the place. He did leave them and returned with a gun after a while, reported Telugu 360. 
Srinivas Kuchibhotla, an engineer at Garmin was killed and his colleague Alok Madasani was injured in the incident. According to reports, Kuchibhotla died at a local hospital and Madasani and Ian Grillot were hospitalized. 
According to Daily Mail, the deceased Kuchibhotla had a Master’s degree in electrical and electronics engineering from University of Texas, El Paso and in 2014 was picked by Garmin International to look after the company’s chopper programs. 
At least one witness has claimed that Purtinton yelled ‘get out of my country’ and also heard him screaming racial slurs before opening fire. 

Five hours after the shooting, Purinton was in a bar around 140 km’s away in Clinton, Missouri and told a barman that he killed two men from the Middle East and wanted a place to hide, upon hearing this the barman called the cops. The cops then spoke to Adam over the phone and convinced him to surrender. 
Purinton is being held at Henry County Jail and no charges have been levied upon him yet. He is a former Navy employee who dwells alone, people told Fox 4.
