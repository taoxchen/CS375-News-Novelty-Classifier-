A suspect has been arrested in Olathe restaurant shooting after a massive manhunt was launched by the police in Olathe, Kansas city. The gunman had yelled racial slurs and shot three people at about 7:15 p.m., local time, (8:15 p.m. EST) Wednesday, thus immobilizing almost the whole of southern Olathe, a local newspaper reported. The police said there were multiple victims in the shooting. 
Two of the three victims were of Middle Eastern or Indian descent, according to witnesses and the gunman was also yelling racial slurs just before the shooting, reports said. Two of the victims were in critical condition and one was seriously injured, Olathe Police Sgt. Logan Bonney said, according to Kansas City Star . Until four hours after the shooting, investigators were still inside the bar digging for evidences.


The gunman was believed to be a white man in his 50s who took shelter in a house near 155th and Lindenwood, which was surrounded by police late Wednesday night.
Tyler Lape, 23, whose friend was one of the victims, told the local newspaper he left the bar about five minutes before the shooting. 
Heavy police presence in nearby areas, with police in military-style vehicles and camouflage attire were seen. The suspect may have suffered from post-traumatic stress disorder, according to sources, the local newspaper reported.
