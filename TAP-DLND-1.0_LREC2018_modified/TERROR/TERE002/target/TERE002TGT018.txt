The Madhya Pradesh police have hinted that explosion in Bhopal-Ujjain passenger train this morning was an act of terrorists. 
An explosion had taken place in a coach of the train near Jabdi railway station at Kalapeepal in Shajapur district this morning. 
Inspector General-Law and Order Makrand Deoskar told media in Bhopal that three suspects have been detained from Piparia of the state. 
Meanwhile the police have trapped a militant in Uttar Pradesh. It is suspected that he may be involved in the train blast. 
At least 9 persons were been injured in the incident. The incident occurred in 59320 Bhopal-Ujjain passenger train in Madhya Pradesh’s Shajapur area on Tuesday. The incident took place at 9.50 am near the Jabdi station. 
The Home Minister of Madhya Pradesh, Bhupendra Singh said that all angles are being investigated. Passengers said that there was a suitcase which exploded and fire followed which triggered the panic. 
The Ralway police S P of Indore, Krishna Veni said that after initial reports of a short circuit, claims of a suitcase bomb were also received. 
Several teams of Railway officials, the Intelligence Bureau and Anti-Terrorist Squad were present at the site. The incident comes in the wake of several agencies probing sabotage angles in train accidents that have taken place across the country.
