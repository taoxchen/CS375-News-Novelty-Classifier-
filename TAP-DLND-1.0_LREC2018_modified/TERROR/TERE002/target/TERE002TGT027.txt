Six people were injured in an blast in the general coach of a Bhopal-bound passenger train near Kalapipal town in Shajapur district of Madhya Pradesh . 

According to reports, the blast happened on Tuesday morning at around 10 am in the first compartment of the 59320 Bhopal-Ujjain train when it was passing Jabdi station. 

Three compartments of the trains reportedly derailed after the blast. The reason behind the explosion could not be ascertained. 

"Injured passengers have been admitted to Kalapipal government hospital, medical team sent. The reason behind the explosion could not be ascertained," IA Siddiqui, PRO, Bhopal Division of Indian railways .
