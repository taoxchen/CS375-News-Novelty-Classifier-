Today an explosion reported inside the Bhopal-Ujjain passenger train in Shajapur district of MP. As per the sources more than 6 people got injuredwere injured in the blast. 
The explosion happened in general coach of train between, it was between 9.30 AM and 10 AM when train was heading to Ujjain from Bhopal. Jitendra Kumar Jayant the PRO said that all injured people were rushed to hospital in Kalapipal town of the district. 
The MP government announced Rs 25,000 compensation for the injured people and Rs 50,000 for those who are critically injured.As per Chief Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan, he is looking into this situation personally.
