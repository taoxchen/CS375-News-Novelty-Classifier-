A low-intensity blast was reported in a Bhopal-Ujjain passenger train on Tuesday morning. The train's number is 59320. 
The blast took place in the train's second-last coach, which was an ordinary second-class coach, Indian Railways PRO Anil Saxena said. Four to five passengers were injured and are undergoing treatment, he added. 
The incident occurred at arout 9:50 AM near Javdi station, close to Kalapipal town in Shajapur district. The train was stopped as soon as the blast was reported. 
Saxena said the ambulance reached the site of the blast within ten minutes, and that the last three coaches of the train have been detached. 
State civil police, railway police, forensic police, and the railway protection force have all reached the spot, which has been cordoned off, he added. 
Madhya Pradesh Home Minister Bhupendra Singh said an inquiry has been ordered into the incident to ascertain the nature of the blast. 
The preliminary inquiry hasn't revealed the cause of the blast. Multiple theories - including a mobile phone explosion and chemicals carried in a suitcase - are doing the rounds.
