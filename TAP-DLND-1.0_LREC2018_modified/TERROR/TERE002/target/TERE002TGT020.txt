One terror suspect was killed as anti-terror operation in Thakurganj area of Lucknow ended after almost 12 hours in the wee hours of Wednesday. 

IG ATS Aseem Arun said that the terror suspect, identified as Saifullah, was a member of the ISIS Khorasan module. "We used micro tube cameras for recon of the site, image was not clear hence thought there were 2 terrorists. Turned out only one," he added. 

Saifullah was leader of the group that triggered a low-intensity blast in Bhopal-Ujjain train in Madhya Pradesh on Tuesday morning, injuring 10 passengers. The morning blast on the Bhopal-Ujjain passenger train at Jabri railway station was the first instance of a terror strike by an IS module in India. 

Meanwhile, in an official statement released, the UP Police said three persons were arrested by the Madhya Pradesh Police in Piparia. Two of the arrested persons are from Kanpur, while one is from Aligarh. 

Those arrested are Danish Akhtar alias Zafar, son of Raees Akhtar and a resident of KDA Colony in Kanpur, Aatish Muzaffar alias Al-Qasim, son of Muzaffarul Haque Naqvi and a resident of Jajmau in Kanpur, and Syed Meer Hussain alias Hamza, son of Syed Ehsan Hasan and a resident of Aligarh.
