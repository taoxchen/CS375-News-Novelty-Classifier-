The National Investigation Agency (NIA) today filed a closure report in the 2007 Ajmer blast case, giving a clean chit to four suspects, including Sadhvi Pragya, RSS leader Indresh Kumar and Rajendra alias Samander (accused in Samjhauta train blast case). NIA Special Public Prosecutor Ashwani Kumar Sharma told the court that so far no prima facie case was made out because of lack of evidence against Indresh, Ramesh Venkat Rao Mahalkar, Sadhvi Pragya and Rajendra and they were not wanted for further investigation. “Two other accused — Jayanti Bhai alias Ustad and Ramesh Gohil alias Ghanshyam — had died in jail. So, there is no need for any comment or further investigation,” he said. On March 22, the Special NIA court Judge Dinesh Kumar Gupta had already awarded life imprisonment to Devendra Gupta and Bhavesh Arvind Bhai Patel in the case. “Two accused — Ramchandra Kalsangar and Suresh Nayak — are being declared absconder by another court after NIA efforts. As and when they will be arrested, NIA will produce them before the court,” Sharma said.
