Three metro stations have been closed, local media outlet Fontanka reported. 
Life News, a news outlet with close ties to Russia's security services, published pictures of blown-out train doors and injured people on a station platform. 
A source told news agency Interfax the blast involved a device filled with shrapnel. 
Interfax said 10 people were killed and 20 injured in the blast. 
Russian president Vladimir Putin has been told of the explosion, a Kremlin spokesman told RIA.
