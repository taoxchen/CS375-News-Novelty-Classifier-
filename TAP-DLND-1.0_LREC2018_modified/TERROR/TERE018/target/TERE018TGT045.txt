The twin blasts took place in a subway car at the Sennaya Ploschad metro, reports said. Russian news portal RT Online quoted Transit Systems management as saying that evacuation of passengers was on after the blast at Sennaya Square station. 
The door of a train coach was blown off, media images showed. 
Reports said that Russia President Vladimir Putin was in the city to take part in a meeting with the President of Belarus. However, no connection to the event has been established as yet. Putin has been briefed, Russian media reported. 
"All possible causes, including terrorism, are being considered," Putin told reporters. The Russian president also expressed condolences over the deaths. 
According to AFP , all the stations in St Petersburg, Russia's second largest city, were closed after the twin blasts.
