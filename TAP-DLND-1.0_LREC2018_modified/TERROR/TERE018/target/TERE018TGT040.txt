
At least 10 people were killed on Monday when an explosion struck an underground railway station in the city of St. Petersburg, Russian authorities said. 
Citing official sources, Russian news agencies said the explosion occured just before a train was due to pull out of the Sennaya Ploshchad metro station. 
Unverified images were shared on social media, purportedly showing the aftermath of the explosion. 
Authorities at the metro agency in St. Petersburg said the explosion seemed to have been caused by a device of some sort.
