Several local Russian media outlets , citing the National Anti-Terror Committee, reported at least 10 people were killed in the blast.  
The explosion occurred around the Technological Institute, and Sennaya Ploshchad stations, according to multiple reports by Russian state media. An unnamed municipal law enforcement official reportedly told the state-run Sputnik news service that there are injured and victims.  The metro's Blue line, where the blast took place, has been shut down. Unverified images of wounded people lying on the ground circulated on Russian social media, as well as videos of smoke-filled platforms.
