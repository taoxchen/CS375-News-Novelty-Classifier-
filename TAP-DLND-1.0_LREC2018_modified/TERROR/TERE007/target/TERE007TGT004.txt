
Three local rebels were killed during a night long gunfight with government forces in Pahalgam, south of Kashmir. 
Sources told Kashmir Dispatch that rebels were identified as Adil ahmed reshi from Vijbyor, Abid ahmad shiekh from Satkipora and Masood Ahmad Shah from Bewoora village. Locals tried to match towards the encounter spot and clashed with government forces there. Indian army said they have recovered three AK-47 rifles and other ammunition.
