Deputy Inspector General of Police Hyderabad Range Khadim Hussain Rind Thursday night informed that 60 people were martyred and 250 injured in the suicide attack at the Hazrat Lal Shahbaz Qalandar's shrine at Sehwan Town. 
Talking to media persons outside Taluka Hospital Sehwan, he said the condition of 50 injured persons was critical. 
He informed that after the blast, around 50 doctors had reached from Dadu along with sufficient quantity of medicines and started providing first aid to the injured. 
As a result of immediate response from the volunteers, the number of casualties did not rise, he added. 
The DIG informed that 30 critically injured persons were being shifted to Nawabshah where arrangements were made to provide required medical treatment. The armed forces helicopters were also reaching Sehwan to airlift the critically injured persons to Karachi, he added. 
A forensic laboratory was also reaching Sehwan to carry out forensic examination of the blast, he informed and confirmed that preliminary investigation revealed that a suicide bomber blew himself up.
