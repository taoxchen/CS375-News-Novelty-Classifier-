In Jammu and Kashmir, a policeman martyred and two terrorists were killed in an encounter at Tral in Pulwama district today. 
The encounter, which began last night, continued till early this morning. Security forces had cordoned off the area, where the terrorists were holed up, on the basis of a tip off. 
Police said, the slain terrorists belong to Hizhul Mujahideen and Jaish-e-Mohammed outfits and they were active in the area for the last three years. 
The police constable, Manzoor Ahmed Niak of Uri was killed, when he took on the terrorists head on during the operation. An army major was also injured in the operations.
