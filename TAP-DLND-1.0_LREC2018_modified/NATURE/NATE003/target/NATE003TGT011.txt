Some areas expecting up to 1 foot of rain A massive storm system swept across Northern California on Sunday, bringing the threat of severe flooding and avalanches, as the East Coast recovered from the first winter storm of 2017 that iced roads, dropped more than a foot of snow in some areas and caused at least three deaths. A chain of storms from the eastern Pacific, gathering moisture from the tropics, was aimed at central and Northern California and was expected to deliver up to a foot of rain and several feet of snow at higher elevations in the Sierra Nevada. The storm system could pound the region with precipitation through Wednesday, forecasters said. "We haven't had a winter like this in 10 years," said Tony Fuentes, a meteorologist with the National Weather Service in Reno, Nev. "There's a lot of rain coming through Northern California. Any kind of stream or river or creek or low lying areas can see some flooding."
