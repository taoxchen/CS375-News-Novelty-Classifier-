After several inches of rain fell across California, some communities experienced flooding on Sunday, January 8. 
Water rescues were made just before midday on Sunday when Rancho Adobo fire crews saved people trapped inside a motor home on Old Redwood Highway near Penngrove.
The California Highway Patrol also cited drivers for traveling on flooded roadways.
In Nevada, a state of emergency was declared ahead of what some experts are saying will be the worst flooding to hit the state in 20 years.
