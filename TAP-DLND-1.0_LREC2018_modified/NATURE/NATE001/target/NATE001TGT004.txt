An avalanche warning has been issued by Snow and Avalanche Study EST(SASE) for next 48 hours with medium danger existing for prone slopes above 2600 meter altitude in Kupwara and Baramula districts in Jammu and Kashmir. 
"Medium danger avalanche warning exists for avalanche prone slopes above 2600 meter altitude in Kupwara and Baramula districts," said in a statement.People have been advised not to venture in avalanche prone areas during above mentioned period.
