 
A boat turned turtle leading to the deaths of at least 24 people. 
At least 24 people are dead after two boats carrying about 40 people turned turtle in Ganga river in Patna on Saturday. There are three NDRF teams on the spot. The National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) Director General RK Pachnanda on Sunday said that bodies have been recovered so far and that three NDRF team are at the site to recover more bodies and the rescue operation is underway. 
"Search and rescue operation is underway. Three NDRF teams are at the spot. We are trying our best. We have deported three teams for the operation. It is extremely tragic incident. People were taking boats one after another. When the people realised that their boat was capsizing they tried to jump to the other boat then both the boats capsized due to over load," he added.
