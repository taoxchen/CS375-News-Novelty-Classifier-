Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Saturday night expressed grief over the death of at least 23 people in the Patna boat tragedy. PM Modi also extended his condolence to the bereaved families of the victims. 
“PM expressed grief on the loss of lives caused by the boat tragedy in Bihar. He extended condolences to the bereaved families,� the Prime Minister’s office tweeted. 
At least 23 people, which also included some minors were drowned when an ill-fated boat capsized on the Ganga river in Patna. Some reports suggest that the boat was overloaded. Around 11 people were rescued when the incident took place on Saturday evening at around 6 pm. 
Of those 11 people rescued six have been admitted at the Patna Medical College and Hospital. The vessel wich was carrying people for a free ride from the Ganga Ghat, was overcrowded and drowned at around 6 pm on Saturday. 
Some dramatic footage of the mishap was also clicked by people waiting at the shore. Rescuers were seen diving at the spot to save people while locals also approached at the mishap site to rescue people. 
Following the incident Bihar Chief Minister Nitish Kumar has asked Patna’s district magistrate to conduct a high-level enquiry. Nitish Kumar has also ordered his administration to provide Rs 4 lakh compensation to the families of the dead. The ruling party in Bihar has also cancelled tomorrow’s programme. 
According to some reports, the victims had gathered at Patna’s Ganga Diara to witness a three-day kite festival which is held every year during ‘Makar Sakranti’. After the incident further festivities near Ganga diara had been called off by the government.
