
Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Sunday announced ex-gratia of Rs 2 lakh for next of kin of those deceased and Rs.50,000 for those seriously injured in the Bihar boat tragedy. On Sunday morning, the death toll in the boat tragedy that took place in Chhapra reached 21.
At least 21 people, including some minors, drowned when Makar Sankranti festivities ended in a tragedy after the boat capsized on the Ganga on Saturday. Around 40 people were said to be onboard the country boat. 
 Rescue operation resumed on Sunday morning. Director General of the National Disaster Response Force RK Pachnanda said that three NDRF teams were on the spot.
The rescue operation were halted at night. Patna District Magistrate Sanjay Kumar Aggarwal told reporters that the NDRF, SDRF and civil and police officials would guard the area throughout the night and the divers would start fresh search for victims in the morning.
The DM said prima facie it appears that the boat capsized either due to overloading or collision with another country boat, mid-water. He said the tragedy occurred around 200 metres from Sambalpur diara (riverine) area on another side of Ganga which falls under Sonepur area of Saran district. 
Aggarwal said it has come to light that these people had gone for am unauthorised 'Disneyland fair' near Sambalpur diara area in Saran district and boarded the country boat to return to Patna side when mishap occurred mid-stream.
