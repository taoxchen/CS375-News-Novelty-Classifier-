After Bihar, tragedy seems to have now struck Uttar Pradesh. A boat has capsized in the Virma river of the Hamirpur region of the state. The incident has taken away the lives of two children and one woman. According to the available information, three people are deemed to be missing. It is not known whether there are any survivors, however, rescue and relief operations have been launched. The disaster comes right on the heels of the Bihar boat tragedy , wherein a boat returning from a kite flying festival on the occasion of Makar Sankranti capsized in the Ganga River of Bihar. The tragedy has resulted in the loss of lives of twenty-four people. Eight people were rescued from the boat and twenty-five swam to safety. A rescue and relief operation has been launched in Patna too. Meanwhile, political leaders across the country have expressed their condolences. The Prime Minister of India has also announced a relief measure of Rs 2,00,000 to the next of kin of the deceased from the Prime Minister’s National Relief Fund. Further, Modi has approved an ex-gratia sum of Rs 50,000 for those seriously injured in the boat tragedy in Patna. The Uttar Pradesh boat tragedy comes at a time when people had just begun questioning the safety of the Indian waterways. The cause of the tragedy remains unknown.
