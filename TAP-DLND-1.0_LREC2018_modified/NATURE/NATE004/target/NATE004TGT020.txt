The Tamil Nadu government today declared all districts in the state as drought-hit. Also, a series of announcements including waiver of land tax for farmers has been made by chief minister O Panneerselvam. In a statement here, he said that he had a detailed discussion with ministers and higher officials on Monday, based on reports submitted by committees of ministers and district collectors. The committees were set up by him last week. "All districts in Tamil Nadu will be announced drought-hit. Land tax will be waived off for farmers across Tamil Nadu. Tenure to pay farm loans will be extended. Since huge money needed to protect drought-hit people, a detailed memorandum will be prepared and sent to the union government, to seek the Centre's help," he said. Announcing relief to farmers who have faced crop loss more than 33 per cent, he said Rs 5,465 per acre will be given to paddy, Rs 3,000 per acre for 'manavari' crops, Rs 7,287 per acre for long time crops and Rs 3,000 per acre for other crops. He said crop cutting experiment will be carried out soon to enable farmers to get insurance claim. The CM said if the damage is 100 per cent, paddy farmers can get Rs 21,500 to Rs 26,000 per acre as compensation from insurance companies. Panneerselvam said the the duration under rural work guarantee scheme will be increased from 100 days to 150 days and works such as desilting of lakes and other water bodies will be taken up at a cost of Rs 3,400 crore. Stating that 17 farmers had commited suicide in the last two months due to drought, he said Rs three lakh each would be given as financial aid. He said more compensation would be announced after getting detailed report from collectors.
