Himachal Pradesh Chief Minister Virbhadra Singh on Sunday said the victims who lost their houses in the landslide in Mandi will be provided money to rebuild them . Singh said the rescue operation to evacuate people from the debris of the landslide will continue till the last body is recovered. At least 17 persons were confirmed dead and 45 people were reported missing in the landslide on the National Highway 21 in Mandi district of the state. Considering the gravity of the situation, National Disaster Relief Force and Indian Army were called in by the administration.
