At least 46 people were killed and over 18 are missing after a landslide buried two buses on the Mandi-Pathankot highway near Joginder Nagar in Mandi district of Himachal Pradesh on Saturday night. 
Sources said both buses had stopped at a roadside tea stall at Kotrupi near Padder in Mandi around midnight when the landslide swept everything in its way for about eight hundred meters and washed away a short stretch of the road. 
So far, all the forty-six bodies have been recovered with three dead from the first bus and 42 from the second, of which 23 have been identified. A biker was also killed in the calamity. 
The Jogindernagar-Mandi section of the Pathankot- Mandi National highway has been closed. 
The district administration has advised people to take the Mandi-Kullu route via Kalota. 
The two buses belonged to the Himachal Road Transport Corporation (HRTC). The air-conditioned bus carrying 47 passengers to Manali from Chamba, has also been retrieved now, said sources while the bus heading to Katra from Manali with eight passengers was wrecked. Three people died as it was the first to be hit by the landslide. 
Two of the deceased include bus driver Chandan Sharma and conductor Satpal. 
Mandi deputy commissioner Sandeep Kadam said, “Twenty four bodies were recovered and five rescued after a long operation. The death toll could rise as the search continues. Rescue teams were digging throughout the day to find survivors trapped in the rubble.’’ 
24 bodies were recovered by search and rescue teams comprising Army and National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) personnel and state administration. Five people were admitted to the Manali zonal hospital. 
The other deceased were identified as Rajesh of Dharamsala, Manju of Karsog, Sachvetra of Rampur, Anita of Mandi, Beauty Sharma of Patna, Bihar, Shubam of Pathankot and Rajesh of Dharamsala. 
Himachal Pradesh Transport Minister GS Bali said that according to information the bus was packed. Chief minister Virbhadra Singh visited the spot and announced an ex gratia of Rs 5 lakh to the kin of the deceased. 
Virbhadra Singh said rescue operations would continue until every body was found. He directed an assessment of loss to property at the earliest so that compensation and assistance could be provided. Singh also met the victims’ kin. 
Prime Minister Narendra Modi tweeted, "Pained by the loss of lives due to landslide related accidents in HP's Mandi district, my condolences with the families of the deceased.’’ 
This is said to be the third such big accident involving landslides and buses in the state. In 1994, 42 people were killed in a landslide at Luggar Hati in Kullu district and in 1988 around 45 passengers were buried in a landslide near Matiyana in Shimla.
