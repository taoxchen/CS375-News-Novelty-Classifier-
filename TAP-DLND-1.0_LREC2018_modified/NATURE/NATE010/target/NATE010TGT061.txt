Six people were confirmed killed and several feared trapped as a massive landslide hit Mandi district of Himachal Pradesh on Sunday. As per reports, the landslide occurred around midnight and damaged the Mandi-Pathankot National Highway 154 as well. Initial reports claimed several vehicles, including a bus is feared trapped in the debris on NH 154 at Kotrupi village of Padhar in the district. The injured persons were taken to a hospital. According to Hindustan Times , rescue operations were affected by rains. Indian Army personnel and members of the NDRF have been called for fast execution of the rescue operations. Apart from the locals, the police, fire services and home guard personnel were already on the spot carrying on the rescue operations. Hindustan Times quoted Mandi deputy commissioner Sandeep Kadam who said that ‘possibility of more deaths could not be ruled out.’ As per reports, National Highway 21 from Mandi to Aut has also been closed for traffic due to landslide threats.
