Eight people were killed and 23 others are feared dead after a landslide swept away two buses parked on the roadside on National Highway 154 in Himachal Pradesh on Saturday night. 
Sources said around midnight, the two buses had stopped for a tea break at Kotrupi near Padder in the Mandi district when the landslide struck the area. 
Most of the passengers were from Pathankot and Chamba. One of the buses was headed to Manali from Chamba with 47 passengers on board while the other was going to Katra from Manali with eight passengers. 
So far, eight bodies have been recovered by the search and rescue teams comprising members of the Army and the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF). Five people have been rescued and admitted to Zonal Hospital in Mandi. 
The rescue teams were finding it difficult to retrieve the remains of the Manali-bound bus, which rolled down 800 meters off the road and got buried under mud. 
Both buses were buried under a sea of mud, prompting fears that the death toll would spike. 
The vehicles belong to the Himachal Road Transport Corporation (HRTC). 
Those killed have been identified as Chandan Sharma (driver of one of the buses), Satpal (its conductor), Rajesh from Dharamsala, Manju from Karsog, Sachvetra from Ampur, Anita from Mandi, Beauty Sharma from Patna, Shubam from Pathankot. 
Mandi deputy commissioner Sandeep Kadam said that the number of people killed is likely to increase. The state transport minister GS Bali said the number may touch 50. 
Revenue minister Kaul Singh announced an ex-gratia relief of Rs 4 lakh to the next of kin of those killed.
