A Donald Trump waxwork has been unveiled at Madame Tussauds ahead of his presidential inauguration.  
The scowling lifelike figure of the soon-to-be 45th President of the United States is portrayed in a mock-up of the White House’s Oval Office.

Dressed in a navy suit, crisp white shirt and a red tie – plus an American flag lapel pin – Mr Trump’s doppelganger replaces Barack Obama’s waxwork in the set.  
A wax figure of Donald Trump in the Oval office, is unveiled at Madame Tussauds in London. 
He will join various other world leaders at the popular London attraction, including Vladimir Putin and the late South African leader Nelson Mandela. 
Chris Garigulo and Gemma Sim apply the finishing touches to a wax figure of Donald Trump in the Oval office (Nick Ansell/PA).
