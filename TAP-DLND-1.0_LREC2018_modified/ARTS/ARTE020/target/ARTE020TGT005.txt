In memory of Khanna, the team has decided to go ahead, minus the celebrations.
Vinod Khanna has passed away. He was 70. The veteran actor was suffering from bladder cancer and succumbed to the deadly disease on Thursday morning.
Vinod saab's death has shocked the whole industry and the nation. Bollywood has lost one of its biggest stars and it's a moment of sadness for the film fraternity. 
On the other hand, Baahubali 2 team was gearing up for a big celebration night as the makers had hosted a grand premier show for their period saga. But now, after news of Vinod Khanna's death, they have changed their plans. 
The spokesperson has released a statement confirming the same, "It is a sad moment for the industry. No one will be in the mood to show up decked in finery on a day when one of their own has departed. However, the show will happen minus the celebration. Although all film industry luminaries may not turn up. The cast of the film has not seen the film yet."
