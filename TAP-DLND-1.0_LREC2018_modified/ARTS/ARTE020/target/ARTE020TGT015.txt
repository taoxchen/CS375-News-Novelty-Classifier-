
Bollywood actor Vinod Khanna passed away at a Mumbai hospital this morning. He was 70. Khanna was admitted to the Sir HN Reliance Foundation hospital on March 31 with severe dehydration.
Indian cricketers Virender Sehwag, Harbhajan Singh, Shikhar Dhawan, Mohammad Kaif as well as commentator Harsha Bhogle took to social networking site twitter to pay their respects to the veteran star.
