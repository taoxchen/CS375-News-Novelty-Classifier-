Vinod Khanna was one of the most sought after actors in Bollywood who passed away on Thursday morning. His legendary performances have left an indelible mark in our minds and hearts and such talents can never ever be replaced. Bollywood industry was shaken by the news of his demise and took to Twitter to express their grief over the late actor's death. Salman Khan too had high respect for the actor and they had even teamed for 5 movies since 1992. Their first collaboration was 'Nischay', where they played brothers, followed by 'Wanted' in 2009, where Vinod Khanna played his father. Looking at their excellent rapport and bond, they came together as father and son again for 'Dabangg' and 'Dabangg 2'. Since all of their collaborations have done wonders at the box-office, Salman Khan considered Vinod Khanna as his lucky mascot. In an interview Vinod Khanna said, " Salman is a very nice person to work with. A very good human being and I had worked with him even in the beginning of his career. It is a co-incidence that the films in which I have worked with him have done good business. I don't know whether I am lucky for him, but he thinks I am his lucky mascot ." Salman even visited the ailing late actor Vinod Khanna at Sir HN Reliance Hospital in Mumbai.
