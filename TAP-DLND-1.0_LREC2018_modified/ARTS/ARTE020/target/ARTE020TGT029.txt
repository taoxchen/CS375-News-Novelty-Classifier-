Bollywood suffered a huge loss as veteran actor Vinod Khanna passed away on Thursday morning. The senior actor, who was suffering from advanced bladder carcinoma, breathed his last at 11:20 am at Sir HN Reliance Foundation hospital in Mumbai. 

As Bollywood celebrities mourned the loss of the superstar, we got in touch with ace producer Mukesh Bhatt , who was a long standing friend of Khanna . "I have lost a friend, a brother; his demise has created a vacuum in our lives. We shared a great bond. We used to spend many evenings together and have dinner at each other's house. I have spent some of the greatest times of my life with him," he said. 

While the news of his ailment broke a few weeks back, Mukesh revealed that Khanna was battling cancer for the past two years. "He was suffering from cancer for the past couple of years. He seemed to have recovered at one point, but then he suffered a relapse. The last time I met him was about six months back," he recalled. 


The duo has worked on a number of films together. However, what many don't know is that Mukesh worked as the star's business executive for as long as four years before turning producer. "When I was about 22-23, I used to work for him and handle his business. I worked for him for four consecutive years after which I turned to production. The first film we did together was 'Jurm' (1990)." 

The 'Raaz' producer fondly remembered his friend saying, "He was more of a brother and a great friend to me. I have never come across such a lionhearted person in my life. He was a gem of a person. He was one of those very few people to live their lives on their own terms; this is something I have learnt from him. We were a fantastic trio - me, Vinod and my brother Mahesh (Bhatt)."
