Actor Vinod Khanna passes away Actor Vinod Khanna passes away. 

The actor was hospitalised due to "severe dehydration". 
A sitting Bharatiya Janata Party MP from Gurdaspur district of Punjab, Vinod was seen in 141 films between 1968 and 2013. He has given blockbusters like Mere Apne , Mera Gaon Mera Desh , Gaddaar (1973 film), Jail Yatra , Imitihaan , Inkaar , Kuchhe Dhaage , Amar Akbar Anthony , Rajput , Qurbani , Kudrat , Dayavan , Kaarnama , Suryaa: An Awakening and Jurm . 
In 1982, at the peak of his film career, Khanna decided to quit the film industry temporarily though to follow his spiritual guru Osho Rajneesh. 
After a long hiatus, he returned to the film industry with hits like Insaaf and Satyamev Jayate .
