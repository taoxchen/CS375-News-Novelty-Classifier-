The stars will hit the red carpet tonight for the 74th Golden Globe Awards. 
The best in film and TV will be honored in Los Angeles with the Tonight Show's Jimmy Fallon hosting. 
"La La Land" leads the pack with 7 nominations, including Best Motion Picture Musical or Comedy. 
It’s competing against "20th Century Women,""Deadpool,""Florence Foster Jenkins" and "Sing Street." 
"Hell or High Water,""Lion,""Manchester by the Sea" and "Moonlight" are up for Best Motion Picture Drama. 
For the fourth straight year, Game of Thrones is up for Best Drama Series, facing off with newcomers "The Crown,""Stranger Things,""This Is Us" and "Westworld." 
And the Best Comedy Series nominees are "Atlanta,""Black-ish,""Mozart in the Jungle,""Transparent" and "Veep."
