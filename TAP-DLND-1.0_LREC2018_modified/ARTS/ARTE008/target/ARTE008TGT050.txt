Zac Efron is La La Land ‘s biggest fan! 
The 29-year-old actor took to his Twitter to wish the film’s music composers – Benj Pasek and Justin Paul – a good luck at tomorrow’s Golden Globes . 
La La Land is nominated for seven awards – including Best Musical or Comedy and Best Original Score. 
In Zac‘s tweet, he wished the guys best of luck along with sharing a picture of them with Hugh Jackman ! 
Zac and Hugh are both set to star in the upcoming film The Greatest Showman – with Benj and Justin serving as musical composers.
