Directed by Nitesh Tiwari, Dangal is based on the real-life story of Haryanvi wrestler Mahavir Singh Phogat who trained his daughters Geeta Phogat and Babita Kumari to become world-class wrestlers. 
Dangal’s story with its themes of patriotism, achievement in sports and women empowerment touched the hearts of one and all. 
The performances of Aamir Khan as Mahavir as well as those of Zaira Wasim/Fatima Sana Shaikh as Geeta Phogat and Suhani Bhatnagar/Sanya Malhotra as Babita Kumar have been widely praised. 
The pre-release buzz around the film centered on the underdog tale of Mahavir Singh Phogat’s struggle as well as the incredible physical transformation that Aamir Khan went through to play Mahavir’s character to perfection. 
At the beginning of the year, fans and trade pundits were looking forward to the box-office battle between Dangal and Sultan since both were big-ticket films featuring Aamir and Salman respectively and both films centered on the world of wrestling. However, having beaten Sultan who’s lifetime domestic collection was only Rs 306 crore, perhaps, it is time to crown Aamir as the new sultan of Bollywood.
