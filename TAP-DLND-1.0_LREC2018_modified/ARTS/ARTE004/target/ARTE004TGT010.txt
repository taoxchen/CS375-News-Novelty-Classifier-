DANGAL has continued its record smashing spree in overseas crossing 25 mil USD in two weeks of its release. The movie has collected an impressive 25.01 mil USD [170.47 cr] at the close of third Friday with some screens and territories yet to report. Dangal has already surpassed SULTAN [23.09 mil USD] in the overseas markets and has emerged as the *highest grosser of 2016* in the overseas. 
The movie has grossed approx 10.32 mil USD [70.20 cr] at the North America box office till date. With some screens yet to report, the Gulf box office has already crossed the 26.36 mil AED [Rs 48.92 cr] At the U.K. box office, the film has crossed the 3 mil USD mark with the collections pegged at 3.44 mil USD [23.44 cr].  
In Australia, Dangal has collected 1.69 mil USD [11.52 cr] till date and has emerged as the All Time Highest Grosser in the region.
