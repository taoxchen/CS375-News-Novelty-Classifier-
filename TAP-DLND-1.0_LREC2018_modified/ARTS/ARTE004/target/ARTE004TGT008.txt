The overseas markets have always been a cash cow for Bollywood films especially the ones features the big Khans of the industry viz Salman Khan , Shah Rukh Khan and Aamir Khan. However over the years the overseas markets have opened up to films that also feature other actors that have eventually gone on to do well in these markets. The recently released Aamir Khan starrer Dangal is no different in this regard. In fact the film that released across 1000 screens in the overseas markets has been doing brisk business. 
The movie had already emerged as the All Time Highest Opening Weekend Grosser as well as the Highest Opening Week Grosser at the North America box office. Having grossed approx 10.86 mil USD [74.08 cr] at the North America box office till date, Dangal has now emerged as the All Time Highest grosser at the North America box office at the end of Day 17. 
List of All Time Highest grossers at the North America box office 
Dangal – 10.86 mil USD 
Bajrangi Bhaijaan – 8.11 mil USD 
Dhoom 3 – 8.09 mil USD
