On the last day of its second weekend, superstar Aamir Khan ‘s latest offering Dangal has reported higher business than most of the movies released in 2016. This shows, how wonderfully it is running on box office across the country. Being immensely awaited, it was released with huge hype and received overwhelmed response from audience. As a result within the 2 weeks of release, the movie has emerged as the highest grosser of the year. With its strong content and brilliant performances by the lead stars, Dangal has impressed everyone and received very positive reviews from critics as well as moviegoers. As per critics, all the actors especially girls have given award winning performances in the movie. Not only from critics and audience, but entire Bollywood is praising their work and dedication. Nitesh Tiwari’s directorial has left everyone surprised with its exceptional run on box office so far. In the opening week, Dangal recorded tremendous occupancy and did the phenomenal business of 197.54 crores including 107.01 crores of weekend and 90.53 crores of weekdays. In its second weekend, it again showed impressive jump and reported 73.70 crores with 18.59 crores of Friday, 23.07 crores of Saturday and 32.04 crores of Sunday. Then in weekdays, the movie faced some drops but still remained strong through out. On its 2nd Monday, Disney’s Dangal witnessed around 30% of drop than last Friday and managed to add 13.45 crores to its account. With further decrements, it raked the business of 10.46 crores on Tuesday and 9.23 crores on Wednesday. On the last day of week i.e. Thursday, the movie minted the amount of 9.12 crores with slight more drop and made its 2 weeks total as 313.50 crores on domestic box office. From overseas also, Aamir Khan starrer reported impressive and registered the business of 166.07 crores from there so far.
