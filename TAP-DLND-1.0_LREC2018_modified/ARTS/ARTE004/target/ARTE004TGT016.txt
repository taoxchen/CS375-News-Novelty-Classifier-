DANGAL has continued its record smashing spree in overseas crossing 24.43 mil USD in two weeks of its release. The movie has collected an impressive 24.43 mil USD [166.19 cr] at the close of Week 2 with some screens and territories yet to report. Dangal has already surpassed SULTAN [23.09 mil USD] in the overseas markets and has emerged as the *highest grosser of 2016* in the overseas. 
The movie has grossed approx 10.32 mil USD [70.20 cr] at the North America box office till date. With some screens yet to report, the Gulf box office has already crossed the 25.34 mil AED [Rs 46.93 cr] At the UK box office, the film has crossed the 3 mil USD mark with the collections pegged at 3.38 mil USD [23.01 cr]. In Australia, Dangal has collected 1.65 mil USD [11.23 cr] till date and has emerged as the All Time Highest Grosser in the region.
