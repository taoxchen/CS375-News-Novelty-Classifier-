PUNE: The Management Development Institute (MDI) a B-school, has announced the successful conclusion of the second edition of its annual leadership conclave, Samvaad. The event was themed -- success through synergy was attended by senior executives from a spectrum of industries.
A statement issued by MDI stated that, Samvaad is an annual business symposium organized by the students of MDI which provides an opportunity for the exchange of ideas among the brightest minds in the country. Panelists at the event comprised of leading entrepreneurs, company heads, leadership gurus, academicians and industry stalwarts from various sectors and start-ups who shared their views and experiences of the corporate world.
The theme was selected keeping in mind the need for emerging leaders to achieve a higher quality and quantity output with synergy, and gave an opportunity to the students to get an insight on the strategy, leadership, innovation, and creativity in the current business ecosystem. A panel discussion on 'India on the Rise - Purpose, Passion and Leadership/Path to Synergy' with Sanjiv Navangul, managing director, Janssen India was held. The second panel discussion was on the topic -- Redefining Status and the final topic for panel discussion was conducted on Creating Connections: Building Bridges. The students learnt about the importance of team synergy through examples and experiences quoted by industry veterans.
