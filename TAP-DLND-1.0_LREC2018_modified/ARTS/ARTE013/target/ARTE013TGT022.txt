PM Modi reaches Patna Gandhi Maidan to attend Guru Govind Singh Prakash Parv celebrations PM Modi reaches Patna Gandhi Maidan to attend Guru Govind Singh Prakash Parv celebrations 6   IBNS Patna, Jan 5 : Prime Minister Narendra Modi has reached the Gandhi Maidan in Patna to attend the 350th Prakash Parv celebrations of Guru Govind Singh on Thursday.  Earlier in the day, PM Modi was received by Bihar Governor Ram Nath Kovind and Chief Minister Nitish Kumar upon his arrival in Patna. On Thursday, the Prime Minister had tweeted, Delighted to be a part of #350thPrakashParv celebrations of Guru Gobind Singh ji in Patna today. Guru Gobind Singh ji was blessed with indomitable courage tremendous knowledge. His bravery is etched in the heart mind of every Indian, he said.
