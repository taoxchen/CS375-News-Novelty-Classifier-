Parkash Singh Badal , Deputy Chief Minister Sukhbir Singh Badal, Union Food Processing Minister Harsimrat Kaur Badal - all of the Akali Dal - visited Patna Sahib on the occasion. 
They were present in Prime Minister Narendra Modi's programme in which a commemorative stamp was released. The chief minister also delivered a speech before Modi. 
On reaching Patna on Wednesday, the CM said, "We will win three-fourth of the total Assembly seats in the Assembly polls in Punjab as there is no opposition against us." 
As far as the vote share is concerned, Akali Dal had won the 2012 Assembly elections in the state, garnering 35 per cent of the total votes, while BJP got 7 per cent of the votes. 

3. AAM AADMI PARTY 
Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal visited Patna Sahib on January 2, becoming one of the first senior leaders to visit the shrine. He offered prayers and sought blessings at the Takht Sri Harmandir Sahib, the birthplace of the 10th Sikh Guru. 
The Delhi government also declared a holiday in the national capital on Thursday and issued newspaper advertisements to mark the occasion. 
Kejriwal is working hard to win back the Sikhs, who his party has offended on a couple of occasions in the past.

AAP's youth manifesto, released on July 3, courted controversy when the party's election symbol, a broom, was printed alongside an image of the Golden Temple. Kejriwal profusely apologised for the faux pas. He even did penance by washing utensils at the Golden Temple. 
AAP also drew flak for its leader Ashish Khetan comparing the manifesto with scriptures of the Guru Granth Sahib, Gita and Quran. Even though Khetan apologised for the blunder, a case was registered against him. 
Kejriwal seems to have learnt a lesson from the faux pas. Care has been taken to avoid any such error in the advertisement issued on Thursday. His head is covered with a scarf and his photograph is smaller and below that of Guru Gobind Singh. 
It is AAP's maiden entry in the fray in Punjab. After winning four seats in 2014 Lok Sabha elections, the party's chances in the Assembly appeared quite bright till mid-2016. However, the party has been dogged with one controversy after another.
