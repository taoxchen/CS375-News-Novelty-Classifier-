Prime Minister Narendra Modi offered prayers at Patna Sahib on the occasion of 350th birth anniversary of Guru Gobind Singh on Thursday. The Prime Minister said, he is delighted to be part of Prakash Parva celebration of Guru Gobind Singhji. Bihar Governor Ram Nath Kovind, Chief Minister Nitish Kumar, Punjab Chief Minister Prakash Singh Badal, Union Ministers Ravi Shankar Prasad, Ram Vilas Paswan and Harsimrat Kaur were present on this occasion. Prime Minister Modi exhorted people to adopt the path of Guru Gobind Singh ji Maharaj and maintain high moral values, standards and social harmony in their lives. Addressing devotees at Patna Sahib, PM Modi said Guru Gobind Singhji's entire life was devoted to serving the people and fighting for values of truth, justice and compassion. PM said that Gobind Singhji fought against social evils to tie society with the thread of unity and want to implement same principles in the country.
