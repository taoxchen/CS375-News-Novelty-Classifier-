 Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Thursday said he will attend the 350 th Prakash Parv celebrations of Guru Gobind Singh in Patna. [NK India] Delighted to be a part of #350thPrakashParv celebrations of Guru Gobind Singh ji in Patna today, Modi tweeted. Guru Gobind Singh ji was blessed with indomitable courage tremendous knowledge. His bravery is etched in the heart mind of every Indian, he said. Guru Gobind Singh jis entire life was devoted to serving people and fighting for values of truth, justice and compassion. #350thPrakashParv, he said.
