It was the chemistry between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Bihar Chief Minister Nitish Kumar which dominated the show at the Prakash Parv festival celebrated in Patna on Thursday. 
A reserved body language at the beginning slowly gave way to smiles, pleasantries, handshakes, and then a candid discussion which did not go unnoticed. 
PM and Nitish, both are known to have bitterness if not hatred for each other but ending all kind of animosity, both leaders looked completely at ease triggering speculations. It may be remembered here that it was Nitish who took the first step towards breaking the ice.
