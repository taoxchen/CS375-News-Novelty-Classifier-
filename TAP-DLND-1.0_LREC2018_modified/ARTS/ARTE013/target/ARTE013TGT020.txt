Controversial JDU MLA Shyam Bahadur Singh has courted yet another controversy, this time by roaming on the streets of Patna on an elephant. 
The JDU legislator who has earlier been caught on camera on several occasion shaking his leg with bar dancers took the elephant ride to greet the people of the city on the occasion of Prakash Parv, the 350th birth anniversary of Guru Gobind Singh which is being celebrated in Patna. 
The MLA's royal ride gave some anxious moments to the commuters on the street who were returning to their homes from offices. The elephant's presence on the road also created a traffic jam but the JDU MLA from Barhaiya in Siwan remained undeterred. 
Speaking Exclusively to India Today, the JDU MLA said that he had planned this ride ever since he came to know that Prakash Parv would be celebrated in Patna. 
WELCOMING GUESTS IN REGALIA 
"Since the time Bihar CM Nitish Kumar said that this festival would be celebrated in Patna, I had decided that I will take out an elephant ride to greet the people in Patna. I am welcoming guests who have come from other parts of the country in this manner," said Shyam Bahadur Singh. 
When questioned that the presence of the royal animal on the middle of the street was causing immense problem to the people, the MLA remained defiant. 
" There is no traffic chaos being caused to the people as the management of traffic is very good in Patna due to Prakash Parv," said the controversial MLA. 
TO CONTINUE WITH HIS RIDE 
The MLA did not stop there and said that on Thursday again he will be sitting on the elephant and taking a ride. He also denied allegations that he has ever danced with bar dancers. 
"I have danced with artists and not bar dancers. I have lived all my life with artists," said the MLA. 
The MLA's penchant for elephant rides are well know and recently he had even attended the session of Bihar Assembly when he reached the Assembly on an elephant.
