New Tensions For Baahubali 2 In Karnataka 
on Apr 25, 2017 India's much awaited film of the year- Baahubali: The Conclusion is all set to release worldwide on April 28th. The film had problems in Karnataka with few Pro Kannada leaders warned to ban the film in the state if Sathyaraj who played Kattappa won't tender apologies for his remarks on the state over Cauvery water issue. Recently, Sathyaraj tendered apology to Kannadigas and makers got green signal for release from them. Now, new tensions start for Baahubali 2 in Karnataka. Multiplex owners in Bangalore are demanding 60% share from Baahubali 2 makers in order to screen the film. Negotiations are happening and bookings will open only after a clarity comes on this. On the other hand, when Tamil Nadu theatrical distributor and sub distributor were unable to sort out their financial issues, TN Distributors Federation stepped in to resolve the matter. If they haven't come in aid of the Baahubali 2 distributors, it would be a troublesome for the makers as well. Fortunately, all issues surrounding Baahubali 2 release in TN have been sorted out now.
