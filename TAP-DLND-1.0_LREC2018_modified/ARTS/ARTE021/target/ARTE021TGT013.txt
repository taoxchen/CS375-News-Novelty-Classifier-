In only four days, the storm is ready to hit the Indian box office. If you know what we mean. Baahubali 2 or Baahubali: The Conclusion is all set to hit about 8000 screens across the country. The advance booking is open and is fast filling. Trade website Koimoi.com is out with a detailed report for the metros. Yes, and it looks like the sequel is all set to trump the first day collections of Rs 50 crore of Baahubali: The Beginning. Don’t believe us? Take a look at this detailed report and you would know what we’re saying. 
So, according to the trade website, advance booking in most regions of Mumbai (western, southern and central) are fast filling or even FULL for the Hindi version of Baahubali: The Conclusion. Booking for Tamil and Malayalam has not opened in the Mumbai circuit yet. As far as booking for Baahubali in Telugu is concerned, the report looks average but is expected to pick up in next couple of days. For Mumbai, the single screens are seen doing better business than multiplexes… for now. 
Hyderabad, which is a core market for a magnum opus like Baahubali, has all screens FULL for the Telugu language shows. Surprisingly, masses have also booked heavily for the Hindi shows. This is unexpected but will guarantee awesome business for the makers. In Delhi (NCR), plexes in Ghaziabad, Noida and Gurgaon are all booked for shows in the national language. In Pune, unlike Mumbai, people are heavily booking in Hindi as well as Telugu languages. Plexes and single screens like the E-Square chain in Hinjewadi, University Road and the single screen Laxmi Narayan Cinema are all full. In Bengaluru and Chennai though it is the regional language shows which are preferred over Hindi. But this time, Chennai masses are seen booking tickets in Hindi along with Tamil and Telugu. In Bangalore, Anjan Digital 4K A/C Cinema, Cauvery Theatre, Gopalan Cinemas, Gopalan Grand Mall, Lakshmi Digital 2K Cinema, Srinivasa Digital 2K Cinema, Urvashi Digital 4K Cinema are preferred over the rest. In Chennai seats in Rohini Silver Screens, Sri Vigneshwara Theatre DOLBY ATMOS, Kumaran Theatre, SK-Marlen Cinemas are already filled up. While almost all major theatres and multiplexes chains across the country are almost booked, the response for Baahubali: The Conclusion is comparatively low in Kolkata but the trade suggests that it will sure pick up on and after the release date.
