This shocking incident came to the fore when Shobu tweeted regarding the same... 
Baahubali producer, Shobu Yarlagadda, has accused a staff member of the Emirates airlines of being rude and harrasing the team and also for indulging in racism. 
On Wednesday morning, he tweeted, "Flying to Hyd on @emirates EK526. Airline staff at gate B4 were very rude n harassed our team unnecessarily! Bad attitude n service!"(sic). 
In another tweet, he mentioned that he was the regular flyer with the airlines and it was the first time that such a thing had happened. "emirates I think one of the emirates staff was being racist.. I fly emirates regularly n this is 1st time I have come across this kind of attitude", he tweeted.

The good part is that Emirates airlines responded, asking him to share the details of his travel so that they could look into the matter. 
Baahubali 2 is all set to release on April 28 and is directed by SS Rajamouli. The team has worked on a two-part series over five years. The film starts Prabhas in the title role and also Rana Daggubati, Sathyaraj, Nasser, Anushka Shetty, Tammannah and Ramya Krishnan among others. The music for the film has been composed by MM Keeravani. The film is said to have been made on a budget of over Rs 300 crores and will be released in around 9000 screens worldwide.
