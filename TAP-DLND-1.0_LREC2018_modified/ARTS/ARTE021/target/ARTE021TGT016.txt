When SS Rajamouli came up with Baahubali, little did he know that the franchise will go on to become one of the highest grossers in film history. However, the complete credit to the franchise's success cannot be given to just Rajamouli, but it must be extended to his family as well. It may come as a surprise but most of the filmmaker's family is part of the franchise.
Baahubali is written by the filmmaker's father Vijayendra Prasad and the songs are penned by his younger brothers K Shiva Shakti Datta and K Ramakrishna. The creative streak doesn't stop just there.
Music has been rendered by Rajamouli's cousin and Datta's son, MM Keeravani, while his brother Kalyani Malik has worked as a sound recordist and younger son Sri Simha assisted as music director. Keeravani's eldest son Kaala Bhairava has also crooned a few songs for Baahubali.
Not only are the males in the family, but the women also quite gifted. Keeravani's wife Sree Valli is the line producer of the film, while Rajamouli's wife Rama is the costume designer.
This is just not it! SS Karthikeya'a Rajamouli's son assisted on sets as second unit director and Rajabali - Ramakrishna's son and Rajamouli's cousin - worked as VFX assistant.
On Monday, news of Rajamouli and his nephew Keeravani's daughters Mayuka and Kumudvathi were in the 'Saahore Baahubali' song that released yesterday. Kalyani Malik's son Mayur has also lent his voice for a bit in the film.
As the anticipation builds up ahead of its release this Friday, we can certainly say, this project is all in the family.
