The sequel of the highest grosser Indian film Baahubali-The Beginning will hit the screens on April 28. Though he looks relieved after a strenuous journey, Rajamouli has already planned for life for the movie well beyond the screen avtar. The makers of the film, made with highest budget so far in the Indian film industry, has prepared a blueprint. The World of Baahubali brings in firms working in mobile gaming, app development, animation, merchandising and book publishing. As the film is slated for release across the world in a variety of languages and formats (regular and IMAX presentation), film’s director Rajamouli and producer Shobhu Yarlagadda lined up all the partners that would extend the life of Baahubali well beyond the movie avtar. ArcaConnect, an entity set up by Baahubali’s producers, has partnered with a variety of partners to tap the business potential of the movie. “We have realised the potential as soon as we zeroed in on the script.
