Prabhas’ next will be a high-octane, trilingual thriller 
He will probably be playing Baahubali for one last time. Up next for Prabhas is a trilingual film, Saaho , labelled by its producers as “an ambitious hi-tech action drama�?. 
Saaho is reported to be a fast-paced action thriller, with a healthy dose of romance and drama. Produced by Hyderabad-based UV Creations, the film is directed by 27-year-old Sujeeth (he directed the 2014 comedy, Run Raja Run ). The soundtrack will combine the talents of Shankar-Ehsaan-Loy, and National Award-winning lyricist Amitabh Bhattacharya. 
The rest of the crew includes cinematographer Madhie, who worked on last year’s The Ghazi Attack , and production designer Sabu Cyril (fresh off Baahubali 2 ). The filmmakers have also roped in a “globally-acclaimed�? stunt choreographer. 
Saaho is being shot simultaneously in Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu. Its teaser will be released in theatres along with Baahubali 2 on April 28.
