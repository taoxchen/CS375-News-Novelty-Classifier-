
Prabhas has invested around 5 years of his life to one film. The film which has been shot in two parts has been in the pipeline for 5 years and has borne perfect results for the actor and the entire team.
The film is currently the 4th highest grossing Indian film and with the release of Baahubali 2 this Friday, history is all set to be created. But do you know that PRabhas has already been offered another big film, post Baahubali.
Says a source, "A 1000 crore film is being planned now. It will be the adaptation of Mahabhrata on celluloid. Prabhas has been offered an integral role in the film. The makers are spellbound with Prabhas' brilliant acting genius in Baahubali and they have offered him a prominent part in the epic film."
Last we heard, Prabhas was busy reading Mahabhrata. Could that be a hint? Yes, your guess is as good as ours!
