Everyone's favourite Katappa (played by Sathyaraj) is also there. These pictures from the sets of Baahubali: The Beginning are pure gold. Prabhas plays double roles, of father Amarendra and son Mahendra Baahubali, in the film. Rana Daggubati plays Bhallala Deva, who competes with his cousin for the throne of Mahishmathi. Baahubali: The Beginning starts with Bhallala Deva's rule (not a very kind one) and by the end audience gets to know that Amarendra Baahubali was announced as Mahishmathi's next ruler while Bhallala Deva was given the charge of leading the army. Baahubali: The Conclusion will reveal what happened in between and how Mahendra Baahubali will reclaim the throne from his uncle.
