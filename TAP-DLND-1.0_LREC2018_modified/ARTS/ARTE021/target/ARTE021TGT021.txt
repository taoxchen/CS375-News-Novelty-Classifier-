Baahubali 2 Premieres At Reasonable Price 
on Apr 26, 2017 Usually, most of star hero films will have special benefit and paid premiere shows one day earlier or on wee hours of release day. However, SS Rajamouli's magnum opus Baahubali: The Conclusion aka Baahubali 2 will only have paid premieres in Hyderabad. Asian will be screening three shows in each Multiplex in Hyderabad starting from 9:30PM to 11PM on April 27th. What's more, ticket price for these shows is the regular price of 100-200. This is indeed great move because the price is affordable for all section of audiences. Moreover, they will be overjoyed to know the Kattappa secret one day prior to the film's actual outing on 28th. With five shows in Nizam and six shows in Andhra Pradesh, Baahubali 2 is sure to smash day one, first weekend, first week and few other records for sure.
