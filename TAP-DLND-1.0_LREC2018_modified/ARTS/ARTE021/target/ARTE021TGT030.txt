
Since yesterday, there have been several pictures claiming to be of Baahubali The Conclusion’s leaked version doing the rounds on social media. One pirate is even rumored to have uploaded​ Prabhas’ introduction scene on YouTube. 
Needless to say that these images and rumors made the fans of Baahubali franchise anxious as the film’s theatrical release is just a few hours away. Meanwhile, the film’s producer Shobu Yarlagadda took to Twitter and said that except for the censor boards in various countries, Baahubali 2 has not been screened anywhere for common public. 
Baahubali The Conclusion is going to have its world theatrical premiere in Mumbai tomorrow evening. Except for screening to various "censor boards" in different countries, there have been no screenings of @BaahubaliMovie 2 .
