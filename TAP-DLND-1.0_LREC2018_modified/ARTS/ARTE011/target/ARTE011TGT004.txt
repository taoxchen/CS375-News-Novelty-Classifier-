Meryl Streep slated Donald Trump at the Golden Globes 
Her Cecil B. Demille Award was presented by Viola Davis, and the veteran actress had to struggle with a sore throat as she graciously accepted her prize. 
The 67-year-old called out the President-elect for a moment in the election campaign when he seemed to mock a disabled reporter. 
"It kind of broke my heart when I saw it, and I can't get it out of my head, because it wasn't in a movie. It was real life," she said.
