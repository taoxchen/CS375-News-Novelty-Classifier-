The legendary actor, who is known for his roles in films such Ardh Satya, Bhavni Bhavai, Mirch Masala and Dharavi died after a heart attack.
His sudden death was confirmed by his close friend and Indian filmmaker Ashoke Pandit as he took to Twitter to express his condolences.
Sad & shocked to know that versatile actor Om Puri jee has expired due to heart attack early morning today. #RIP .
— Ashoke Pandit 
Another close friend, UN Women official Anupam Kher also took to Twitter to condole the passing of his long-time friend.
Seeing him lying on his bed looking so calm can’t believe that one of our greatest actors #OmPuri is no more. Deeply saddened & shocked.
— Anupam Kher 
I have known #OmPuri for d last 43yrs. For me he’ll always b a great actor, a kind & generous man. And that is how world shud remember him.
