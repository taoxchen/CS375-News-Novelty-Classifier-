News of veteran Indian actor Om Puri's sudden death has left a void in the industry. While the entire B-Town is mourning his death on Twitter, friends and relatives are also reminiscing fond moments spent with the actor. 
Om ji passed away at 66 due to a severe cardiac arrest in the early hours of Friday morning. But he still has two films lined up for release. One Indo British film called Viceroy's House and another one - a biggie in the form of Kabir Khan's Tubelight starring Salman Khan. 
While Viceroy's House is all set for a March 3 release, Tubelight will release on Eid later this year. So Tubelight will officially be Om Puri's last film. Director Kabir Khan too spoke about him on Twitter after his death. 
He wrote: 
Omji... u were laughing with us on set jst a few days ago! We have lost one of the greatest actor & the warmest person in the film industry
— Kabir Khan 
Omji... I will miss that warm tight hug that you gave me every morning on set. Khudahafiz sir... you were the best!
— Kabir Khan
