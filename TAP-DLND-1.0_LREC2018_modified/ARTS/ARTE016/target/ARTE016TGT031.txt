Born on October 18, 1950, hailing from Ambala in Haryana, Om Puri made his film debut in 1976 with the Marathi film Ghashiram Kotwal. 
The actor has appeared in mainstream Bollywood cinema as well as Pakistani, British and Hollywood films. The actor has received an OBE as well as Padma Shri, the fourth highest civilian award of India. 
An alumnus of Film and Television Institute of India as well as National School of Drama, Om Puri was on the forefront of what was called art films movement in the 70s. 
Om Puri has worked in acclaimed films such as Bhavni Bhavai (1980), Sadgati (1981), Ardh Satya (1982), Mirch Masala (1986) and Dharavi (1992). Prime Minister Narendra Modi has condoled the death of veteran Actor. In a tweet, Mr. Modi recalled Om Puri’s long career in theatre and films.
