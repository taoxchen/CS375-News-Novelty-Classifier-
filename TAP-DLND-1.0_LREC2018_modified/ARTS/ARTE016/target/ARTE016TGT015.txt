Hindi film industry's veteran actor Om Puri died of a heart attack in his residence in Mumbai. Filmmaker Ashoke Pandit confirmed the sad news of the actor's demise.
Sad & shocked to know that versatile actor Om Puri jee has expired due to heart attack early morning today. #RIP .
— Ashoke Pandit
Prime Minister Narendra Modi paid a tribute to the legendary actor on Twitter. "The Prime Minister condoles the passing away of actor Om Puri & recalls his long career in theater & films," the official account of the PMO India tweeted.
The veteran actor's body is currently at his residence in Andheri. His friends and colleagues from Bollywood have started dropping in to pay their last respects.
