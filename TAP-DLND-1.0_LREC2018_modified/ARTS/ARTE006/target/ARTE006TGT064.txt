Legendary action-star Jackie Chan will be seen promoting his forthcoming multilingual action-adventure comedy film Kung Fu Yoga in the upcoming episode of The Kapil Sharma Show. Funnyman-actor Kapil Sharma, who hosts the Sony Entertainment Television show, took to Twitter on Monday to announce the news. “Oh my god. Jackie Chan on The Kapil Sharma Show,” Kapil tweeted. Kung Fu Yoga is part of the three-film agreement signed between India and China during Chinese President Xi Jinping’s visit to India. It also stars Amyra Dastur and Sonu Sood. The film is slated to release on February 3.
