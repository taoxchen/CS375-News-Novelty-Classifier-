
After China and Malaysia, International star Jackie Chan and director Stanley Tong have come to India for a day to promote their upcoming film Kung Fu Yoga . Jackie, along with Sonu Sood , Amrya Dastur and Disha Patani will be seen promoting the action comedy on the The Kapil Sharma Show. The entire cast shot the episode today afternoon at Goregaon film city. It will air on 28th January. This is not the first time when an International star is coming on the show. Dwayne Bravo ,Brett Lee and Chris Gayle have earlier appeared on the popular show. 
Kapil seems to have lined up a lot of interesting stuff for Jackie. 
Jackie is also expected to meet Salman Khan during his one-day long stay in India. “Jackie’s co-star Sonu Sood is close to Salman (they worked together in Dabangg) and it was he who arranged for this meeting between the two stars. Jackie will have a press conference in the afternoon and host a small, intimate dinner for some select people. He won’t have time to meet many people, but he is keen on meeting Salman during his stay here. Sonu has spoken a lot about Salman’s superstar status in India and his charity work and the action star expressed a desire to meet him. Though Salman is shooting for Kabir Khan’s Tubelight, he plans to meet Jackie at an Asian bar and restaurant at JW Marriott, where the star is staying,” a spurce told DNA. 
Kung Fu Yoga will hit screens on February 3. It was earlier going to clash with Shah Rukh Khan’s Raees and Hrithik Roshan’s Kaabil on 25th of January, but it later got postponed to February. The film will release in China on January 28.
