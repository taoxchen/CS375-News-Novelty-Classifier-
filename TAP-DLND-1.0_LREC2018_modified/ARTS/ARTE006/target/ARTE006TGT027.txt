 Legendary action star Jackie Chan during his visit to India met Bollywood superstar Salman Khan, actor is present here to promote his upcoming movie “Kung Fu Yoga”. 
Salman shared his photograph with the “Rush Hour” star from their meeting that took place yesterday. Picture shared shows the two actors posing with two stuffed panda toys. 
Earlier, Salman Khan took to the micro blogging site Twitter and thanked Jackie Chan on Twitter for offering this film to Sonu Sood. 
Sonu had replied to the “Bajrangi Bhaijaan” star by tweeting: “Bhai Salman Khan I have an unusual power-packed surprise for you. See you soon in India… Pandeji Hum aah rahe hai. Jackie Chan.” 
“Kung Fu Yoga” stars actors Disha Patani, Sonu Sood, Amyra Dastur and Sonu Sood and is directed by Stanley Tong and has been shot in India, Dubai and Beijing. The movie was been planned during three-film agreement that was penned down between China and India sometime back at the time when Chinese President Xi Jinping visited India.
