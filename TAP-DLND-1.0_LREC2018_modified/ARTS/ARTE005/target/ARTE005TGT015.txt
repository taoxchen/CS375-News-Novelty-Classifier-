 
The festivities of December may have ended but it’s on to the start of awards season. Last night saw Bollywood attend one of the biggest Bollywood award ceremonies, the 62nd Jio Filmfare Awards. Braving the slight drop in temperature, celebrities stepped out in their finest for the show. 
Alia Bhatt, who took home the Best Actress award for Udta Punjab , wore a custom Prabal Gurung gown befitting the occasion. Sonam Kapoor, who swung the critics vote for Neerja picked a sheer, appliqué ensemble by one of her favourite red carpet designers—Elie Saab. Jacqueline Fernandez went with a sparkly one-shoulder gown by Ali Younes couture. 
Shilpa Shetty, Raveena Tandon and Preity Zinta followed the international colour trend for the red carpet and sported sunny yellow numbers last night. Here’s a look at all the outfits that you may have missed from the night.
