 It was yet another gala night for the Bollywood to remember as the 62nd Filmfare Awards function in Mumbai witnessed power packed performances from some of the most popular celebrities of the film industry. Actor Aamir Khan not only got the best actor’s award, his latest release ‘Dangal’ won the best film’s award. While, it’s director Nitesh Tiwari received the award for the best director, actress Alia bhat bagged the best actor (female) award for his superb performance in Udta Punjab. The award nights also saw Rishi Kapoor getting award best actor (male) in a supporting role for ‘Kapoor and Sons’ and Shabana Azmi getting the award for best actor in a supporting role (female) for Neerja. Bollywood superstar Shah Rukh Khan and ace filmmaker Karan Johar hosted the event.
