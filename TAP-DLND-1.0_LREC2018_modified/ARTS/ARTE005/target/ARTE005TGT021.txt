
Once again, it’s time for B-Town to come together and celebrate success. The 62nd Jio Filmfare Awards 2017 was hosted in Worli’s NSCI Dome on 14th January 2017 and the event was a grand one. All biggies under one roof was indeed a great thing it undoubtedly, it was a fun evening. 
SRK and Karan Johar were hosting the show and it goes without saying that their witty 1-liners were enough to make the public laugh hard. 
There was a little disappointment over the nominations, because Akshay Kumar’s name was completely excluded from the list, in spite of giving so many good movies. 
On the other hand, was a nail-biting night because all 3 Khans, SRK, Salman and Aamir were fighting for the best actor award for FAN, Sultan and Dangal respectively… 
So aren’t you eager to know the winner? Here’s the list… 
Best Action : Sham Kaushal Dangal 
Best Background Score : Sameer Uddin Kapoor & Sons 
Best Choreography : Adil Shaikh – Kar gayi chul (Kapoor & Sons) 
Best Visual Effects : Redchillies VFX – Fan 
Best Costume : Payal Saluja – Udta Punjab 
Best Cinematography : Mitesh Mirchandani – Neerja 
Best Editing : Monisha R Baldawa – Neerja 
Best Lyrics : Amitabh Bhattacharya – Channa mereya (Ae Dil Hai Mushkil) 
Best Story : Shakun Batra and Ayesha Devitre – Kapoor & Sons 
Best Screenplay : Shakun Batra and Ayesha Devitre – Kapoor & Sons 
Best Dialogue : Ritesh Shah – Pink 
Best Playback (Male) : Arijit Singh – Ae dil hai mushkil (Ae Dil Hai Mushkil) 
Best Playback (Female) : Neha Bhasin – Jag ghoomeya (Sultan) 
Best Music Album : Pritam – Ae Dil Hai Mushkil 
Best Actor In A Supporting Role (Female) : Shabana Azmi – Neerja 
Best Actor In A Supporting Role (Male) : Rishi Kapoor – Kapoor & Sons 
Best Debut Director : Ashwiny Iyer Tiwari – Nil Battey Sannata 
Best Debut (Male) : Diljit Dosanjh – UdtaPunjab 
Best Debut (Female) : Ritika Singh – Saala Khadoos 
Critics’ Award For Best Actor (Female) : Sonam Kapoor – Neerja 
Critics’ Award For Best Actor (Male) : Manoj Bajpayee & Shahid Kapoor for Aligarh and UdtaPunjab respectively. 
Critics’ Award For Best Movie : Neerja 
Best Director : Nitesh Tiwari – Dangal 
Since all 3 Khans were competing; fans were bound to get nervous with regards to the win of their favorite actor. And last but not the least, best actor & actress award goes to:- 
Best Actress : Alia Bhatt – Udta Punjab 
Best Actor : Aamir Khan – Dangal And the biggest award of the day best film goes to:- 
Best Film : Dangal
