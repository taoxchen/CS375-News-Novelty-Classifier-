Like every year, the film industry has again come together to validate and award the best talents this year. The Jio Filmfare Awards 2017 are being held at the NSCI Dome in Worli, Mumbai and after a starry red carpet, the industry is now cheering and rooting for the best in the business. While Shah Rukh Khan-KJo's entertaining one-liners are already a hit at the main-event, let's have a look at the winners of #JioFilmfareAwards 2017. Here is the list of all those who won big and are taking the black lady home from the 62nd Jio Filmfare Awards 2017: 
1. Best Visual Effects: Red Chillies for Fan 
2. Best Editing: Monisha Baldawa for Neerja 
3. Best Costume: Payal Saluja for Udta Punjab 
4. Best Action: Shyam Kaushal for Dangal 
5. Best Background Score: Sameer Uddin for Kapoor & Sons (Since 1921) 
6. Best Choreography: Adil Shaikh for 'Kar Gayi Chull' in Kapoor & Sons (Since 1921) 
7. Best Cinematography: Mitesh Mirchandani for Neerja 
8. Best Playback Singer (Male): Arijit Singh for 'Ae dil hai mushkil' from Ae Dil Hai Mushkil 
9. Best Playback Singer (Female): Neha Bhasin for 'Jag Ghoomeya' from Sultan 
10. Filmfare RD Burman Award for New Music Talent: Amit Mishra for 'Bulleya' from Ae Dil Hai Mushkil 
11. Best music album: Pritam for Ae Dil Hai Mushkil 
12. Filmfare Lifetime Achievement Award: Shatrughan Sinha 
13. Best Dialogue-Ritesh Shah - Pink 
14. Best Production Design-Aparna Sud and Anna Ipe - Neerja 
15. Best Screenplay- Shakun Batra and Ayesha Devitre - Kapoor & Sons 
16. Best Sound Design- Vivek Sachidanand - Phobia 
17. Best Story- Shakun Batra and Ayesha Devitre - Kapoor & Sons 
18. Best Actor in a Supporting Role (Female): Shabana Azmi for Neerja 
19. Best Actor in a Supporting Role (Male) - Rishi Kapoor 
20. Best Debutante (Male): Diljit Dosanjh for Udta Punjab 
21. Best Debutante (Female): Ritika Singh for Saala Khadoos 
22. Best film (fictional short film) - Jyoti Kapoor Das for Chutney 
23. Best Actor (female)short film-Tisca Chopra for Chutney 
24. Best Actor (Male) in a Short Film: Manoj Bajpayee for 'Taandav' 
25. Best Short Film People's Choice: Khamakha
