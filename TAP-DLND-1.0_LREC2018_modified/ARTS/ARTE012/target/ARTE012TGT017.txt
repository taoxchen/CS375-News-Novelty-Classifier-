
Nahid Afrin a 16-year-old singer from Biswanath Chariali, Assam got into a national controversy when 46 clerics issued a fatwa against her. Before we go to the details make sure that the issue we are talking here is crystal clear to all of you. A fatwa in the Islamic faith is a non-binding but authoritative legal opinion that the Sheikhul Islam, a qualified jurist or mufti(cleric), can give on issues pertaining to the Islamic law. 
A police officer believed that the Clerics may have issued a fatwa against her for singing a song against terrorism and the Islamic State (ISIS). The clerics said that her songs are anti-Islamic and against sharia (Islamic canonical law based on the teachings of the Koran and the traditions of the Prophet). Printed sheets of paper bearing the fatwa in Assamese and the names of the clerics were distributed across Nagaon and Hojai districts in central Assam on 14th March. It said, 
“If anti-sharia acts like musical nights are held on grounds surrounded by masjids, idgahs, madrassas and graveyards, our future generations will attract the wrath(extreme anger) of Allah.” For More Videos, Subscribe Us Now 
No doubt that Nahid, the Indian Idol runner-up who made her Bollywood debut in 2016 in the movie Akira was completely shocked and broken after the fatwa was issued. She even thought that she will never be able to sing again but many Muslim singers backed her up. 
On Wednesday she said, 
“I was shocked and broken from inside at first. But, many Muslim singers gave me inspiration to not quit music. I will never do so.” 
While talking to Mid-day she said, 
If I were a boy, they wouldn’t have had an issue. They are trying to browbeat me because I’m a girl 
One of the major reasons of the fatwa being issued now is to prevent Nahid from singing at the programme in Udali Sonai Bibi College in Lanka on 25th March 2017. 
But despite getting so many threats from various organisations and leaders Nahid stays strong and being a Muslim herself she believes that she isn’t doing anything against the religion. She even further said, 
“I think my music is God’s gift to me. I believe it must be properly utilised; not doing so is ignoring God”. 
Even the organisers of the programme stand strong and have confirmed that the show will not be cancelled under any circumstances. The police have ensured that Nahid and her entire family will be given a full cover security. People all over India are standing in favour of Nahid including the minister of Law and Justice in India, Ravi Shankar Prasad and famous musician Vishal Dadlani. 
Nahid is very happy to receive such tremendous support. In a recent interview with the Mid-day, Afrin said, 
“I got calls from [composer-singer] Vishal Dadlani sir and others from the fraternity after news of the fatwa broke. They said they are ready to help me in every possible way. Last night, the chief minister (Sarbananda Sonowal) also contacted us and offered to provide security, if needed. He asked me not to worry.”
