A Muslim teen singer in Assam has been guaranteed protection by the government after reports of written opposition to her concert, scheduled for March 25 in Central Assam. 
Ahead of her performance in Hojai on March 25, pamphlets found near the venue warn young people against singing and dancing and describe music concerts and events like a recently-held magic show as against Sharia law. The 40 signatories include local clerics. Hojai is located 162 km from the capital of Guwahati.  
Singers, music composers, actors and politicians have come out to support singer Nahid Afrin after a ‘fatwa’ was issued by 46 Muslim clerics of Assam directing her not to perform on stage.
