
Raghu Karnad, journalist and author, has decided 
not to participate in the Jaipur Literature Festival starting at Diggi Palace in Jaipur on Thursday. 
Mr. Raghu Karnad’s post in his Facebook account said, “Bowing out of JLF this year, so @theHinduLitforLife is the end of the tour.” 
His decision gains significance at a time when Communist Party of India (Marxist) Polit Bureau member M.A. Baby refused to participate in the literary event, protesting against invitations extended to two Sangh Parivar representatives to the festival. 
However, Mr. Raghu Karnad refused to attribute any reason for bowing out of the Jaipur event. 
When The Hindu contacted him, he said, “I do not want to make a statement about it. I can just say I am not participating because of a private disagreement with the organisers.”
