 Lakhs of people today formed the human chain in Bihar to show their support on the prohibition of liquor in the state. The chain comprised of men, women and children and even political leader of several parties.
Bihar Chief Minister Nitish Kumar, RJD chief Lalu Prasad, Congress, along with the opposition BJP leaders participated in the human chain. Chief Minister Nitish Kumar imposed the liquor ban in the state on April 5, 2016.
