A day after the Bharatiya Janata Party participated in the Bihar government's human chain event in support of prohibition, the party lashed out at Chief Minister Nitish Kumar for using the event to promote his own self and brand. BJP alleged that the state machinery forced school children and the general public to participate in this mega event to make it a success. 
"90 per cent of the school children who were standing in the human chain were forcibly pressurised to do so. Had people and school children voluntarily participated in the human chain event then it would have been a matter of pride. Nitish used this event for self branding. Wherever long human chains have been formed in the world, never have people or school children been forced to do so", said Sushil Kumar Modi,a senior BJP leader. 
The party also alleged that Nitish government turned the human chain event into a school children chain event since mostly children participated in it. BJP further claimed that the Patna High Court's directions with respect to not asking students to forcibly participate in human chain events has also been violated.
