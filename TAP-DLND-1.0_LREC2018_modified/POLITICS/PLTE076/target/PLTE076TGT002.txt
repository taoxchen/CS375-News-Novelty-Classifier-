GHAZIABAD: Congress vice-president Rahul Gandhi during the last leg of his roadshow on Thursday, passed through Ghaziabad on way to Delhi and made an unscheduled brief stoppage at number of places in Modinagar and Muradnagar amid cheering supporters. Congress leader, who hopes to revive party's political fortune in the upcoming assembly elections next year, interestingly took on Modi government on number of issues related to farmers. 
Rahul who had stopped in Modinagar at a famous 'shikanji' shop spoke after relishing local delicacy taking on Narender Modi. "Modiji has so much of time to visit foreign countries but has no time to meet farmers and the unemployed," said Rahul Gandhi. He hoped that Congress will form next government in the state and said, "there are number of issues that are plaguing farmers and I will pressurize this government to waive farm loan and will also press for slashing electricity bill by half."


'Jain Shikanji' located near Modinagar police station is a famous eatery shop in Modinagar and is frequented by many. But on Thursday, it was a pleasant surprise for its owner Swadesh Jain when Rahul Gandhi along with his entourage made an unscheduled stoppage at his shop.


"I was inside the shop when one of our worker Sachin came and informed that Rahul Gandhi is outside the shop and has demanded a glass of shikanji, saying 'ek fresh shikanji dena'," says Swadesh Jain, owner of 'Jain Shikanji.' "I rushed out and saw Rahul Gandhi and Raj Babbar along with many of his supporters taking sip of Shikanji, which Sachin, who was at the counter at that time, had handed over to them," adds Jain. "Later, Rahul ji ordered one plate of 'papri chatt' which he relished," continues Jain.


He remained there for about eight minutes and handed over a 500 rupee note before leaving the place.
