
Delhi University's Ramjas College witnessed heavy clashes between students belonging to the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) and All India Students Association (AISA). The clashes came a day after the college cancelled its invitation to Jawaharlal Nehru University (JNU) students Umar Khalid and Shehla Rashid to speak at a seminar, following violent protests by ABVP activists. 
On Tuesday, a large group of students belonging to the ABVP, disrupted the first session of a two-day seminar on 'Culture of Protest', organised by Wordcraft, the college's Literary Society. Calling Khalid “anti-national”, the members of the ABVP, which is affiliated to the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), and Delhi University Students Union (DUSU) refused to allow him to take part in the event. Khalid was among the three students charged with sedition for allegedly raising anti-national slogans at the JNU campus on February 9 last year. Even after Khalid's invitation was cancelled, the ABVP members went on a rampage destroying college properties. 
To protest against the disruption of the event and to demand action against the ABVP members, a section of teachers, students and AISA members of the Ramjas College had planned to take out a march to the Maurice Nagar police station on Wednesday. 
But they were stopped outside the college campus by ABVP members, following which clashes broke out between the student groups, despite a heavy deployment of police in the area. Even teachers and journalists were not spared in the melee. Prof. Prasanta Chakravarty, English Dept. DU attacked by #ABVP , admitted to Hindu Rao hospital. 
There were allegations that students were locked up inside the college and beaten up by members of the right-wing group. 
Rashid, former vice-president of JNUSU, claimed that the police did nothing to stop the ABVP members, and lathicharged the peaceful protesters instead. There were also allegations that many female protesters were groped during the clashes.
