Tension prevailed at Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU) late on Friday evening following a clash between members of the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) and Students Federation of India (SFI).
The clash was a result of protests staged by both groups over the news of a scuffle at Ramjas College in New Delhi and State Minister Vinod Tawde’s controversial statement. Members of SFI from SPPU alleged that ABVP members attacked them, resulting in the clash on the university campus.
Speaking to Sakal Times, Dr Sanjay Dabhade of the Democratic Youth Federation of India (DYFI) said a medico-legal case was registered with the local police following the clash. He added that the SFI members were protesting against Vinod Tawde’s and MLC Prashant Paricharak’s statements.
“We want the university officials to investigate the incident. The ABVP members assaulted the SFI activists, who were putting up posters protesting the Ramjas College incident and the remarks. A thorough investigation into the same should be carried out by the university officials to ensure that there is peace and security on the campus,” added Dabhade.
He said this is part of a nation-wide trend of intolerance. “Since the Sangh Pariwar and the BJP came to power at the Centre, State and now even in the municipal corporation, we see this devastating trend, where students are intolerant about opinions differing with their own,” said Dabhade.
However, speaking on the issue, Pradip Gavade, Secretary, ABVP, Pune, alleged that the clash was initiated by the members of SFI. “Our members had protested on Friday morning regarding the visit of Umar Khaleed and other anti-national elements at a college in Delhi. To oppose that, the members of SFI wrote ‘ABVP murdabad’ slogans on the university campus. They also assaulted our members. We have filed a complaint at the police station,” said Gavade.
