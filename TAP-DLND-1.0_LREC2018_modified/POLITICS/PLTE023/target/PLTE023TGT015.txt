Scuffle broke out between AISA and ABVP students on Wednesday over cancellation of JNU student Umar Khalid's talk at Ramjas College in Delhi. 

Ramjas college students were on a protest against ABVP students' disruption of a literary event at the institute. 

On Tuesday Umar Khalid and Shehla Rasheed were scheduled to take part in a session during a two-day seminar on "Culture of Protest" organised by Wordcraft, Ramjas college's Literary Society.

Members of Delhi University Students Union (DUSU) and RSS' students wing ABVP gathered outside the college and shouted slogans demanding that the invite to the "anti-nationals" be cancelled.
