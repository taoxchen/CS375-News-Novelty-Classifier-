The students and members of ABVP shouting slogans as JNU student Omer Khalid was to attend some program at Ramjas College in New Delhi, India, on Tuesday, February 21, 2017. 
Scuffles have broken out at the Ramjas College during a protest march against the ABVP’s disruption of a literary event in the college on Tuesday. Students from the college were supposed to marchg to Maurice Nagar police station, demanding action against the ABVP, when they were allegedly stopped by ABVP members. 
Amidst sloganeering and fights, police was deployed to bring the situation under control but the scuffles and the demonstrations did not stop. 
Students from Ramjas and other DU colleges say this is a “breach of freedom of expression”. The ABVP had on Tuesday disrupted an event in which JNU student Umar Khalid was speaking, prompting the college to take back an invitation for Wednesday to another JNU student Shehla Rashid.
