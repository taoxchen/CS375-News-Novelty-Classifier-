A clash broke out between students of left-affiliated AISA (All India Students Association) and the right wing Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) at Ramjas college on Wednesday a day after an invitation to JNU students Umar Khalid and Shehla Rashid for a panel talk was cancelled.
According to the police, the scuffle began after a group of students and teachers tried to take out a march demanding action against ABVP for alleged vandalism and disruption of a seminar which was scheduled on Tuesday. 
Professors and students from Ramjas planned a march from the campus to Maurice Nagar police station on Wednesday to condemn the alleged attack by ABVP, which had objected to the college's invite to JNU students Umar Khalid and Shehla Rashid for a panel talk.
Teachers and students were beaten up during violent clashes between two student groups
The ABVP members did not allow the march to proceed and allegedly locked the students and teachers inside Ramjas college while AISA members tried to rescue the captives. 
'Easily 15 ABVP students cornered my students. I have rescued five students myself from getting smashed on the floor,' Vinita Chandra, professor from Ramjas College told Mail Today. 
The college authorities have suspended classes for Thursday to avoid any further risk of problems. 
It's claimed that the ABVP had locked down the college's seminar room on Tuesday and pelted stones in protest against the invitation to the JNU students to address a seminar there. 
Students of Ramjas college during the protest march on Wednesday
'Some of the students, who were protesting were removed by the police force. However, none of them have been detained. Meanwhile, we have received cross complaints from both the ABVP and AISA students groups,' said a senior police official. 
Another professor from Ramjas said: 'We have begged the police to protect our students. My students were locked down for four hours in their own college premises. This is not about Left or Right. It is about violence. They have issued threats in the open to my students.' 
ABVP leader and former joint secretary of the JNU students' union Saurabh Sharma said: 'After a fight within AISA and Umar Khalid's supporters, DUSU had to intervene. Even ABVP students are hospitalised, forcing us to lodge an FIR.' 
Sharma however refuted the allegations of molestation against the girl students on the campus. 
'ABVP will continue to oppose Ramjas college's invite to someone who has raised the slogans of desh ki barbaadi and that too when this was not in the knowledge of college principal,' Sharma said. 
Condemning the attack on students while talking to Mail Today, Umar Khalid said: 'This is an attack on the democratic space Delhi University provides. The attackers want to create an environment of fear against speaking up but we will collectively fight back.'
