West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee urged President Pranab Mukherjee to "save the country from the mess" and sought a "national government" without Prime Minister Narendra Modi. 
Terming the current situation in the country as "unacceptable,” Banerjee, also the Trinamool Congress chief, said the "national government" could have another Bharatiya Janata Party leader at its helm. 
"Yesterday (Jan. 5) the president also voiced his concern about demonetization. We welcome his statement. We appeal to the President to save the country from the mess,” Banerjee said. 
"To save this country, let a national government be formed: Advaniji, Rajnathji or Jaitleyji can head it," she added, referring to BJP leaders L.K. Advani, Rajnath Singh and Arun Jaitley. 
She targeted Modi in particular for the adverse situation in the nation. 
"All the institutions are under attack. This is a dangerous game. The PM is behaving like Kalidas, trying to cut the branch he is sitting on," she said. 
"'He has to go." Banerjee continued, "President and Supreme Court have (a) role (to play)." 
Taking a swipe at the BJP leadership for proposing President's rule at the West Bengal after their party headquarters here was attacked, she said there should be president's rule at the center instead. 
"It is time for President's rule at the center," she said. 
Banerjee claimed a huge loss in terms of revenue due to the demonetization Nov. 8 and said a huge number of people in the state had been affected across various sectors because of the note ban. 
"We have lost Rs 5,500 crore revenue due to demonetization. 1.7 crore people in the state have been affected," she said after an administrative review meeting at the Town Hall here, adding 81.5 lakh workers had lost their jobs in sectors like tea, jute, jewelry and beedi industry. 
Comparing the center's governance with "terrorism", Banerjee also criticized the central government's decision to dismantle the Planning Commission and dissolve the Board for Industrial and Financial Reconstruction. 
She said the central government is "breaking the spine" of the country's economy with these decisions. 
"There is terrorism and hallabazi in the name of governance at the center. They even dismantled the Planning Commission and set up Niti Ayog with their own men who do not understand the country." 
"BIFR was constituted for reconstruction of industries and protection of workers. Sick industries registered in the Board could reconstruct their assets and run the company accordingly. Suddenly, the center dissolved it," she added.
