 The cricketer-turned-politician had been mum about his next political move after splitting from his party Awaaz-e-Punjab. 
Ahead of Punjab assembly elections, Navjot Singh Sidhu joined the Congress on Sunday. Sidhu met Congress Vice president Rahul Gandhi at his residence in Delhi.
On Wednesday, Punjab Congress chief Amarinder Singh had said that cricketer-turned-politician would soon join the party "unconditionally".

Navjot Singh Sidhu joins Congress. He met Congress Vice president Rahul Gandhi a short while ago. 
Singh had said Sidhu would be the party's candidate from Amritsar East, as announced by his wife Navjot Kaur Sidhu. "Sidhu's decision to join the Congress was unconditional. No strings attached," the former Punjab chief minister said.
"The delay in his joining was due to his professional commitments, which he wanted to conclude before getting into the election gear," Amarinder said in a statement.
