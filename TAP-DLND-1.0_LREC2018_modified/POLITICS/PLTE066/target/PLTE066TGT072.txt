
Shiromani Akali Dal president Sukhbir Singh Badal on Sunday criticised Navjot Singh Sidhu calling him a “first class opportunist” after the latter joined the Congress Party early in the day. The Deputy Chief Minister of Punjab said that the cricketer turned politician Navjot Singh Sidhu has been trying to fool the people of Punjab and stated that the voters cannot trust Sidhu as he “has taken more turns than any cricket pitch in the world.” 
As reported by NDTV, Sukhbir Singh Badal stated that “This man has taken more turns than any cricket pitch in the world. From singing praises to the SAD-BJP combine, he has courted the Aam Aadmi Party and now landed on the lap of the most hated Congress party. His shelf life in the Congress is also likely to be short.” He further stated that Navjot Singh Sidhu has been missing from the state from the past seven months and alleged that the former BJP MP has more interest in commercial investments. 
Sukhbir Badal on Twitter questioned Sidhu asking “Why have you come to the state 20 days before election? What took you so long? What was the bargain? Come clean and tell Punjabis.”
