
Former Cricketer Navjot Singh Sidhu today formally joined the Congress party after quitting BJP in September last year. Siddhu had left BJP in 2016, leading to a lot of speculations about him being a part of AAP. But Sidhu instead joined Congress and met the Congress Vice-President Rahul Gandhi today. This comes as a big asset for Congress ahead of the Punjab 2017 Assembly polls. Sidhu has earlier contested from Amritsar seat of Lok Sabha in 2004 and 2014.
