
Born October 20, 1963, Navjot Singh Sidhu, who formally joined Congress today, is a former Indian cricketer. He wears several hats. He is a politician, a political commentator and an entertainer. 
Having played over 50 Test matches and over 100 ODIs and scoring over 7,000 international runs, Sidhu retired from all forms of cricket in December 1999. 
After retirement from cricket, Sidhu took up television commentary, TV shows and politics. 
Though Sidhu's father was a Congress general secretary, Sidhu joined BJP. Congress' Punjab unit chief Capt Amarinder Singh had said in September 2016 that Sidhu has Congress in his DNA. All you need to know: Sidhu was elected to the Lok Sabha from Amritsar in 2004 on a BJP ticket. However, he resigned from Lok Sabha in 2006 on being convicted for homicide in a road rage incident of 1988. He appealed to the Supreme Court which suspended his sentence. In February 2007, he contested the Lok Sabha bye-election, again from Amritsar won. He contested the 2009 general elections and retained the Amritsar. Sidhu did not contest the 2014 Lok Sabha elections. He, instead, vacated it for Finance Minister Arun Jaitley who lost to Capt Amarinder Singh of Congress. He was offered to contest from any other seat but he declined. In a bid to placate him, the Narendra Modi government nominated Sidhu to the Rajya Sabha in April 2016. However, he resigned from the Rajya Sabha on July 18, 2016. Subsequently, Aam Aadmi Party courted him. Sidhu held negotiations with AAP convener and Delhi Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal . However, the talks failed and Sidhu refused to join AAP. Along with hockey player-turned-politician and Bains brothers, Sidhu launched a front named Awaz-e-Punjab (AeP) and lashed out at Kejriwal. Finally, he held negotiations with Congress. His wife Navjot Kaur Sidhu, who had resigned from BJP, and Pargat Singh joined Congress in November. 
Sidhu's chances of joining Congress grew brighter with his wife saying she and her husband were two souls and one body and that souls cannot remain away from the body. 
After prolonged negotiations and amidst uncertainties, Sidhu has finally joined Congress and is all set to start a new political inning.
