Cricketer-turned-politician Navjot Singh Sidhu , who ditched BJP some time back, today joined the Congress party. He met Congress Vice president Rahul Gandhi earlier this morning, according to ANI. 

Sidhu's wife, Navjot Kaur had joined Congress earlier. She had earlier said that Sidhu will join Congress and will contest from Amritsar East Assembly constituency. 

Sidhu's entry into Congress has been speculated for a long time. Earlier, he was in talks with AAP but the negotiations did not yield any result. 

Sidhu resigned from BJP in September last year and floated a political outfit called Awaaz-e- Punjab. The 52-year-old former cricketer had represented Amritsar in the Lok Sabha between 2004 and 2014. 

BJP decided not to field him from Amritsar in the 2014 parliamentary elections.
