AHMEDABAD: Three days ahead of the `Rail Roko' stir called by dalit activists to press for land rights, Jignesh Mevani , convener of Rashtriya Dalit Adhikar Manch (RDAM) and eight others, were booked under charges of unlawful assembly and damaging police vehicles. The move has raised eyebrows and is being dubbed as a precautionary measure by the state government to nip at the bud another major confrontation like the Patidar stir. Joint commissioner of police of city crime branch, J K Bhatt, said late on Tuesday night Navrangpura police had registered a case against Mevani, lawyer-activist Amrish Patel and seven others for causing damage to police vehicles and beating the driver of a police van, while they were being taken to the police stadium in Shahibaug area from Income Tax Crossroads, under detention.Police said earlier demonstrations by dalits have remained largely peaceful and confrontation was witnessed only during protests at Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation (AMC) head office at Danapith and Jamalpur bus depot."Without any prior permission, the demonstrators jammed traffic and shouted anti-government slogans" said Bhatt."Other accused named in the FIR are Mana Pateliya, Ramesh Parmar, Mukesh Patel, Darshan Pithadiya, Mahesh Fuliya, Darshan Parmar and RTI activist Bharatsinh Zala. They had allegedly thrashed the driver of the prison van and damaged seats and glass windows of vehicle," said Bhatt."Until now, we have kept a restrained and controlled approach, but protest demonstrations without per mission, violence during such demonstrations and disturbing the routine of other citizens will not be tolerated" Bhatt said.Mevani dubbed the charges as ridiculous, and said the agitation was well within his constitutional rights. "I pray to the state government to accept our legitimate demands of allotting lands to landless dalits," Mevani said."If the government can go beyond constitution to provide package to Patidars, if it can hold dialogues with Patidars and withdraw cases against them, then why not listen to our demands?" asked Mevani."We have no issues with Patidars, we are against the double standards of the Gujarat government," said Mevani, who was detained thrice within a fortnight.
