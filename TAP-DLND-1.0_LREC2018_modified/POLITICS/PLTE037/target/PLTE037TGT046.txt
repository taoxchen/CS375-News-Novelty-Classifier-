
George HW Bush has been admitted to intensive care, a spokesperson for the former US president has confirmed. 
In a statement, Jim McGrath said the 92-year-old had been admitted to the intensive care unit in the Houston Methodist Hospital to address an "acute respiratory problem stemming from pneumonia". 
The former president was sedated for a procedure to 'protect and clear' his airway. 
He is said to be stable and resting comfortably.

George HW Bush Bush served as the 41st US President from 1989 to 1993, and is the father of the 43rd President George W Bush. 
It was reported today that neither he or his wife would be attending the inauguration of President-elect Donald Trump this week.
