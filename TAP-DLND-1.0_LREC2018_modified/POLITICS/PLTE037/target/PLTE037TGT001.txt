Spokesman Jim McGrath said in an email to Reuters that Mr Bush, who at 92 is America's oldest living ex-president, has "responded very well to treatments" at the Houston Methodist Hospital. 
"Doctors and everyone are very pleased, and we hope to have him out soon." 
Mr and Mrs Bush are the parents of former President George W. Bush and former Florida Governor Jeb Bush, who sought the 2016 Republican presidential nomination. 
Mr Bush, a Republican like his sons, was vice president during Ronald Reagan's two White House terms before being elected president in 1988. He lost his re-election bid to Bill Clinton, then the Democratic governor of Arkansas, in 1992. 
A naval pilot during World War Two, he became an occasional skydiver after leaving the presidency and marked his 90th birthday with a jump out of a helicopter. 
He has used a wheelchair in his later years and was hospitalized twice in 2014, once for seven weeks with pneumonia and again for breathing difficulties. In July 2015, he broke a bone in his neck in a fall at the family home in Maine. 
Mr Bush was already planning to miss President-elect Donald Trump's inauguration on Friday due to health reasons. 
In a letter to Donald Trump earlier this month, he said: "My doctor says if I sit outside in January, it likely will put me six feet under."
