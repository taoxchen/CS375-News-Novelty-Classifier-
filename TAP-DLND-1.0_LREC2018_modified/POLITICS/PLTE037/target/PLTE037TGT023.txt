
Former US president George H W Bush has been hospitalised in Houston, according to media reports quoting his former chief of staff. 
Bush chief of staff Jean Becker said the 41st president was recently admitted to Houston Methodist Hospital after becoming ill, the Houston Chronicle and KHOU-TV reported early Wednesday. 
Becker did not specify the reason the 92-year-old was hospitalised. 
Both media outlets say his office expects him to go home in a couple of days. 
Bush was hospitalised in Maine in 2015 after falling at his summer home and breaking a bone in his neck. 
He served as US president from 1989 to 1993.
