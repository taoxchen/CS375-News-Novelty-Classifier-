
He played the Hitler…err Haanikarak Baapu to her in his movie Dangal. Aamir Khan was at his best in the movie and 16 year old girl Zaira Wasim too ensured that she delivers her excellent performer. And now when the whole wide world is talking about the issue revolving around Wasim’s controversy, Aamir Khan is nowhere to be seen question Twitterati. From Anupam Kher to Javed Akhtar to Phogat sister themselves have come in support for Wasim. Should Mr Perfectionist express his views too? Or given his stardom if he speaks it will add more fuel to the already blazing fire? 
A seemingly innocuous meeting with Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Mehbooba Mufti has been sensationalised claim reports. Wasim who portrayed the character of young Geeta Phogat in the Aamir Khan’s Dangal posted an apology Facebook page for‘offending’ people.Post this she recived mixed reactions. “This is an open confession/apology. I know that many people have been offended and displeased by my recent actions or by the people I have recently met,” she wrote. 
Former Chief Minister of J&K Omar Abdullah jumped in her support. He took to Twitter to state this “A 16 year old shouldn’t be forced to apologise and that too allegedly for meeting @mehbooba Mufti. What are we coming too. I’ve a problem with @MehboobaMufti trying to userp other peoples success to cover up her own failures but why punish/troll people she meets? These people succeed IN SPITE of the Govt not because of it so it’s best to let them enjoy their success instead of trying to hijack it.” 
The real Dangal sisters, Phogat sisters – Geeta and Babita – too came in for support. Babita Phoghat tweeted, “Zaira Wasim you r deal. No pressure should b enough to make u feel apologetic about ur achievements.Go ahead,chase ur dreams,inspire!!” 
When such sentiments are pouring in, should Aamir Khan express his opinion too? But given the past and the experiences he had where his comments could be twisted and tweaked so far the 51-year-old star has not spoken about the issue. Perhaps the superstar is waiting for this issue to die its natural death, we would like to assume.
