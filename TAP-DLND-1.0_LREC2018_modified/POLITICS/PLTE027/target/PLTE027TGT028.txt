 Zaira, who is Kashmiri, played the young version of wrestler Geeta Phogat in Dangal Aamir Khan's 16-year-old Dangal co-star Zaira Wasim has posted an apology on Facebook, apparently in response to being massively trolled for meeting Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Mehbooba Mufti on Saturday. Zaira, who is Kashmiri, played the young version of wrestler Geeta Phogat in Dangal , which released last month to great acclaim. In her Facebook post, Zaira writes, "This is an open confession/apology. I know that many people have been offended and displeased by my recent actions or by the people I have recently met." Zaira urges everyone to treat her as a 16-year-old, writing, "I hope people still remember that I'm a just a 16 year old girl and I hope you treat me accordingly." Over the weekend, Zaira and her parents met Ms Mufti and discussed Zaira's education, career and experiences while filming Dangal . Ms Mufti said in the state council today, "When it comes to talent, youngsters from Jammu and Kashmir are next to none and we must celebrate them and not those who post their photos on Facebook with guns in their hands." The meeting allegedly led to Zaira, who lives in Srinagar, being trolled. Her Facebook post has also received some comments like "This profile is fake" and "Why are you meeting culprits instead of victims" - other comments assure her that she has done nothing wrong and need not apologise. "You are a true model" reads one comment, although Zaira writes in her post: "The first and foremost thing is that I am being projected as a role model for Kashmiri Youth. I want to make it very clear that I do not want anyone to follow in my foot steps or even consider me as a role model." She has already been cast in Aamir Khan's next production, Secret Superstar . There is some speculation that Zaira's account, which is verified, may have been hacked because of this response from her to one comment: "The profile might be fake but the sentiments behind this post aren't. Trust me." Zaira Wasim, who grew up in Srinagar, appeared in a handful of commercials before being cast in Dangal . She plays Geeta, the elder daughter of Aamir Khan's character, Mahavir Singh Phogat. Dangal is the true story of how Mr Phogat trained his daughters Geeta and Babita as medal-winning wrestlers. The older version of Geeta Phogat is played in the film by actress Fatima Sana Sheikh.
