The Uttarakhand BJP legislators will meet today in the presence of party’s central observers to decide the new Chief Minister of the State. 
BJP Sate President Ajay Bhatt has asked all 57 BJP MLAs to be present in Dehradun today for the meeting. The BJP had won 57 seats in the 70-member Assembly.

The State BJP continues to maintain suspense over its chief ministerial candidate by first announcing the date for the oath taking ceremony and much later the date for the BJP legislatures party meeting. 
However, Trivendra Singh Rawat, a leader with RSS background, has emerged as the front-runner in the race for the coveted chief minister’s post in Uttarakhand. 
Prime Minister Narendra Modi, BJP National president Amit Shah and other leaders of the party will attend the oath taking ceremony.
