Former RSS Pracharak Trivendra Singh Rawat will be new CM of Uttarakhand . He was elected leader of BJP legislature party on Saturday afternoon. 

Trivendra has won from Doiwala seat of Dehradun. He has served as in-charge of BJP' Jharkhand unit. He is also BJP's National Secretary. 

He will take oath as CM on Saturday in presence of PM Narendra Modi and BJP's national president Amit Shah . 

His name was considered among frontrunners from the very beginning. BJP has got massive mandate by wining 57 seats in 70-member House.
