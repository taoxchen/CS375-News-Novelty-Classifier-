BJP's Trivendra Singh to be sworn in as new CM of Uttarakhand on Saturday in BJP's Trivendra Rawat to take oath as Uttarakhand CM on Saturday Mr. Trivendra Singh Rawat was chosen as the leader of the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) legislature party in Uttarakhand here today, paving the way for his swearing-in as the new Chief Minister of the state tomorrow. Mr. Rawat, 57, was elected at a meeting of the newly-elected MLAs of the party at a meeting here today. Later, Mr. Rawat met Governor K. K. Paul, along with other leaders of the party, and staked claim to form the next government in the state. Mr. Paul invited him to form the new government and he will take oath of office and secrecy tomorrow, sources said. Mr. Rawat, a former pracharak of the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS), the ideological mentor of the BJP, has been elected to the Uttarakhand Legislative Assembly from the Doiwala constituency, from where has won twice earlier. He had played a key role for the BJP in Uttar Pradesh during the 2014 Lok Sabha elections and also in Jharkhand during the Assembly elections there. In the Assembly elections held on February 15, the BJP had won a landslide victory, bagging 57 seats in the 70-member House, while the ruling Congress got just 11. Two seats went to independent candidates. "I am grateful that the BJP legislature party has given me this responsibility. Ours will be a corruption-free government. Poverty eradication will be our top priority,” Mr. Rawat said after his election today. Prime Minister Narendra Modi and BJP President Amit Shah are expected to attend the swearing-in ceremony here tomorrow.
