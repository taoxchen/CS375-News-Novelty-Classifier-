Pakistani police say 104 fishermen from India were detained in the Arabian Sea for violating Pakistan’s territorial waters. The fishermen were detained by Pakistan’s Maritime Security Agency and brought to the southern port city of Karachi where they were handed over to the police. The fishermen are due to appear in court. Pakistani and Indian fishermen who ply the waters off the two countries’ coasts are often arrested for allegedly violating territorial waters. One of the detained fishermen, appealed to the Indian government to intervene for their release.
