Civil Judge South Salman Mujahid s court has on Monday sent 104 Indian fishermen to Karachi jail who were presented by Docks Police, reported Dunya News. According to details, these fishermen were arrested by Pakistan Maritime Security Agency (PMSA) on Saturday on the charge of illegally fishing within the territorial sea limits of Pakistan. 104 Indian fishermen were arrested by PMSA and 19 wooden boats were seized from them.
