Pakistan Maritime Security Agency (PMSA) has on Saturday arrested over 100 Indian fishermen on the charge of illegally fishing within the territorial sea limits of Pakistan. 
As per details, over 100 Indian fishermen were arrested by PMSA when they were fishing within the sea limits of Pakistan as 19 boats were also seized from them. 
They were later handed over to the police for further legal action. 
Meanwhile, the security of maritime borders had further been tightened after the incident.
