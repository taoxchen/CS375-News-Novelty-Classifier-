Pakistani security forces have arrested 104 Indian fishermen in the last few days for entering the country's territorial waters at a time of heightened tensions between the two nations, a police official told EFE on Monday. 
Abid Ali, police spokesperson of Karachi port, where the fishermen are being held, said the detainees were traveling in six boats that entered Pakistani waters without authorization, and that they will be presented before a judge in the next few days.
