The indefinite strike called by the Meat Murga Vyapar Kalyan Samiti (MMVKS) further intensified and spread across the state on Monday as more and more meat sellers and eateries joined the protest. 
The strike continued successfully across Uttar Pradesh and over 90 percent closure was reported from various regions. 
"Barely 10 percent shops, and that too selling only chicken and eggs, were seen functioning in Meerut, Muzaffarnagar, Saharpur, Bhagpat and other adjoining districts. The eateries catering only non - veg foods closed down gradually by evening", a resident of western UP informed MAIL TODAY. 
The MMVKS also made similar claims. "The stock (of red meat) will end in the next few hours and the eateries will have no option than to shut down. The business cannot survive only on chicken", Iqbal Qureshi, president of the MMVKS said. 
The strike will continue and more sellers will automatically join in, he added. "We will continue until the government comes up with an amicable solution", Qureshi said. 
Meanwhile in Lucknow, all the famous joints preferred to remain closed in protest. Tunday Kababi pulled down its shutters on Sunday. "Chickens are not ment for kebabs. With our famous buffalo kebabs missing from menu, we are facing loss ", Mohammad Usman, owner of an outlet of the chain said.
