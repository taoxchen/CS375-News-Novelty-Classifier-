In a move to protest against the crackdown on illegal and mechanised slaughterhouses in Uttar Pradesh, meat sellers across the state would go on an indefinite strike from tomorrow. Commenting on the issue, Mubeen Qureshi, Lucknow Bakra Gosht Vypar Mandal office bearer, said, “We have decided to intensify our strike from tomorrow. All shops will remain closed. Fish sellers too have joined us and are extending support to us.”
Apart from meat vendors, even the fish vendors are reported to have resolved to join the protest which has seen non-vegetarian delicacies go off the menu in several parts of the state. Further speaking on the issue, Qureshi said that in the wake of the crackdown by the new UP government, there was no question of the strike being called off anytime soon. “It will go on indefinitely,” he added. Yogi Adityanath’s ban on illegal slaughterhouse of the state have hit non-vegetarian food chains to the worse as these outlets have shifted to mutton and chicken dishes after buffalo meats became scare. The outlets include some of UP’s famous eating joints such as Tunday and Rahim’s.
“The meat sellers are piqued over the crackdown on slaughter houses which has adversely hit the livelihood of lakhs of people,” Qureshi said. Soon after forming government in the state, Adityanath and his team has ordered closure of illegal slaughterhouses and strict enforcement of the ban on cow smuggling to fulfil a key electoral promise as mentioned in BJP ’s election manifesto.
Speaking on the development, BJP national spokesman Sambit Patra said in Delhi that the government in UP was only following a court order as illegal abattoirs were contributing to state’s ill health by getting ground water polluted. He also claimed those running meat outlets legally and in accordance with norms were not being victimised.
