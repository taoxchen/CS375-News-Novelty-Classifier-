The Lucknow Bench of the Allahabad high court on Monday sought explanation from the state government and the Lucknow Municipal Corporation (LMC) as to under which authority the meat shops were forced to shut down in the city. It further took to task the LMC authorities on their lethargy to take decision regarding renewal of licenses to run meat shops. Granting three days to the state and LMC to file their response, the court set the matter for next hearing on April 3. 

A bench of justice AP Sahi and justice Sanjai Harkauli passed the order on a pending petition filed by Shahabuddin and nine other shopkeepers. 

All meat sellers and several non-veg restaurants are on strike in the city since the past two days protesting crackdown by LMC officials against " unlicensed shops ". The crackdown began on March 21 soon after BJP government assumed power. 

The petitioners had pleaded that they were granted licenses to run meat shops. When the term of license expired, they applied for renewal. But LMC seems reluctant to renew the licenses on the pretext that as per guidelines of the National green Tribunal (NGT), the MC had no slaughter houses of its own and as such the licenses could not be renewed. 

The petitioners' counsel GC Sinha argued that if the LMC had no slaughter houses, it was no fault of the license holders. The petitioners charged the LMC officials that they allowed to run the shops after taking bribes and if licenses are granted, the shop keepers would not grease the palm of the LMC officials. 

On the said petition, the court had in 2015 directed the state and the LMC to file response but the same was not filed till date. 

Meanwhile, the petitioners filed an application in the said petition alleging therein that on one hand the LMC was not granting licenses or renewing it and on the other hand LMC officials have started forcing the shopkeepers to shut shops even without passing any orders.
