Slaughter house owners and meat sellers in many districts including state capital Lucknow, Allahabad, Agra, Kushi Nagar and Aligarh have announced indefinite strike against government’s crackdown on illegal slaughter houses. 
They have also protested against municipal authorities and police raids on their establishments alleging exploitation by the authorities. 
They have complained that they are being raided by the police despite having licences. They have also said that they are forced to close their shops due to shortage of supplies. At some places fish sellers too have joined the strike extending their support to meat sellers. 
Butcher shop owners have said that their incomes had fallen by as much as a half because of the crackdown and authorities have forced the chicken shops to install curtains in front of them. Many others have complained that the municipal authorities were dragging its feet on issuing fresh licenses and renewing old ones. 
Municipal authorities and police have maintained that they were only following procedure as per municipal anti pollution acts and no shop and slaughter house will be allowed without proper license. 
After coming into power Adityanath Yogi government has ordered closure of illegal slaughter houses and strict enforcement of the ban on cow smuggling as promised in BJP’s Sankalp Patra.
