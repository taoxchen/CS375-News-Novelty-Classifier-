"Our agitation and march towards Delhi has been called off," AIJASS chairperson Yashpal Malik says 
After holding talks with Haryana Chief Minister Manohar Lal Khattar on Sunday agitating Jat leaders postponed their protests and called off their plan to gherao the Parliament in New Delhi on Monday. 
"Our agitation and march towards Delhi has been called off," All India Jat Aarakshan Sangharsh Samiti Chairperson Yashpal Malik said after the meeting. 
Meanwhile, Mr. Khattar addressed the media saying "Centre and Haryana will soon begin the process of giving Jats reservation." 
All dharnas in Haryana are to be lifted by March 26 except a few token dharnas by the samiti. 
Earlier in the day, Mr. Khattar held talks with the agitating Jat leaders from the State on the quota issue. Besides Mr. Khattar, Union Cabinet Minister Birender Singh was also present in the meeting.
