Former prime minister Manmohan Singh, in the presence of state leaders, including PPCC chief Capt Amarinder Singh, on Monday unveiled the Congress manifesto for Punjab at the party headquarters here.
Manmohan Singh said: “The election manifesto of the Congress is a visionary document, which promises the people of Punjab that we'll undo the damage done by the previous government in the last 10 years.”
Amarinder Singh said: “We will end the drugs menace in four weeks in Punjab.”
The Congress screening committee met in Delhi on Sunday to discuss 40 pending seats. It was learnt that 18 seats have been sealed and discussions on the remaining will continue ahead of the central election committee’s meeting on January 10.
Punjab will go to the polls on February 4.
