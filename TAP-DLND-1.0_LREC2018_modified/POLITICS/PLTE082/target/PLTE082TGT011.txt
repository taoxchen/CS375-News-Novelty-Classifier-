Few friendships run as deep as that of AIADMK General Secretary Sasikala Natarajan and former Tamil Nadu Chief Minister J. Jayalalithaa. While Sasikala might have enjoyed a life of luxury and inherited a position of power in AIADMK by virtue of having been Amma’s closest aide, this very relationship has also cost her dearly. Death might’ve done Jayalalithaa and Sasikala apart; but their misdeeds, during the former’s reign as CM, has certainly bound them to a life beyond death in prison. Sasikala who has been convicted in the disproportionate assets case has now been lodged in the Parappana Agrahara Central Prison in Bengaluru.
Here are interesting facts about how Sasikala spent her first night in prison and what her days ahead will be like– a stark contrast from her aspirations of usurping the TN CM throne by virtue of having been Amma’s soul-sister.

Sasikala is lodged in a 10X8 cell on the first floor of the women’s block at Parappana Agrahara Central Prison in Bengaluru. She will serve her sentence of four years in jail from this cell. She shares this cell with co-accused and sister-in-law, J Elavarasi. She spent the first night of her jail term on the floor and slept on a straw mat. In the morning she had tamarind rice with chutney from the breakfast menu. Her request for a class 1 cell with privileges such as TV and home-cooked food were denied. As an inmate, she has currently been assigned the task of making candles. She will be earning a daily wage of Rs 50 by making candles.
