
The Election Commission of India has asked VK Sasikala to respond to a petition that contests her appointment to the position of All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazagham’s general secretary. The petition, submitted by caretaker chief minister O Panneerselvam, her rival, claims that the appointment was against rules: she was chosen by the general council of the party and not the primary members, the rebels said. Sasikala, who is currently in a jail in Bengaluru for holding assets exceeding her known sources of income, has until February 28 to respond, failing which the commission will continue its proceedings against her. Panneeselvam — or, as he is popularly called, OPS — and his supporters submitted two petitions contesting Sasikala’s appointment to the most powerful position on December 29. The development comes a day after Edappadi Palaniswamy — a minister under both former chief minister J Jayalalithaa as well as OPS — was appointed to head the AIADMK government after the Supreme Court held Sasikala guilty in a 19-year-old disproportionate case, in which both she and Jayalalithaa were accused. Sasikala’s February 5 selection by her party to take over the government from O Panneerselvam has split the party. An initially seemingly agreeable Panneerselvam (Sasikala repeatedly mentioned that it was he who suggested she take over as chief minister) rebelled two days later: he accused Sasikala — a confidante of late chief minister J Jayalalithaa — of having arm-twisted him into resigning. The days that followed saw accusations fly, as each marshalled supporters to bolster their own case. Palaniswami will be sworn in on Saturday. However, he must stand a floor test in the state assembly. —
