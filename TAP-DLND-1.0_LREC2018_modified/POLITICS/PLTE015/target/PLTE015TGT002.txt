The Bharatiya Janata Party has expressed shock over the supreme court's refusal to deliver its verdict on Jallikattu before Pongal and said the central government is discussing with legal experts steps to conduct the bull taming sport. Speaking to reporters here, the party's state unit president Tamilisai Soundararajan said, "the apex court's verdict amounts to treating a patient tomorrow at a time when he badly needs treatment today itself." She said the BJP-led government in the Centre is certainly not against Jallikattu. "The union government's counsel has clearly told the court that the bull taming sport should be conducted." When asked whether the BJP   defying the central government's ban, she said, "it is not right to talk like this at this point of time. But BJP is sure in favour of the sport." The supreme court on Thursday rejected a plea urging it to pass judgement on bull taming sport Jallikattu before the harvest festival of Pongal on Saturday. A bench comprising justices Dipak Misra and R Banumathi told a group of lawyers, who requested for the verdict, that it is unfair to ask the bench to pass an order. The apex court, however, said that the draft of judgement has been prepared but it was not possible to deliver it before Saturday when Jallikattu is to be organised.
