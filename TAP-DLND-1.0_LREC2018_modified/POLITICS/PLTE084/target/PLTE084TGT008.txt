The Samajwadi Party-Congress seems to be on rocks as the SP has released the first list of its 191 of 209 candidates for first three phases of ensuing UP Assembly elections here on Friday. 
The SP took the lead and came out with the list which was supposed to be released after the formal joint announcement of the pact between the two parties in the presence of senior SP and Congress leaders on Friday. 
Talking to the media persons after the announcement of the names of 191 party candidates, Samajwadi Party national vice president Kiranmoy Nanda said that Congress was offered 18 seats to Congress in the first three phases on the basis of the position of their candidates in the previous 2012 Assembly elections, however, he added that the alliance was not yet over. 
“SP is willing to spare 80 seats to Congress, now the call has to be taken by them,” he told the media persons. He went on to add that SP had offered 54 those seats to the grand old party where it had its sitting MLAs (28) or those who remained runner up in 2012. “Over and above this figure of 54, we can spare 20 more seats to them,” Nanda stated. 
As per the seat sharing formula, the SamajwadiParty has decided to field its candidates on all the Assembly seats in Amethi and leave one out of five in Rae Bareli for the Congress. The two districts are known Congress bastions. 
On the issue of releasing the list apparently without Congress being in the loop, Nanda said in case of an alliance SP would withdraw and adjust candidates on seats that should go to Congress. 
Meanwhile, the list of 191 released by SP state unit chief Naresh Uttam to the media for the first three phases of seven-phase UP elections commencing on February 11, comprises of the name of beleaguered ex state president of the party Shivpal Yadav from his traditional Jaswant Nagar seat. Shivpal has been representing Jaswantnagar in Assembly since 1996. 
Among the other prominent name in the SP list include Azam Khan’s son Abdullah Azam from Suar (Rampur), Azam Khan himself from his traditional Rampur seat. While Naresh Agarwal’s son Nitin has been given ticket from Hardoi and Akhilesh loyalist minister Arvind Singh Gope has got it from Ramnagar (Barabanki). Gope has replaced Rakesh Verma, son of senior Samajwadi Party leader and Rajya Sabha member Beni Prasad Verma. Rakesh was given ticket by the then SP state unit chief Shivpal. 
The other prominent name which has failed to make it to this list is that of mafia don-turn-politician Atiq Ahmad who has been replaced by Mohammad Hassan Rumi on Kanpur Cantt seat. Atiq had on Thursday made it clear that he would not contest as he did not want his tiant to tarnish Akhilesh Yadav’s image. 
Among the SP candidates declared on Friday, 52 are Muslims and 18 women. Despite Akhilesh’s reservations over fielding tainted faces with criminal credentials, Vijay Singh, the main accused in the murder of BJP leader Brahmdutt Dwivedi has got the ticket from Farrukhabad. 
Filing of nominations for the second phase of Uttar Pradesh Assembly polls on February 15 began on Friday, covering 67 constituencies spread over 11 districts in Muslim-dominated western UP. The filing of nominations will continue till January 27. Withdrawal of candidature could be done till February 1 and polling will be on February 15.
