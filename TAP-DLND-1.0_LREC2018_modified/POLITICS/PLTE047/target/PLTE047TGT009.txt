
Haryana Speaker Kanwar Pal has said that the assembly would discuss the ongoing Jat agitation in the state on Wednesday.
Indian National Lok Dal (INLD) leader Abhay Singh Chautala moved an adjournment motion during the Budget Session e on Tuesday. The Congress party, led by former Chief Minister Bhupinder Singh Hooda, also wanted the issue to be discussed, but Pal had refused citing an ongoing court case over Jat reservation.
Pal later changed his mind after the INLD and Congress claimed that the protests were not limited to demands of reservation.
Chautala accused the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) government in the state under Chief Minister Manohar Lal Khattar of not fulfilling protesters demands despite agreeing to them last year.
Statewide protests by the community began on January 29. Talks between the Haryana government and Jat leaders hit a roadblock recently after the former said it could not withdraw cases being investigated by the Central Bureau of Investigation against some Jat leaders over violence during the agitation last year.
Among the community’s various demands are reservation, jobs to the next of kin of those killed in violence in the Jat agitation last year, compensation to those injured, withdrawal of cases against them and action against the officers who ordered action against the Jats, among others.
Violence during the agitation last year had left 30 people dead and over 200 injured. Government and private property worth hundreds of crores of rupees was damaged during the violence in February 2016.
