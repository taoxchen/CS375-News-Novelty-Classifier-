
Sham Maandi, district convener, Loktantra Surkasha Manch, has said it was not Kurukshetra MPRaj Kumar Saini but UP Jat leader Yashpal Malik who was trying to divide society. Addressing mediapersons here today, Maandi said the state government should rein in Malik, who was allegedly bent upon vitiating the peaceful social environment by his inflammatory speeches. Malik was trying to turn the peaceful agitation violent, he alleged.
