Veteran South Indian actor Kamal Haasan on Monday came forward in favour of the popular ancient bull-taming sport, Jallikattu of Tamil Nadu, which was banned by Supreme court citing animal welfare issues. 
Reiterating his opinion over the ban on the popular sport , the Tamil Nadu superstar said it is a traditional sport of the state and should be allowed to be continued. 
He said that those who think the Jallikattu leads to cruelty to animals should stop consuming biryani as well as slaughtering of animals is cruelty. 
“It is about taming the bull and not creating physical harm by breaking its horns or other body parts.” the actor said. 
“If you want a ban on jallikattu, let’s also ban biryani,” Kamal opined at the recently held India Today Conclave, adding that he is one the few actors who has played the bull sport and is proud of the tradition. 
Justifying his take on the sport, Kamal said: “More people die in motor vehicle accidents than ever would happen on bull fight.” 
The 62-year-old actor said that the bull sport is not similar to the Spanish bull sport. The bulls in Jallikattu are not harmed and in fact they are treated like Gods. 
“It is about taming the bull and not creating physical harm by breaking its horns or other body parts,” he said. 
The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) has also come out against the ban on Monday accusing the Animal Welfare Board of India of working at the behest of “petro-dollar mafias”. It also said that they are working to undermine the culture importance of the bull taming sport. 
The Supreme Court banned Jallikattu in Tamil Nadu in May 2014 citing cruelty to bulls.
