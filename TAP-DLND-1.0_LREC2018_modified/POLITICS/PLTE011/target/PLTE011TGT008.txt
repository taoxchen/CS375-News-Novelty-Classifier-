In 2014, the supreme court had banned Jallikattu under the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals Act and now that there is less than a week for the harvest festival, there are people who are striking a pro-jallikattu debate. Defending the game of jallikattu currently banned in Tamil Nadu, Kamal Haasan expressed his views. Being a big fan of the sport, he said that those who hate the bull sport should give up on biryani too. Talking at the India Today South Conclave in Chennai, Kamal Haasan said, "If you want a ban on jallikattu, let's also ban biryani. I'm a big fan of jallikattu. I'm probably one of few actors who've played jallikattu. Am a proud Tamilian, this is our culture.” This is not the first time that the actor expressed his opinion on restarting the ‘tradition’. Earlier too he told people to not get confused between jallikattu and bull-fighting in Spain. Justifying his stand, he had said that in Spain, people hurt the animal and it would die, but Tamil Nadu treats bulls as "god, as part of the family".
