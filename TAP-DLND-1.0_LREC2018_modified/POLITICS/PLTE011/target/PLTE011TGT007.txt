Star hero, director and producer Kamal Haasan has came up with a crazy demand. He had asked to ban biryani, the favourite of dish many people across India. 
But do you know, why he has asked to ban biryani? 
Defending Jallikattu, a traditional sport of Tamil Nadu involving bulls, Kamal Haasan made these comments. 
“Those who are opposing Jallikattu and advocating for its ban, on the basis of cruelty towards animals, should also ban biryani,” said Kamal Haasan in an interview to Tamil media persons. 
Kamal Haasan further said that Jallikattu does not involve any cruelty towards bulls, and comparing it to bull fight of Spain is completely out of place. 
It is a known fact that Kamal Haasan is an ardent fan of Jallikattu and even showcased the game in his movies. 
Jallikattu has once again come into discussion as Tamil Nadu Chief Minister O Pannerselvam wrote a letter to Prime Minister seeking an Ordinance on resuming Jallikattu. Jallikattu was banned by Supreme Court in 2014.
