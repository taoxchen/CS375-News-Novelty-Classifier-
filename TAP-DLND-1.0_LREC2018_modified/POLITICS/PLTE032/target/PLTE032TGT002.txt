Uddhav is a harmonious leader, just that he needs to be made aware of his role, says the chief minister

Mumbai: Even as Shiv Sena, the junior partner in the Maharashtra government, continues to play the role of the opposition, Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis said that he is keen on alliance with Sena for the upcoming local body elections in the state.

Speaking to reporters on Monday, Fadnavis said, “Uddhav Thackeray is a harmonious leader, only he has to be made aware of the role. There will be no alliance with Congress, Nationalist Congress Party or Maharashtra Navnirman Sena.”

Recently, Fadnavis faced flak for mediating between filmmaker Karan Johar and MNS chief Raj Thackeray, who had threatened that it won’t allow release of any movie in India that featured Pakistani artistes.

First time since that controversy, Fadnavis spoke up and said, “Mediated in the protest of MNS so that there is no tension during the Diwali festival. The mediation had the support of the government.” He added, “We are not going to go with Raj Thackeray [for the upcoming elections].”
