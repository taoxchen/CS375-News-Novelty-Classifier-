The Indian army will not accept any amount related to the release of Karan Johar's Ae Dil Hai Mushkil, sources of a leading news channel have claimed on the microblogging site Twitter. "Army will not accept donation of Rs 5 crore offered by Karan Johar's Dharma Productions ahead of 'Ae Dil Hai Mushkil' release," claimed sources of the channel.
The producers of Ae Dil Hai Mushkil had recently met with MNS chief Raj Thackeray and Maharashtra CM Devendra Fadnavis to resolve the issue of the Bollywood release during Diwali. Raj Thackeray was against letting the film release because it had Pakistan actor Fawad Khan, which he said would show disrespect for the martyrs if his film was allowed to release.
The solution of the meeting came in the form, Thackeray pronouncing that he would advise his activists to not resort to violence if producer/director Karan Johar agreed to pay Rs 5 crore as a penance for working with Pak actor Fawad Khan. And he went on to add saying that all films which featured an actor from across the border would be applicable for this 'penalty'.
Several ex-Armymen and veterans had been vocal about how the army should not be made a part of the scheme of local party politics. And that the army should refuse the amount. Now if the sources of the armed forces are to be believed, they will not accept any amount as penalty for the release of Ae Dil Hai Mushkil.
