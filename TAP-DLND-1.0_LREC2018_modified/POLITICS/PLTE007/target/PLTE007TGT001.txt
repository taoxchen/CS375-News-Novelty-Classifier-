 A day after External Affairs Minister Sushma Swaraj’s warning to Amazon, the online shopping giant has apologised for selling Indian Flag-themed doormats on its website. 
Earlier in the day, Amazon removed the items from its website after EAM’s warning to cancel visa for their employees and demanded unconditional apology. 
“Amazon India is committed to respecting Indian laws and customs…At no time did we intend or mean to offend Indian sentiments,” Amit Agarwal, Vice President and Country Manager, Amazon India said in his letter to Swaraj.

The doormats bearing Indian flag had outraged many people among the Indian diaspora in Canada, who posted pictures of them on social media and urged the foreign minister to formally object.
