
Hyderabad University students held a major protest on the first death anniversary of Dalit student Rohith Vemula who had committed suicide last year. The student protested in order to seek justice for Rohith, demanding the arrest of the Vice Chancellor. The student union said that the administration is trying to suppress there voices. However the administration and the police said that they are abiding by the orders from High Court. Students from various political wings have been protesting and soon will be joined by the brother of Mohammad Akhlaq who was lynched by a mob along with the mother of Najeeb Ahmed, student who went missing within the JNU campus and some other Dalit protestors.
