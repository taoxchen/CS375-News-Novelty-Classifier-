Calling BJP's student being ABVP the party's goon brigade, AAP has asked what action has been taken by Delhi Police against those ABVP members who allegedly indulged in violence at Ramjas College + last week. 

Senior party member Atishi Marlena said that Delhi Police, which has travelled to even Gujarat to arrest an AAP MLA, had failed to take action against a single ABVP member despite proof of violence against them. 

"A DU student who spoke out against ABVP violence , Gurmehar Kaur, is being explicitly threatened with rape but what has Delhi Police done about it? And this is not about specific threats. Women online are being threatened with death and rape all the time and many of those issuing these threats are people being followed by senior BJP members, including prime minister Narendra Modi," said Marlena. 

Sarita Singh, AAP MLA from Rohtas Nagar, said the BJP was trying to capture Indian universities through ABVP's 'gundagardi'. "DU and JNU have united over several issues in the past year. What has happened over the past two years that there is no space for dissent, that difference of opinion is tackled through violence?" she asked.
