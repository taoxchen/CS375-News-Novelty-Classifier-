
Delhi University student Gurmehar Kaur has hit back at her detractors, who have criticised and trolled her for signaling out ABVP as the wrecker-in-chief during the Ramjas College violence last week. Gurmehar Kaur made it clear that her opposition to the ABVP was not on the grounds of ideology but for what happened on the Delhi University campus last week. Gurmehar said, “Stones were not pelted on Umar Khalid (the JNU student, who had been invited to a DU event, which the ABVP objected to). He was not even present there. The stones were pelted on students who were there.” The ABVP has maintained that violence took place as it opposed invitation to JNU student Umar Khalid. Gurmehar Kaur has been trolled for taking a stand against ABVP, the RSS-affiliated student union. Her trolls include several celebrities including former India cricketer Virender Sehwag and bollywood actor Randeep Hooda. But, the 20-year-old DU student has held her ground. “It is ABVP or any other organisation, no one has the right to take law and order in their hands,” Gurmehar Kaur said about the violence at the Ramjas College.Trolls have questioned her loyalty to the country. They have called her names saying that she has shamed her martyr father, who died fighting against Pakistan during 1999-Kargil war. Gurmehar has responded to such people saying, “I will not be scared or be cowed down. My father took a bullet for the country and I am also ready to take bullet for the country.”“I love my country and I also love my fellow students and I support their right to freedom of speech,” Gurmehar said. But, her trolls have not shied away. BJP MP from Mysore Pratap Simha has tweeted two photographs comparing Gurmehar Kaur, Kargil martyr’s daughter with underworld don and most-wanted criminal Dawood Ibrahim, whose father was in police.
