
A series of marches have been organised on the Delhi University campus in protest against violence at the Ramjas College last week. 
A march has been called under a ‘Fight Back DU’ campaign from Khalsa College to Arts Faculty against “ gundagardi ”. 
Members of the Delhi University Students’ Union (DUSU), led by the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP), took out a ‘tiranga march’ from the Ramjas College to the Vivekanand Statue at Arts Faculty on Monday.
