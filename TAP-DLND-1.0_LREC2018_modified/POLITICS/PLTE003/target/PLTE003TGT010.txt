There were reports that he would meet Sena president Uddhav Thackeray, who has sought an explanation from him over the incident. While talking to news agency PTI, Harshal Pradhan, media adviser to Sena president Uddhav Thackeray had said,“The party has sought an explanation from Gaikwad over the incident. The Sena does not condone violence of any kind.” But earlier in Sunday Times of India report claimed that the Shiv Sena chief cancelled the meeting to avoid media and further controversy. “Uddhav had sent a message to Gaikwad, asking him to avoid coming to Matoshree till the matter cools down,” a source told the daily. 
On March 23, Air India and major domestic airlines had barred the 57-year-old MP from flying as he refused to apologise for the incident that triggered nationwide outrage. Left with no option, Gaikwad then boarded the August Kranti Express at 4.50 pm on Friday. Delhi Police has registered two FIRs, charging him with culpable homicide not amounting to murder. Gaikwad has remained defiant and unrepentant, and instead blamed the airline employee, R Sukumar, for the incident. “Kaahe ka pashchaataap (what is there to repent)? I will not apologise … he (Sukumar) should come and apologise. Then we will see. A 60-year-old man should know how to behave,” he had said earlier. 
Sukumar, whose effigy was burnt by Sena workers in Tuljapur yesterday, has alleged that the MP had used foul language, snatched his glasses, pinned him to the ground and hit him with a slipper when he tried to persuade him to get off the plane.
