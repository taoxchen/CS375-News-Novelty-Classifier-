Shiv Sena MP Ravindra Gaikwad received another jolt today when an Air India ticket from Mumbai to Delhi he booked was cancelled by the airline. This is the third time Gaikwad's air ticket has been cancelled following the Shiv Sena MP assaulting an Air India staffer last week. 
Gaikwad was banned by Federation of Indian Airlines (FIA) after he hit the Air India staffer on March 23 "25 times". The Osmanabad MP had booked a ticket with Air India from Delhi to Pune on March 24. 
However, Air India cancelled the ticket. Subsequently, the Shiv Sena MP booked another ticket, this time with IndiGo, which was also cancelled as domestic airlines had put him on the no fly list. 
As a result of the cancellation of tickets, Gaikwad was forced to travel by train to Maharashtra. 
The matter was raised in both Houses of Parliament on March 27. Thereafter, Speaker Sumitra Mahajan convened a meeting of the Shiv Sena MPs and Union Civil Aviation Minister A Gajapathi Raju. She suggested an amicable solution to the issue. 
The matter remains unresolved with neither the FIA seeking any apology nor Gaikwad offering any unconditional acceptance of guilt.
