
Gujarat Chief Minister Vijay Rupani on Wednesday announced a judicial inquiry into allegations of gangrape and sex racket in Kutch in which four BJP workers are accused of having been involved. Rupani’s announcement in the Gujarat Assembly came after a meeting with Leader of Opposition Shankarsinh Vaghela and some other leaders of the Opposition after Congress, the principal Opposition, refused to let the House function until the state government ordered a judicial inquiry. Rupani also told the House that all suspects in the case had been arrested and booked. In an FIR filed at Naliya in Kutch earlier this month, a 24-year-old woman claimed that she had been raped by at least nine persons on different occasions a year ago. She claimed some suspects had recorded the act to “blackmail” her. The woman claimed some BJP workers had exploited several women similarly to run a sex racket. A Special Investigation Team of police has arrested eight people —four local BJP leaders among them. The BJP leaders have been suspended from the party. —
