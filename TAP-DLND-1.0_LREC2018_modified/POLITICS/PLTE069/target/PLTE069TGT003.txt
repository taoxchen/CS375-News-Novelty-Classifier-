
As Punjab is gearing up for elections, debutant Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) will be more muscular with the support of Non-Resident Indians (NRIs). According to a report on NDTV, many NRIs from Punjab have come back to support AAP in the upcoming elections. Reportedly around 2,500 NRIs have come home to support the new party and are also campaigning for AAP by asking people to end Akali rule and elect Kejriwal government. 
Few NRIs told NDTV that some of the people have sold their lands abroad just to fund their three-month stay in India and those who were unable to make it are lending their efforts from phone. As per reports, AAP enjoys the support of more than 35,000 NRIs and Kejriwal’s party AAP gets 20 per cent of its funding from NRIs. The NRIs want to see the same democracy in Punjab as they see abroad. As per a survey, one person out of 5 in Punjab dwells on the foreign soil. 
The massive NRI support is boosting AAP’s ‘chalo Punjab’ campaign launched in December last year, which appealed Punjabis living across the country and abroad to free their “native land from the clutches of a corrupt regime. 

Punjab polls would be conducted in a single phase. The elections across the 117 seats would be held on February 4, 2017.
