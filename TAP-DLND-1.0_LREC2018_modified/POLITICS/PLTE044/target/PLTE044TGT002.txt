NASHIK: The supporters of former Nationalist Congress Party leader Chhagan Bhujbal have decided to take out a protest rally in the city on October 3 to support their leader.During the meeting held at Jayshankar Festival Lawns on Aurangabad Road on Sunday, Bhujbal supporters decided to hold a rally in the city with people from other backward class (OBC).A Bhujbal supporter said, "The government is deliberately lodging false cases against our leader Chhagan Bhujbal. This is nothing but a conspiracy by the govrnment to finish his political career. So, we have decided to take out a huge rally in his support," he said, adding that that rally has nothing to do with any community or caste.The enforcement directorate (ED) arrested Bhujbal on March 14 in a money laundering case registered following a Bombay high court directive of December 2014 to appoint a special inquiry team to probe allegations of his misconduct. Bhujbal and his nephew Sameer, a former NCP MP from Nashik, are jailed for the same.The ED is investigating the two cases against Bhujbals under the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA) related to the Sadan scam and a fraud case registered in Kharghar, Navi Mumbai. Chhagan is accused in the Sadan scam, his son Pankaj and nephew Sameer are accused in both cases.
