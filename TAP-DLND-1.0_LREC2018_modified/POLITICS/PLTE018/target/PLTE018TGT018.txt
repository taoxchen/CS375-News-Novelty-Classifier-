
A bomb exploded near the Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) office in Kozhikode on Thursday. Four BJP members were injured in this bomb blast that took place in Nadapuram. 
The police has cordoned off the area after security was tightened in the Nadapuram locality. However, the accused are still at large. 
"The Home Ministry should take steps to curb these attacks," says Rakesh Sinha, RSS ideologue. 
The blast comes after a RSS leader from Madhya Pradesh had offered a Rs 1 crore bounty on Kerala Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan's head.
