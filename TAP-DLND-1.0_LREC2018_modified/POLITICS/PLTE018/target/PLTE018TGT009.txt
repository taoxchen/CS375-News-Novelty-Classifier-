
A day after a bomb was hurled at a Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) premises in Kallachy near Nadapuram, a Communist Party of India (CPM) office at Vishnumangalam was put on fire by a few unidentified people.

In the attack at the RSS office, three RSS workers had sustained injuries. They were rushed to the Government Medical College in Kozhikode.
The attacks come after RSS leader Kundan Chandrawat offered Rs 1 crore bounty to the one who would bring Kerala Chief Minister P. Vijayan's head to him.
"If anyone beheads the Kerala CM, who is the murderer of RSS activists, and brings his head I will bequeath my property worth more than Rs 1 crore to him," he had said.
