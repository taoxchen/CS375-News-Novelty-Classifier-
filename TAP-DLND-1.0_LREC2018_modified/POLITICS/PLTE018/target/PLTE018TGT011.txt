 An injured RSS worker being taken to hospital in Kallachy near Nadapuram on Thursday.
A bomb was hurled at a Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) office in Kallachy near Nadapuram on Thursday. 
At least three RSS workers have sustained injuries in the attack. They were rushed to the Government Medical College in Kozhikode. 
Police is investigating the matter.
