The Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP) on Saturday released its third list of 100 candidates, taking the total to 300 seats for the upcoming assembly elections in Uttar Pradesh. A total of 300 seats have been declared till now. The party on Thursday released its first list of candidates and declared names of its official candidates for 100 of the 403 Assembly seats in Uttar Pradesh and gave over one third to Muslims, who account for nearly 20 per cent of the state’s electorate. A total of 36 tickets have been given to Muslims. The first list includes Muzaffarnagar riots accused and sitting MLA of Charthaval constituency Noor Saleem Rana. The list includes candidates for Mainpuri, Etawah, Lucknow, Amroha, Badaun, Bareilly, Pilibhit, Shahjahanpur, Lakhimpur, Sitapur, Hardoi, Farukkhabad, Kannauj, Auraiyya, Unnao, Kanpur and Kanpur Dehat.
— The BSP, in the second list that was released on Friday has given 27 tickets to people belonging from the Scheduled Castes. The party, so far has announced tickets to 58 Muslim candidates of total 200 candidates. As per ANI report, the BSP supremo on Tuesday announced that her party would field 97 Muslims along with 113 candidates from the upper castes, 106 candidates from backward castes and 87 Dalits in the upcoming elections. “List of candidates for other seats will be released soon,” BSP said in a release. The party has already shortlisted names of candidates for all the 403 seats, BSP supremo Mayawati said at a press conference
— On January 4, the Election Commission announced that there would be seven-phase polling for 403 assembly constituencies in Uttar Pradesh. The polling in crucial state Uttar Pradesh will commence on February 11 and conclude on March 8. The counting of votes will take place on March 11. Earlier today, BSP Chief Mayawati met party coordinators, candidates, MPs, MLAs and office bearers in Lucknow.
