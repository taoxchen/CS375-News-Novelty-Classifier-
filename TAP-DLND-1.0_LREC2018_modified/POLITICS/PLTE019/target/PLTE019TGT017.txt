The Bahujan Samaj Party has released its fourth list of 101 candidates for the Uttar Pradesh assembly polls. 
With the latest announcement of candidates the party has announced all nominees for all but two reserved seats. 
BSP's focus remains on Dalits, Muslims BSP'S LIST OF CANDIDATES: THINGS TO KNOW In the 403-seat Uttar Pradesh assembly, the BSP has announced its candidates for 401 constituencies. In the previous three lists, the BSP had declared nominees for 300 seats. The two assembly segments, where candidates are still to be cleared by BSP president Mayawati, are the reserved seats in Sonbhadra district of Uttar Pradesh. Uttar Pradesh goes to polls in seven phases between February 11 and March 8. Before announcing the names of candidates for assembly polls, Mayawati held several rounds of meetings with party office-bearers, leaders, MLAs, MPs and party ticket nominees. Mayawati asked the party cadre to hit the ground in their respective constituencies and ensure BSP's victory. Mayawati also told the party workers to expose the ruling Samajwadi Party's weakness, particularly on the law and order front. Mayawati also asked her partymen to exploit the post-demonetisation sentiment of the people by cashing in on their anguish due to cash crunch that followed note ban.
