Cricketer-turned-politician Navjot Singh Sidhu is likely to join the grand old Congress party in the presence of party vice-president Rahul Gandhi on January 9. 
"Rahulji was expected to return today. Both me and Navjot Singh will take up the roles decided by Rahulji and Captain Amarinder Singh," said Sidhu's wife Navjot Kaur. 
The Congress on Thursday said that Sidhu will contest in the upcoming elections as a party nominee. 
"Navjot Singh Sidhu will contest from the constituency of his wife, Navjot Kaur Sidhu," former Punjab Chief Minister and Congress state unit chief Captain Amarinder Singh told reporters here. 
"Sidhu will contest from Amritsar (East) seat as a Congress nominee," added Singh. 
The final list of Congress candidates for the February 4 Assembly polls in Punjab will be out by January 9 said Singh. 
The cricketer-turned-politician had resigned from the Bharatiya Janata Party on September 14, 2016.
