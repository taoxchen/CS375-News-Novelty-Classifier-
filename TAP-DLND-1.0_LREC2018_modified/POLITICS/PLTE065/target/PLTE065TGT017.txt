Congress Punjab President Captain Amarinder Singh quashed all reports of promising the Deputy CM post to cricketer turned politician Navjot Singh Sidhu. Speculations are being made that Sidhu might be inducted into the Congress soon. The former soldier further said that Mr. Sidhu will contest election from Amritsar-east. Amarinder Singh was asked on Navjot Singh Sidhu after Sidhu’s wife, who recently joined Congress claimed that her husband was promised the Deputy CM post if Congress comes to power in Punjab. 
On Sidhu’s induction, Singh stated that the Congress party will gain strength with his presence. In an exclusive interview to ABP news, the former soldier said that Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) which is a fresh entry into the Punjab elections and have kept hopes really high will come on the ground level, post elections. 
On the post of Chief Minister, Deputy Chief Minister and other positions, Congress Punjab President said that it is up to the Congress President and vice president to take calls on various positions. 
Congress president of Punjab Captain Amarinder Singh will contest from Patiala and is pitted against General JJ Singh in the upcoming polls. JJ Singh, a debutant is fielded by the ruling party Shiroma Akali Dal (SAD) in state. 
Punjab Polls: 
The Punjab polls would be conducted in a single phase. The Election Commission announced that Punjab elections across the 117 seats would be held on February 4, 2017. 
The electoral battle in Punjab is a trilateral contest. The incumbent ruling regime of Shiromani Akali Dal (SAD) and Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) is facing a stiff competition from Congress and a fresh entry into the battle- Aam Aadmi Party (AAP).
