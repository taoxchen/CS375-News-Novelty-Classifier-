Former Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) leader Navjot Singh Sidhu is likely to join the Congress Party today. 
Reports said that Sidhu delayed his entry into the grand old party because he wanted to join the Congress in presence of party vice-president Rahul Gandhi. Since Rahul is out of country, Sidhu decided the date accordingly. 
The Central Election Committee of Congress for Punjab is set to meet on January 10. After that, the remaining 40 candidates for the assembly polls will be declared. 
Both Sidhu and his wife had quit the BJP last year over differences with the top leadership.
