
The Election Commission is likely to give tomorrow an interim order on the dispute in the ruling Samajwadi Party in Uttar Pradesh, with the option of freezing the symbol “cycle” most likely to be exercised. The three-member EC, headed by Nasim Zaidi, heard both the sides on Friday and reserved its order. The order is most likely to be delivered tomorrow, a day ahead of the start of filing of nominations for the first phase of the election in UP, which will see polling in seven phases. Sources said with hardly any time left for the EC to go into the authenticity of the documents filed by both the sides, it may come out with an interim order freezing the symbol. The two sides may be allowed to pick up symbols from among the free symbols available in the state.
