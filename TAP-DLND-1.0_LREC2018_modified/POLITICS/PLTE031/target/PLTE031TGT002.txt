A marathon Election Commission hearing of the warring factions of the Samajwadi Party remained inconclusive on Friday as both sides kept arguing that there was no split in the party.
At the same time, they sought the party symbol “cycle”. The poll panel has reserved its order.
The Akhilesh faction has claimed that 90 percent of the party stands with him on the basis of affidavits signed by over 5,000-odd party delegates. They also submitted affidavits of 212 MLAs, 56 MLCs and 15 of the 24 MPs. While lawyers from the Mulayam Singh argued that all the affidavits submitted by Akhilesh faction are forged. The Akhilesh faction was represented by Kapil Sibal and Rajiv Dhawan while the Mulayam team was represented by former solicitor general Mohan Parasarn.
Mulayam, along with his brother Shivpal Yadav, was present during the hearing. From Akhilesh’s side, Ramgopal Yadav, along with his son Akshay Yadav and MPs Kiranmoy Nanda and Naresh Agarwal were present. “Nobody wanted the symbol frozen or a split in the party. It is not for EC to decide who will be party president. In case of a dispute the civil court can adjudicate on the issue,” Dhawan said. The Mulayam faction argued that not merely the documents submitted by Akhilesh faction are forged, the convention where the CM took over was illegal too. “Mulayam remains the president. No side has pleaded for a split in the party,” advocate Gauri Neorampal of Mohan Parasaran’s team said.
