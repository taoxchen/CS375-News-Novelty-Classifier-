The DMK will stage a demonstration outside Chennai district collectorate on Wednesday to protest against a ban imposed by the Centre on the sale of cattle for slaughter . 

In a statement, the DMK said party working president M K Stalin would lead the protest, which will start at 9am. 

"The decision by the Centre has affected the village economy. Several states have expressed their opposition to the ban, but the Tamil Nadu government is silent on it, and it is condemnable," the party said in a statement. 

The DMK said the Centre had been snatching away the rights of states one after another. "The AIADMK government is maintaining a stoic silence and is refusing to raise its voice against the Centre's actions," said the party. 

"Cattle are being maintained by farmers as their own children or as one of the family members. Cattle have been the lifeline for farmers especially during situations like drought. It is only when the cattle are not in a position to be used as a milch animal or for agricultural activities that they are sold to butchers. This has been happening for decades," the statement said. 

After the Centre's decision only a few cows and oxen were brought to the famous Anthiyur cattle market in Erode district on Sunday, the party said.
