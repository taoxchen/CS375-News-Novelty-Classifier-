
The Bengaluru Police today said that it will not allow beef festivals in the city. The decision comes in wake of the on going controversy over Centre's decision to ban the sale and purchase of cattle for slaughter. 
According to the police, they took the decision to prevent any unprecedented chaos in the city. 
A public rally was organised in Kochi, Kerala, where a cattle was slaughtered by Youth Congress workers. However, the party high command condemned the incident and the perpetrators were suspended from the party. Similarly, in Tamil Nadu, a beef fest was organised in IIT Madras by the students. 
Union Minister, Ravi Shankar Prasad, said, "Why are the Congress workers playing with the sentiments of the people. Everyone has the right to speech in India. But I don't know why this is happening. It is condemnable."
