Various organizations across the city are gearing up to celebrate International Yoga Day on June 21 by organizing special camps and events. Taking the lead with a community event at Yashwant Stadium is Nagpur Municipal Corporation (NMC). "12 yoga organizations of the city like Janardan Swami Yogabhiyasi Mandal, Esha Foundation, Art of Living, Bramhakumaris will be joining us and we have provided them with slots of 5 to 25 minutes depending upon their requirements," mayor Nanda Jhichkar told TOI. The civic body has made arrangements to accommodate up to 20,000 persons. "A green carpet will be laid out, but considering the weather, the modules designed are of standing postures and no aasan which requires lying down has been included," said Jhichkar and added that NMC will also use this event to spread awareness about segregation of dry and wet garbage and tree plantation. The event will be held from 6am to 8am. A rally was taken out on June 18 by Shri Janardan Swami Yogabhiyasi Mandal in Sitabuldi and west Nagpur to create awareness about the event. The organization will get a 25 minute slot at Yashwant Stadium. "We have prepared a module comprising of aasan, pranayama, dhyan prakriya and chanting of shlokas which help create an inner calm," Ram Khandwe of Yogabhiyasi Mandal said. A special session on aasana siddhi comprising selected postures from Hatha Yoga will be held at Chitnavis Centre in collaboration with Kedar Sanaatana Yoga Shrine. "Hatha Yoga helps in gaining perfect health, cure diseases and reduces dependency on medicines," said yogacharya Kedar R Joshi while addressing the media. "Starting from this day till June 21, 2018 we will also be reaching out to schools, especially in the rural areas where we are targeting children in the age group of 8 to 18 yrs," Joshi said. For the session on June 21 which will be held from 6.30am to 8am, the yogacharya has prepared a module of 15 postures. "A group of doctors and our volunteers will be present to help the participants," he said. More than 10,000 men and women are expected to practice yoga at the Reshimbagh ground between 6am to 8am at a camp organized by Patanjali Yog Samiti. "Government of India has given a protocol under which yoga asana, pranayama and simple exercises will be performed," informed president Pradip Tatekar. "A special song 'tan man jeevan chalo sawarain, yog marg apnaye' has been composed by the Ayush ministry for this occasion. Well known singer Chaya Wankhede will present this song at our camp," he added. Yoga teacher Ghanshyam Varma will give a talk and provide guidance on the importance of yoga at an event to be organized by South Central Zone Cultural Centre (SCZCC) at its premises at 8am. A yoga camp will be organized by Sanjeevani Pranayam Khonde Udyan and Mahal Bahu-uddeshiya Sanskrutik at Khonde Udyan, Mahal, at 6am under the guidance of Yogacharya Keshavrao Kshirsagar and Haribhau Kshirsagar.
