 State government has decided to celebrate third international yoga day 2017 on June 21 with state level function at Central stadium, Thiruvananthapuram. The programme is being organised as per the direction of Government of India. 

The celebration of international day of Yoga will involve training programme on common yoga protocol as well as discourses, lectures and talks by eminent yoga experts. The main programme would be the mass yoga demonstration from 7 am to 8 am in accordance with common yoga protocol at the state, district, block and panchayat levels. Besides yoga, seminars, workshops and musical and cultural programmes based on yoga will be held. Students from schools, colleges, universities and various youth organisations such as NYKS, NSS , residents welfare association and civil society organisations will participate in the programmes. 

The government has directed all district collectors, heads of departments, heads of public undertaking and autonomous bodies to hold a mass yoga demonstration at the state, district, block and panchayat level on June 21. All government offices and educational institutions have also been directed to organise symposiums, seminars and training sessions on yoga to generate awareness about the benefits and therapeutic values of yoga.
