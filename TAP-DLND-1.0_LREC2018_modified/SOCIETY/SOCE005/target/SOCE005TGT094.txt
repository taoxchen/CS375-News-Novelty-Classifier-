
Public Celebration of 3rd International Day of Yoga at a picturesque location in front of Le Yacht Club Zaitunay Bay, Beirut on Sunday 18 June 2017. 
The celebration was inaugurated by Ambassador of India to Lebanon HE Sanjiv Arora and Chief Guest Madame Abir Chebaro, Adviser to Prime Minister of Lebanon HE Saad Hariri. Various Yoga Institutions and yoga experts who collaborated enthusiastically conducted Yoga sessions on this holistic occasion. Few cultural Indian folk dance performances also presented by the Lebanese students of the dance class conducted at the Embassy. Large number of gathering took part on this day.
