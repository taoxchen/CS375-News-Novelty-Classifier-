Countering the International Yoga Day on June 21, the Varanasi unit of Congress will observe 'Kisan Diwas' and stage dharna near the statue of former Prime Minister Lal Bahadur Shastri at his ancestral place in Ramnagar. 

"In counter of the yoga programme with 'empty stomach' by farmers and youths, the Congress will observe the day as 'Kisan Diwas' near the statue of Lal Bahadur Shatstri, who had given the slogan of "Jay Jawan -Jay Kisan", said the district Congress president while speaking at the birthday celebration of AICC vice president Rahul Gandhi on Monday. 

The dharna will be attended by farmers from across the district in large numbers. Addressing the function the former MP and senior vice president of UPCC Rajesh Mishra said that the grassroot struggle for farmers is being fought by Rahul Gandhi only. Former MLA Ajay Rai held the central government's decision of demonatisation responsible for the plight of farmers.
