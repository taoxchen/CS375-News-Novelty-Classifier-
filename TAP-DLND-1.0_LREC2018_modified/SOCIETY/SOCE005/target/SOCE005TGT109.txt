  
SILENT fitness classes are being held on Bournemouth beach to celebrate International Yoga Day. 
Around 100 people will be taking part in sessions hosted by Shush Events on the West Undercliff Promenade on Wednesday, June 21 as part of the initiative, which is also being held to promote the town's FitLiving UK event in September. 
There will be a total of seven different classes taking place throughout the day, offering opportunities for people of all ages and abilities to get involved, including morning sessions, business, school or parent and child yoga, happy hour and evening and sunset yoga.
