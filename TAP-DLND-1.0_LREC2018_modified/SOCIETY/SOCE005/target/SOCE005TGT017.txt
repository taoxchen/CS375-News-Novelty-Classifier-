Stonehenge is a celebrated venue of festivities during the summer solstice—the longest day of the year in the northern hemispherw—and it attracts thousands of revellers, spiritualists and tourists. Druids, a pagan religious order dating back to Celtic Britain, believe Stonehenge was a centre of spiritualism more than 2,000 years ago.
