
To celebrate International Yoga Day, The Sum of Us is teaming up with GuavaPass and Yoga House to host an evening of community-led activities for Dubai’s yoga community to gather and socialise at the café located just off Sheikh Zayed Road. Starting with a gentle flow yoga class at 5:30 pm, guests will be guided through a one-hour session with Karthik, Yoga House’s lead instructor, who has taught both Hatha and Ashtanga for more than 10 years. Following the class, guests can enjoy a sunset dinner. In keeping with the community feel of the event, the café will adopt a sharing platter concept for all three courses. Sharing starters include beetroot hummus, falafel, rice paper rolls with salmon and furikake amongst others while mains feature new menu items such as the greens and grains bowl, super salad bowl and kimchi bowl. Freshly-pressed juices will be offered as well as an assortment of not-so-guilty pleasures (coconut and chia seed pudding, vegan raw bars and more) courtesy of Bull & Roo’s newest bakery concept, Rise & Dawn Bakehouse . For those fasting, dates will be provided at sunset.
