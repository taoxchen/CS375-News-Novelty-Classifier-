 Since June 21 was first established as the International Day of Yoga by the United Nations in 2014, enthusiasm for the holiday has spread across China, inspiring numerous cities to host their own yoga festivals. On Saturday, the India-China (Chengdu) International Yoga Festival kicked off its third year in Chengdu, capital of Southwest China's Sichuan Province. Masters of yoga, Sanskrit mantras and traditional Indian dances are taking part in the festival, which comes to an end Wednesday (June 21). Many of the activities and events are being live-streamed so more people across the nation can view them online. Other cities are also holding yoga festivals this week. Wuxi in East China's Jiangsu Province, will hold its yoga celebrations from Wednesday through Sunday, as will Kunming in Southwest China's Yunnan Province. Yoga's popularity in China has caused it to become increasingly mainstream in recent years. On June 8, the Yunan Minzu University officially established its India-China Yoga College. The college will enroll undergraduate and graduate students starting later this year, according to a report from the Beijing Youth Daily. Students will be trained both in China and India, and study medicine, philosophy and Hindi.
