
The national capital is all set to celebrate its third International Yoga Day. Connaught Place (CP) which is the hub of all activity - from high end stores to shop to numerous roadside trinkets and the much enjoyed night life, is the chosen venue. 
However, today CP wears a different look - no traffic, big screens, posters of PM Modi and green mats rolled out where numerous people will practice Yoga from 7 am. 
"This yoga day is unique because the stakeholders of societies will participate in this yoga day. Anyone can come and participate in this," said Naresh Kumar, NDMC Chairman. 
While over 50,000 people are geared up to attend the Yoga day celebrations with the PM Modi and CM Yogi in Lucknow, thousands are expected to gather at CP. 
"It is important to inculcate this value in people so that people live a healthy life," said Sehaj, a young software engineer. His colleague Sudeep feels that celebrating yoga will boost India's tourism. 
"I think it is good because it will help tourism in India. Many people from abroad come here," he said. 
However, the yoga day prep comes with its own set of problems. The area is likely to witness traffic snarls as vehicles have been restricted to only the putter circle. 
The inner and middle circles are only for pedestrians. The Rajiv Chowk metro station has restricted entry and exit tomorrow morning until 8:30am. 
"It is very hectic when it comes to traffic and metro. They should provide buses for people coming to CP," said Kriti a young shopper. 
CP is on high alert and apart from this all shops have been asked to close at 7 pm on Tuesday evening. Many traders wore a look of gloom as their sales is likely to be affected. 
CP also houses many pubs and restaurants that have been asked to remain shut today evening. 
On the third Yoga day the Centre has also pushed out a mobile app - Celebrating Yoga which helps people locate yoga events happening close by. 
With a few complaining about the inconvenience, many others seem to feel Yoga is the way to go ahead.
