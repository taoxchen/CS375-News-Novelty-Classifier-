 State-run schools across Odisha will skip 3rd International Yoga day to be observed on June 21. Senior officers in school and mass education department said they did not receive any directive from the Centre for any observation. "We have not received any directive from the Centre regarding celebration of Yoga day in school nor has the state government issued any instruction for the same," said director of elementary education, Chudamani Seth. 

The international yoga day, a flagship annual event of the Narendra Modi government started in 2015 to promote yoga, was a lacklustre programme in the state-run schools. 

"As per my knowledge no such notice for celebration for international Yoga day has been issued to schools from the state government," said Biswanath Pradhan , additional secretary school and mass education department. 

But all schools affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) in the state have been asked to observe the day in the school premises and ensure maximum participation. Similarly hundreds of Saraswati Sishu Mandirs under Sikhya Vikash Samiti a group of Odia medium private schools would observe yoga day in their respective schools. 

"We have made elaborate arrangement for the yoga day celebration in our school and would ensure maximum participation of students. A quiz competition will also be held on the occasion. Besides, our NCC cadets will participate in the mass yoga programme to be conducted by the NCC directorate at Government Boys' High School, Unit-6," said Debendra Pati , a teacher of DAV Public School, Chandrasekharpur. 

However, a mass yoga session will be organised by the ministry of Ayush at Janata Maidan while several yoga institutes and voluntary organisations have also been planning to observe the day in a big way in the city. As many as 15,000 participants are expected to practice yoga asanas at the venue. Prayas Yoga, a leading yoga centre in the city, has decided to observe the day at Buddha Mandir followed by a seminar on benefits of yoga on health. The state AYUSH department has also planned to conduct a meeting at Jaydev Bhawan to observe the yoga day on Wednesday.
