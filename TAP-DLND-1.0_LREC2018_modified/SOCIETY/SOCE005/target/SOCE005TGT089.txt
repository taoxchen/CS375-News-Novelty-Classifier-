 Prime Minister Narendra Modi on Tuesday arrived on a two-day visit to Lucknow. During his visit, PM Modi will take part in various programmes including the 3rd International Yoga Day celebrations tomorrow. PM Modi will visit CSIR- CDRI and will also inaugurate a building of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Technical University today. PM will also distribute sanction letters to beneficiaries of PM Awas Yojana. On the occasion of 3rd International Yoga Day, PM Modi will join the Yoga Day event at the Ramabai Ambedkar Maidan in Lucknow.
