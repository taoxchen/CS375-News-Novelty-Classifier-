The International Day of Yoga was officially formed three years ago after India highlighted the importance of the practice to the United Nations. It brought together 176 countries to sign a resolution, marking the June 21 as the official day of yoga. Atlantis, The Palm will celebrate The International Day of Yoga with a free underwater yoga class in front of the resort’s Ambassador Lagoon. Set to be the biggest underwater yoga class in Dubai, the morning will aim to deliver a completely unique experience for beginners, intermediate and advanced yoga practitioners, set against the tranquility of the Ambassador Lagoon. Surrounded by 65,000 sea creatures, you will have the opportunity to rejuvenate and restore yourself in a unique aquatic surrounding practicing Hatha Yoga. The class includes complimentary access to The Lost Chambers Aquarium until 10:00am, a yoga mat, towels and a bottle of water are offered at the venue. Booking is essential.
