 Nearly 35 activists including students who gathered to support the bandh called by farmers were detained by Pune police on Monday. 
Over 50 people gathered at Balgandharva Chowk. Farmers, their students kids, members of Sambhaji Brigade, Swabhimani Shetakari Sanghatana raised slogans demanding loan waiver to all farmers across the state, free electricity for farmers, pension after 60 years and so on. They also protested state government’s decision to offer partial loan waiver and other policies. 
They started to stage rasta roko when police detained 35 of them. Suyash Joshi, police sub inspector, Deccan Police Station, said, “They had taken permission for a strike. 
However, participants started to do rasta roko at Balgandharva chowk for which they had no permission. Hence when they came on road we had taken them in custody. We have realised them in the evening.” 
Yogesh Pande, Swabhimani Shetakari Sanghatana, said, “We were peacefully doing strike. But police illegally detained us without any reason. It is our fundamental right to protest.” 
Meanwhile, Pune Agricultural Produce Market Committee again saw dip in arrival of vegetables and fruits. On Monday, APMC received hardly 150-160 vegetables trucks against 1200-1500 it receives daily. On Sunday, market saw almost 80% arrival of vegetables and fruits. Rates were 10-20% higher than the usual ones. 
“We were expecting 100% arrival but due to bandh we got only 30 percent of produce. We are hoping that market will resume to regular produce arrival soon. According to our observation, farmers are interested to send their produce to market. But people in strike are opposing them to do so,” said Dilip Khaire, chairman, APMC, Pune.
