The Tamil Nadu petroleum dealers have decided not to accept credit or debit cards as banks will now levy 1 per cent merchant discount rate. The banks are also not settling the transactions on time, which has forced the petroleum dealers association to take the decision. 
The outfit holds 4,800 petrol bunks outlets in its association. 
The association said that they have received sudden intimation from the bankers regarding the existing agreements and on their recent decision to levy merchant discount rate and taxes on all transactions. 
The petroleum dealers' association has stated that they do not have any scope to absorb the charges. 
Furthermore, the banks are delaying the settlements, which has complicated the situation. 
"The reconciliation of swipes to amount being credited to our accounts is causing a lot of trouble and the dealer community is incurring losses to a large extent," said KP Murali, president of the association. 
Hence, the petrol bunks have decided not to accept credit/debit card from January 9, 2017.
