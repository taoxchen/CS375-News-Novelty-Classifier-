 Hours after the lynching of activist Zafar Hussein for stopping the municipal commissioner and his employees from clicking the pictures of women defecating in open, the eyewitness account of the incident bring to reality more gruesome part of the killing. Zafar’s wife Rashida Bi and daughter Sabra, while speaking to the Hindustan Times , recalled how how municipal commissioner remained mute spectator to the lynching of his father. Sabra recalls while sobbing, It was about 6:30 am and we had gone for latrine [to relieve ourselves] there when the car of municipal officials came. They started clicking our photos, abusing us and kicking our water mugs. Abba came out and told them to stop after which they started beating him. A similar account was recalled by the neighbours who insisted Zafar had requested municipal corporation to release funds for the construction of toilets in the houses but nobody paid heed to his requests. Shahida, another neighbour too recounts Kamal, a sweeper in the municipal corporation, was beating Zafar mercilessly and his shirt was stained with the blood of Zafar. Although police registered a case against five employees of the municipal corporation in Pratapgarh but not arrested any person in the case. Municipal Commissioner Ashok Jain refuted the charges and said, We were only telling women coming back after defecating to stop doing it in the open. Zaffar came and attacked Kamal.
