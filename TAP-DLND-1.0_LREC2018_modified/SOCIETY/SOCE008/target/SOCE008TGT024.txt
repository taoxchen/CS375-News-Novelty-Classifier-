After a social activist in Rajasthan’s Pratapgarh Zafar Khan was lynched by government officials for stopping them from capturing an image of a woman defecating in the open, his wife, on Saturday, told Hindustan Times that the officials had threatened her daughter. She alleged that officials had threatened the 14-year-old to break her father’s face. The deceased was a member of Communist Party of India (Marxist–Leninist) Liberation-CPI (ML). He had knocked the doors of authorities for construction of toilet in the tribal district as there were none. The younger daughter of the late Khan, Sabra, told the newspaper that as he raised his voice against the officials of the Pratapgarh municipal council, who was on a tour to shame people, he was safeguarding them.  In the early hours of the morning, we had stepped out of the house to defecate in the open due to unavailability of a toilet. Then came the municipal officials, who started clicking pictures and even abused us. My father objected to their actions for which he received a lot of beating, Sabra stated. She even accused the commissioner of the Pratapgarh municipal council of asking his workers to hammer the people with full force so that they die. Maaro, maaro, jaan se khatam kar do’ were the exact line said by the commissioner to his driver who was beating my father, alleged Sabha. After Zafar died, his family demanded compensation and arrest of municipal commissioner. The family was backed by the tribals residing in the area. Upon the complaint filed by Khan’s family, FIR has been registered against Nagar Parishad Commissioner Ashok Jain and municipal employees Kamal Harijan, Ritesh Harijan and Manish Harijan. To earn his daily means the deceased Zafar ran a grocery shop and used to fight for the rights of labourers.
