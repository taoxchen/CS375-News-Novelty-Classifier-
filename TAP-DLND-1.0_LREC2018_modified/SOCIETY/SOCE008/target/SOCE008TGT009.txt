
Municipal employees in Pratpargh town beat a middle-aged man to death when he allegedly tried to stop them from scaring women defecating in the open and taking their photographs. 
The municipal employees of Pratapgarh town municipality tried scaring away the women and took photographs of them defecating in the open. It was then that Zafar Khan intervened and tried to stop the municipal employees from taking pictures. 
In retaliation, municipality employees kicked, punched and beat Khan with a stick, which led to his death, according to the FIR lodged by Khan's elder brother Noor Mohammed.
