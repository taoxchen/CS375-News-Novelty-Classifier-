The Chenani-Nashri tunnel in J&K will be the lifeline of Kashmir Valley, Prime Minister Narendra Modi said after inaugurating the 9.2-km long tunnel on Sunday. It will not only cut the distance but will prove to be a leap towards development of the region, PM Modi said while addressing a rally at Udhampur.
 Modi said advanced technology has been used to construct this tunnel and it will also give a boost to tourism in the Valley. “I want people to inaugurate the tunnel,” Modi said, asking people to use flashlights of their mobile phones to inaugurate the tunnel. Addressing the gathering, Union Minister Nitin Gadkari said the tendering process for 14-km Zojiala tunnel to Ladakh will be started within 2 months, Gadkari said. He announced that work on two ring roads in Jammu and Srinagar will start soon. J&K Chief Minister Mehbooba Mufti said the tunnel will not only cut down distance but also bring Kashmiri people closer to India. She congratulated Modi on victory in the UP polls. Expressing hope that Modi will bring J-K close to India, Mehbooba praised the PM for his commitment and strong will. She also invited PM Modi to Kashmir Valley. “PM Modi can take decisions to address the vexed issues of J&K,” she said.
