
After inaugurating the Chenani-Nashri Tunnel on Jammu-Kashmir Highway, Prime Minister Narendra Modi today addressed a rally in Udhampur. 
The Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister was seen thanking the Prime Minister on the occasion for 'supporting Mehbooba Mufti and her government during very troubled times last year.' 
Kashmir is safe for tourists, claimed Mufti and asked the Prime Minister to visit the state. 
Jammu and Kashmir's Chenani-Nashri tunnel, touted as India's longest tunnel, is also the country's 'safest', with provisions to ensure the safety of passengers from fire incidents and collision inside the 10.89 km-long tunnel on the Jammu-Srinagar Highway, says a top official.
