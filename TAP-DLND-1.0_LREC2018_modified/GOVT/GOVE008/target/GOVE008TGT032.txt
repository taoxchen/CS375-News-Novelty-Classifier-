Mega engineered State-Of-The-Art Chenani-Nashri tunnel between between Udhampur and Ramban in India Chenani-Nashri tunnel Today, on April 2, 2017 – honorable prime minister of India Shri Narendra Modi has inaugurated the south Asia’s 1 st and world’s 8 th long bidirectional Chenani-Nashri Tunnel. It is also known as Patnitop Tunnel. It is the proud moment for the nation, especially for Jammu & Kashmir people who through this tunnel going to bypass minimum 2 hours long Patnitop, Kud and Batote landslide-prone route. It is a no human interference tunnel operated with the highly unique software that controls all the intelligent systems such as integrated traffic control system, evacuation broadcast system, active fire fighting, continuous carbon monoxide level monitoring, 90 second rescue time in case of mishap etc. In the inauguration ceremony PM Modi says that the tunnel will boost tourism in the state, which had come to a virtual standstill due to the recent increase in extremism in the Kashmir valley. About construction The two way structure on NH 44 is built at the altitude of 1,200 meter in Himalayas. With cost of over 3,720 crores the National Highway Authority of India (NHAI) along with Beigh Construction Company (BCC) took around 5 years to complete this mega project. The project was started on May 23, 2011. About the tunnel The Jammu Kashmir National highway runs over 15,000 vehicles (including convoys of security forces) per day that gets mostly disturbed due to the rain and snowy weather of this area. The main tube is 13 meters in diameter. As an ultimate solution to such traffic hurdles, the all weather Chenani-Nashri tunnel travels up to 10.98 km below the mountains, a perfect example of the mega engineering. World’s longest and deepest Gotthard tunnel in Switzerland runs only 2.3 km below the surface of the mountain, but interestingly this tunnel travels entirely through the hills. Saving fuel worth Rs. 27 lakhs per day, it will cut down 41 kilometers long travelling distance between Jammu and Srinagar to just 10.98 kilometers. For security reasons and the emergency reasons, the engineers constructed a parallel escape tube to the main. How it is a perfect mega engineering structure? The tunnel is digged through the world's highest mountain Himalayas, and built with the latest Austrian tunnel technology. The State-Of-The-Art-smart tunnel is decorated with several smart features that make this mega structure really amazing. Most Intelligent features of the tunnel’s operating system Some ultra-modern features of the most advanced structure making it unprecedented are: 1) Fully transverse ventilation system For a tunnel, it is must to maintain the smoke and carbon dioxide level up to a permissible level. I t becomes a big challenge in real for the architects to maintain such a huge daily traffic in such places. To achieve this, t he bidirectional highway tunnel has been designed to be equipped with a fully transverse and effective ventilation system. It has an inlet at every 8 meter distance to carry fresh air into the main tunnel. At every 100 meters opening, there is an exhaust outlet to expel the unwanted gases. For smooth cross ventilation system ABB India deployed a low harmonics variable speed drive (VSD) system. The company installed motors and VSDs at both ends of the tunnel for regular supply of fresh air and exhausting the smog. 2) Active Firefighting System The company also developed and installed the active firefighting systems to control a fire emergency situation and reducing any fatal hazard. 3) Electronic Surveillance and heat detecting system The tunnel shall be monitored continuously by an outer located Integrated Tunnel Control Room (ITCR). With 124 omni-directional cameras and the linear heat detection system, the duty staff will regularly observe the internal situations of the main tube. 4) Commuter safety features The safety features for the accidents and the other unexpected events inside the tube are all set by the Infrastructure Leasing and Financial Services (IL&FS) Company .The company claims two parallel tubes as its unprecedented safety features for this mega project. The second service lane tube is about 6 meters in diameter. Both tubes are interconnected with 29 cross passages to their full length that shall be used in evacuating situations of the commuters. These passages will also be helpful to pull out the accidental or broken down vehicles to the service lane. 5) Mobile connectivity You are allowed to use your BSNL, Idea and Airtel mobile phones inside the tunnel. 6) Integrated traffic control 8) Evacuate broadcast system About fare Obviously according to the size of your vehicle you need pay the fare for crossing this Himalayan tunnel. For one way, you need to pay Rs 55 for a car, Rs 90 for the small buses and Rs 190 for the large buses and the trucks. For moving to-and-fro, you need to pay Rs 85 for a car (and Rs 1,870 for monthly pass), Rs 135 for small buses, and Rs 285 for the trucks and large buses. Wrap up India’s largest and fully automated Chenani-Nashri tunnel is located exactly between Udhampur and Ramban in J&K. It is one of the best engineering marvels in the world and a giant step towards the prosperity of the Kashmir valley. It is going to be the game changer for revenue generation, promotion of trade, tourism, employment. In future it will have a positive economic impact on the state. Nitin Gadkari, current Minister for Road Transport and Highways and Shipping says, “ Chenani-Nashri tunnel is state-of-the-art project. It will increase tourism and ensure supply of essential goods ”. List of world’s 8 longest tunnels: 1) Gotthard base tunnel, Switzerland, 2016 - 57.5 km 2) Seikan Tunnel, Japan, 1988 - 53.9 km 3) Eurotunnel, France, UK, 1994 - 50.5 km 4) Yulhyeon Tunnel, South Korea, 2015 - 50.3 km 5) Lotschberg base tunnel, Switzerland, 2007 - 34.6 km 6) New Guanjiao tunnel, China, 2014 - 32.6 km 7) Guadarrama tunnel, Spain, 2007 - 28.4 km 8) Chenani-Nashri tunnel, India, 2017 - 10.98 km
