 The red beacon VIP culture was brought to an immediate halt in Rajasthan with the state government immediately banning the use of red beacons for all ministers and government officials. 

Chief minister Vasundhara Raje issued the necessary directives on Wednesday evening and said that no minister or government official should henceforth use the red beacons. After the centre announced its decision, Raje took to twitter to share her opinion. 'Curtailing the use of #RedBeacon is a welcome & forward looking step towards having more people-friendly Government,' she said on Twitter. 

The chief minister had herself never used her official vehicle with red beacon. She preferred her private vehicle. The ministers and several senior government officials used the red beacons. However, following the directives, most ministers including Arun Chaturvedi, Rajendra Rathore, Prabhu Lal Saini and other immediately removed them. 

Earlier in the day, the state government had said that the state too would follow soon. Welcoming the Centre's decision to ban red beacons, state home minister Gulabchand Kataria had said, "It is a good decision and the state too will implement it. I have already discussed it with the chief minister and the needful would be done soon.'' 

The impact of the centre's decision was prompt as several VIPs voluntarily chose to immediately give up the red beacons. Chairperson of the Rajasthan Commission for Women Suman Sharma was one of them. "As soon I heard about the order, I directed my staff to remove it. I welcome the decision,'' she said. 

The red beacons, following the Supreme Court order in December 2013, are being used by those only on constitutional posts. Among those who can used the red beacon include governor, chief minister, chief justice and justice of the Rajasthan High Court, cabinet members, assembly speaker, deputy speaker and leader of Opposition. 

Among those who can use the red beacon without the flash include chief secretary, chief electoral officer, commissioner of the state election commission and advocate general. However, the use of red beacons is frequent and often several government vehicles or those carrying government officials are seen using one. 

The vehicles carrying top police in the rank of DGP, ADGP, IG, DIG, SP, Commissioner, Additional Commissioner, DC and AC, ASP, DSP are allowed to use blue light with flasher as top light.
