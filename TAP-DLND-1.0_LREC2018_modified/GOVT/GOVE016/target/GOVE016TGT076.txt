 The red beacon VIP culture would soon be history in Rajasthan as well. Vasundhara Raje government in the state will follow the centre and ban red beacons for the VIPs in the state including the ministers. Welcoming the centre's decision to ban red beacons, state home minister Gulabchand Kataria said, "It is a good decision and the state too will implement it. I have already discussed it with the chief minister and the needful would be done soon." Raje took to twitter to share her opinion. "Curtailing the use of #RedBeacon is a welcome & forward looking step towards having more people-friendly Government,' she said on Twitter. The red beacons, following the Supreme Court order in December 2013, are being used by those only on constitutional posts. Among those who can used the red beacon include governor, chief minister, chief justice and Justices of the Rajasthan high court, cabinet members, assembly speaker, deputy speaker and leader of the opposition. CM Raje has not been using the red beacon on her vehicle. The impact of Wednesday's decision was prompt as several VIPs chose to immediately gave up the red beacons. Chairperson of the Rajasthan Commission for Women Suman Sharma was one of them. "As soon I heard about the order, I directed my staff to remove it. I welcome the decision,'' she said.
