In response to reports of two missing Sufi clerics from Pakistan, the Indian government has decided to take up the issue with the Nawaz Sharif administration. The families of the two clerics, who belonged to Delhi’s Hazrat Nizamuddin Dargah, claimed that they had gone to Pakistan to offer a ‘ chadar ‘ at Baba Farid’s shrine. Responding their distress, External Affairs Minister, Sushma Swaraj sent out a series of tweets indicating that the matter was being taken up at the highest level. She tweeted, “Indian nationals Syed Asif Ali Nizami aged 80 years and his nephew Nazim Ali Nizami had gone to Pakistan on 8 March 2017. Syed Asif Ali Nizami is Head Priest (Sajjadanashin) of Hazrat Nizamuddin Aulia dargah. Both are missing after they landed at Karachi airport. We have taken up this matter with Government of Pakistan and requested them for an update on both the Indian nationals in Pakistan.” Indian nationals Syed Asif li Nizami aged 80 years and his nephew Nazim Ali Nizami had gone to Pakistan on 8 March 2017.

He claimed that while his father went missing upon landing at the Karachi airport, his cousin was not to be found from Lahore itself. Here is all we know so far: The two Sufi clerics, Syed Asif Ali Nizami and Nazim Ali Nizami had gone to Pakistan on 8 March 2017 to offer a ‘ chadar ‘ at Baba Farid’s shrine. It is common for the Nizamuddin Dargah and the Daata Darbar to conduct an exchange of clerics. Before visiting the shrine, the two Sufi clerics had gone to meet their relatives on March 8 in Karachi. After offering ‘Chadar’ at the Data Darbar Sufi shrine in Lahore, the two clerics left again for Karachi on March 14. They were to board a flight from Lahore airport at 4:30 PM. At the Lahore airport, Nazim Nizami was stopped by the airport authorities. The reason stated by them was that certain documentation needed some clarity. Syed Asif Ali Nizami was asked to board the flight while his nephew was being interrogated. The flight left without taking Nazim Nizami on board. The Head Priest of the Hazrat Nizamuddin Aulia dargah reached Karachi at 6 PM the same day. He then immediately called his relative upon landing and asked them to come outside the airport in Karachi. However, he did not emerge out of the airport. It is believed that he was held by authorities from inside the airport. Thus far, Pakistani authorities claim to be unaware of their location. The Indian government will be taking up the issue at the highest level with the Pakistani counterparts.
