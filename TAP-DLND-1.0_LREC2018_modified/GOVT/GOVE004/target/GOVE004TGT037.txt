Two Indian Sufi clerics who had been missing in Pakistan for the past three days safely returned to Karachi on Saturday. 
Asif Nizami and his friend Nazim Nizami belong to Delhi’s Hazrat Nizamuddin Dargah. 
The duo had travelled to Pakistan to visit their relatives in Karachi and then embarked on a pilgrimage to Lahore. 
One of them went missing in Karachi and the other in Lahore, reports claimed. 
Indian authorities had raised the issue with the Pakistan foreign ministry and sought its help in tracing their missing citizens, Indian news agency ANI reported on Thursday. 
The two Indian clerics surfaced in Karachi and told the media that they had gone to meet their devotees in interior Sindh where there was no phone connectivity. 
Monitoring Desk adds: According to foreign media, a Pakistani columnist Mehr Tarar assured minister Sushma Swaraj that "Pakistanis in general" respect Sufis and the "safe return" of the two missing clerics is the responsibility of her country. Swaraj, however, chose to target Pakistan, saying Pak agencies may have picked up the two Indians. 
Meanwhile, Sajid Nizami, son of Azim Nizami, said an acquaintance of his father, Hammad who went to receive the two visitors is also missing. Hammad reportedly works at the airport.
