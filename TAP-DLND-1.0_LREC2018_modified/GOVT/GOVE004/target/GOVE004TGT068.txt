Two senior Muslim clerics Asif Ali Nizami and Nazim Ali Nizami of Delhi's Nizamuddin dargah went missing after their arrival in Karachi on March 8. 
The two had reached the Pakistani port city to meet their relatives after visiting the Data Darbar Sufi shrine in Lahore. The Ministry of External Affairs has raised the issue with its Pakistani counterpart. Indian nationals Syed Asif li Nizami aged 80 years and his nephew Nazim Ali Nizami had gone to Pakistan on 8 March 2017.
