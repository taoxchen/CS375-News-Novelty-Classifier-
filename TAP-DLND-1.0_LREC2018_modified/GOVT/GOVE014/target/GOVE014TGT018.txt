The Prime Minister, Narendra Modi , on Thursday, launched UDAN – the Regional Connectivity Scheme for civil aviation, from Shimla Airport.
Under this scheme, flights have begun today from Shimla, Nanded and Kadapa Airports.
The Prime Minister also unveiled an e-plaque to mark the laying of Foundation Stone of a Hydro Engineering College at Bilaspur, Himachal Pradesh.
What PM said – Top points from his speech
1 - The lives of the middle class are being transformed, and their aspirations are rising.
2 - The aviation sector in India is filled with opportunity.
3 - The aviation sector was once considered the domain of a select few, but that has changed now.
4 - The new civil aviation policy marks an opportunity to cater to the aspirations of the people of India.
5 - Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities are becoming growth engines, and enhanced aviation connectivity between them will be beneficial.
6 - UDAN scheme will help the tourism sector in Himachal Pradesh.
