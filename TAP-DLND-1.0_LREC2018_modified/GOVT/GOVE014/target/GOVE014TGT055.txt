
Prime Minister Narendra Modi is slated to make a day-long visit to Shimla on Thursday during which he will address a public rally at the historic Ridge, besides launching the Centre's subsidised air regional connectivity scheme UDAN.
This will be Modi's second visit to Himachal Pradesh since he became the Prime Minister in May 2014. Himachal Pradesh is scheduled to go to the polls in November. The PM will address a public meeting, which is likely to see participation of over one lakh party workers, at the historic Ridge. The PM will also inaugurated the maiden regional connectivity Shimla-Delhi flight from the airport, located 2,196 metres above sea-level and is likely to hold a roadshow from Chaura Maidan to the Ridge, the rally venue, 3 km away. The Shimla airport has been shut for scheduled flights since September 6, 2012, affecting tourists and business travellers to the state. The Prime Minister will also flag off the regional connectivity Nanded-Mumbai-Hyderabad 'UDAN' flight in Maharashtra and Katappa-Hyderabad flight in Karnataka from Jubbarhatti through video-conferencing, Union Civil Aviation Secretary R.K. Choubey said. Modi will reach Chaura Maidan by a helicopter. BJP leaders are planning that Modi will take a short stroll on the Mall from the General Post Office to the rally venue as this is vehicle-free zone. State Commissioner of Tourism and Civil Aviation Dinesh Malhotra said tickets for the inaugural flight would cost Rs 1,920. He said a regular flight, which will operate five days in a week, will depart at 6.10 a.m. from Delhi and will arrive here at 7.25 a.m. On the same day, it will depart at 7.45 a.m. from Shimla and will return to Delhi at 8.45 a.m.
