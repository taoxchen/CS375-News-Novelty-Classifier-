 An Indian fisherman was allegedly shot dead after the Sri Lankan Navy opened fired at a group of fishermen off the coast of Tamil Nadu, while the fishermen were fishing between Danushkodi and Katchatheevu. A deceased has been identified as 32-year-old Indian fishermen named Britso from Thangachimadam. His body is currently being brought to Rameshwaram. Four other fishermen have also sustained severe injuries in the incident. In an other incident happened two days ago, 15 Indian fishermen were detained by the Sri Lankan Navy and all their equipment and fishing gear was taken away. Around 10 pm, the fishermen at Rameswaram received information that there was a firing at sea creating panic among the fishing community. "One of our fishermen, Britso, (22) from Thangachimadam has died in the firing and our fishermen are returning back to shore", said fishermen leader, S Emerit from Rameswaram.
