 
An Indian fisherman named Britso was killed in the gunshot by the Sri Lankan navy. It is stated that the fishermen were fishing in the maritime boundary and didn’t even cross the borders. "Why did the Sri Lankan navy fired them," asks the public. Following the attack on the fishermen, the TN leaders condemn the Sri Lankan government. 
Indian government should warn the Sri Lankan government for the attack on Indian Fishermen – “ M K Stalin ” 
Stalin also stated that the government should pay a relief fund of Rs. 25 lakhs to the family of the young fisherman Britso, who was killed by the Sri Lankan Navy 
The firing on fishermen is condemnable, says Pon Radhakrishnan , the Union Cabinet Minister 
I strongly condemn the barbaric shooting of TN fisherman by Sri Lankan Navy, says Anbumani Ramadoss. 
Attack on TN fishermen is condemnable – TN Minister Jayakumar 
Indian government should get back the traditional fishing rights - Velmurugan
