 Sri Lanka Navy on Tuesday denied allegations of opening fire at Indian fishermen, who were fishing between Danushkodi and Katchatheevu off the Tamil Nadu coast. Sri Lanka Navy Spokesperson Lieutenant Commander Chaminda Walakuluge vehemently denied allegations of shooting and said the Navy had never been ordered to fire at Indian fishermen nor had they done so, reported Daily Mirror. A group of Indian fishermen were allegedly fired upon by the Sri Lankan Navy in which one fisherman was killed. The deceased was identified as a 22-year-old man named Pricho. Around 500 boats of Tamil Nadu fishermen went for fishing on Friday evening. The Sri Lankan navy allegedly started firing in which Pricho died of the bullet injury on his neck and another man named Saravanan.
