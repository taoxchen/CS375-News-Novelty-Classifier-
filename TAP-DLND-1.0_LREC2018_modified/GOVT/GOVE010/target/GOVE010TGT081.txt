The Apex Court of the country strengthened its stand on the highway liquor ban by modifying its earlier judgement in this case. Liquor vending within 500 metres on national and state highways are further dragged down to 220 metres in areas having a population close to 20,000. The special bench of the SC headed by Chief Justice J S Khehar pronounced this judgement to clamp down the rising number of road accidents owing to drunken driving. The judgement clarified that the licences issued to liquor vendors before the December 2016 landmark judgement, will remain valid until September 30 this year. Though other liquor vendors will shut down their shops from tomorrow. Liquor sellers in the states of Sikkim, Meghalaya and Himachal Pradesh will also be barred from selling liquors within the 220-metre periphery of the highways.
