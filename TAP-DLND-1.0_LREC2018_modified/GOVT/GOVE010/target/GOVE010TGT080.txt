
The Supreme Court on Friday reduced the liquor-free zone along state and national highways from 500 metres to 220 metres.
As per the modified order, no liquor shops can be set up to a distance of 220 metres from the highways. An earlier order of the apex court had fixed the distance at 500 metres.
The order applies only to areas with a population of 20,000 people. It also extends to hill states of Sikkim, Meghalaya and Himachal Pradesh.
The court, in its December 15, 2016, judgment, had ordered a ban on all liquor shops along national as well as state highways across the country and made it clear that licences of the existing shops will not be renewed after March 31, 2017 . It had also directed that all signage indicating the presence of liquor vends would be prohibited along national and state highways.
Around a dozen states, including Punjab, Haryana and Himachal Pradesh have sought modification in the order.
