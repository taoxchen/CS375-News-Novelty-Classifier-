Former AAP leader Prashant Bhushan today fell afoul of the Hindutva squad thanks to his tweet commenting about Uttar Pradesh chief minister Adityanath 's new 'anti-Romeo squads'. The 'anti-Romeo squads' are supposed to crack down on 'eve teasing.' And 'eve teasing' is the benign term used in India for sexual harassment.
