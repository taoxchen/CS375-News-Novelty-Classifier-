In a viral video, three police officials were caught tonsuring a boy during an anti-romeo-drive in Uttar Pradesh's Shahjahanpur. Giving details, a senior police official said the officials have been identified and suspended and strict action will be taken against them. A man in a black shirt is seen pleading for mercy from the policemen, who ask him to remain quiet and let the barber do his job. Earlier in the state, special squads comprising cops from local police stations kept a vigil at markets, malls, schools, colleges, coaching centres, parks and other crowded places to see if women were being harassed.
