
External Affairs Minister Sushma Swaraj on Monday sought a report from the Uttar Pradesh state government over an attack on Nigerian students who were assaulted by local residents at Pari Chowk, Greater Noida. 
Residents of NSG's Black Enclave on Monday were protesting following the death of a teenage boy due to suspected overdose of drugs. According to the residents, the five Nigerian students who were living in the same society had drugged 19-year-old Manish Khera. I have asked for a report from Government of Uttar Pradesh about the reported attack on African students in Noida. 
The Nigerian students were booked, detained, and released because of lack of evidence. 
The residents were protesting against African residents living in the colonies of Greater Noida
