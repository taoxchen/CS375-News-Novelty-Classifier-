Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath's government has promised a thorough investigation and quick report in a mob attack on Nigerian students in Greater Noida, near Delhi. Three of the students attacked on Monday evening are in hospital. A video apparently shot on a mobile phone showed a large group kicking and punching one of the African students and hitting him with steel dustbins at a shopping mall. The police said they have arrested seven people so far and have registered cases of attempt to murder and violence against about 300 unidentified people. They have promised more arrests. Here are the 10 latest developments on this big story: Foreign Minister Sushma Swaraj tweeted this morning that she has spoken to the UP chief minister, who she said has promised a "fair and impartial" investigation. An African student in Greater Noida had in a tweet pleaded with Ms Swaraj for swift action, saying that living in the area has become "a life threatening issue." Ms Swaraj spoke to the UP chief minister after promising the student "immediate action." "Attack on Nigerian students a serious issue. Proper probe will be done. Report will be provided to Centre at earliest," senior UP minister Siddharth Nath Singh said today. On Monday evening, over a thousand local residents were marching in a candlelight protest against the death of a Class 12 student, when some of them spotted some Nigerian students at the shopping mall and at other places. "Some hooligans beat them up," a police officer said. The protesters alleged that the student, Manish Khari, 17, was supplied drugs by Africans who live in the area. Manish had been missing for a day and was found in bushes near his home. He was taken to a hospital, where he died of a cardiac arrest. Top UP police officer Daljeet Singh said Manish's postmortem report does make the cause of death clear. "His parents have alleged that he died of an overdose of drugs supplied to him by Nigerian students," Mr Singh said. Security has been strengthened in areas where the Africans, mostly students in Greater Noida's universities, live. African students will meet the Greater Noida police and senior government officials this afternoon. The police has banned processions or protests in Gautam Buddha Nagar, of which Greater Noida is a part. There have been several attacks on Africans in and around Delhi, most recently in the capital's Vasant Kunj area, where a Congolese man was stoned to death last week. Last year, some Nigerians were beaten up in Delhi's Chhatarpur.
