According to initial reports, it’s being suggested as a premeditated murder. Around three assailants entered her Bengaluru residence and fired three shots at her as soon as she opened the door. 
“She was shot dead from a close range at her house in Rajarajeshwari Nagar around 8PM,� Bengaluru Police Commissioner T Suneel Kumar informed reporters. 
Three unidentified assailants fired three to five bullets at Lankesh, with one hitting her in the forehead and another in the chest, and fled as she collapsed on the spot.
