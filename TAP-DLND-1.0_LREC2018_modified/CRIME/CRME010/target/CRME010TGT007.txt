While the family members of slain journalist and activist Gauri Lankesh have set a deadline before the state government that it should refer the case to the CBI if the state police fail to arrest the killers in two weeks, I Am Gauri the forum for movement against Lankesh’s assassination has written to the CM asking him not to refer the case to the CBI.
The forum, formed by Gauri’s friends and likeminded people has written to CM Siddaramaiah, demanding booking those who are celebrating the murder on charges of instigating it. “It’s a dangerous trend that murders are being celebrated on social media.
