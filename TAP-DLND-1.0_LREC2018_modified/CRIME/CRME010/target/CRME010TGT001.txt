A prominent female Indian journalist seen as fearless and independent was shot dead, Indian media reported late on Tuesday.
Gunmen attacked Gauri Lankesh at her residence in Rajarajeshwari Nagar, in Bengaluru.
Lankesh, 55, known as one of the strongest opponents of right-wing forces, was reportedly shot seven times by unidentified men from close range.
Three bullets hit her on the neck and chest, police told the network, said police.
"We learnt that the victim was shot dead from close range when she was standing at her house in Rajarajeswari Nagar (in the suburbs) around 8pm," NDTV.
Lankesh ran a weekly tabloid in the local dialect Kannada, spoken by about 40 million people in and around Karnataka, in south-western India.
She was seen as outspoken and independent.
Mama Banerjee, a prominent Indian politician and the first woman to hold the office of the Chief Minister of West Bengal (since 2011), has twitted that the assassination of Lankesh was "most unfortunate" and "very alarming".
