
Various organizations have filed a police complaint against BJP Sringeri MLA D N Jeevaraj for his odd and controversial remarks regarding journalist Gauri Lankesh’s murder. 
After starting the ‘Mangaluru Chalo’ rally in the morning hours on Wednesday, the MLA had publicly given a statement said that Gauri wouldn’t have been brutally murdered had if she he had avoid these harsh words like ‘Cheddigala Maaranahoma’ (genocide of RSS workers) in her tabloid “Gauri Lankesh Patrike.�? 
Taluk Raitha Sangha president Navin Karuvane irked by this statement has filed a complaint with the police with evidence and has requested to initiate action against the MLA. Navin even suspects that Jeevaraj must be the culprit behind the murder of Gauri. Gauri has earned his grudge after the details of corruption were published in her journal during his tenure as Food and Civil Supplies minister. Meanwhile, Jeevaraj said that he had used some words in high spirits in his speech during the bike rally in haste and there was no personal bad intention behind it.
