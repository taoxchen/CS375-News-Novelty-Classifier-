Noted journalist and activist Gauri Lankesh was shot dead outside her residence in Rajarajeshwarinagar, west Bengaluru on Tuesday night. 
According to eye witnesses, the 55-year-old journalist was standing near the gate of her house when three men on a motor bike sprayed her with bullets. The incident took place at around 8.25pm. 
Gauri sustained bullet injuries on her neck and chest and is believed to have died instantly. 
City police commissioner T Suneel Kumar confirmed the murder and said that the police teams were rushing to the spot.
