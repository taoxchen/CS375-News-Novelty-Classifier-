
Representatives of progressive organisations have appealed to the public to take part in the national-level protest meet in Bengaluru on Tuesday to condemn the killing of journalist-activist Gauri Lankesh. 
Writer and advocate Banu Mushtaq, poet Rupa Hassan, CPM leader Dharmesh, DSS leader Narayana Das and Raitha Sangha leader Kottur Srinivas held a joint press conference here on Saturday. 
Ms. Mushtaq said Gauri Lankesh’s murder was a threat to democracy. “I’m surprised that the Hassan district unit of Kannada Sahitya Parishat has not responded to the brutal killing. Gauri was a journalist, an activist and a writer as well. It was the duty of the parishat to raise its voice against the crime,� she said. 
Ms. Hassan said whoever has faith in the Indian Constitution should condemn the killing. 
Mr. Dharmesh said many progressive organisations had joined hands to organise the meet at the Central College grounds at 10.30 a.m.
