Women activists of the country have demanded death penalty for the alleged pedophile who was recently arrested by the Delhi police. Following Rastogi's arrest, women activists have demanded that the accused be given death penalty. Former Delhi Commission for Women (DCW) chief, Barkha Shukla has also demanded death penalty for the accused. "Cases of rapes have been increasing in the national capital. This man getting arrested is a big achievement. Now that he has confessed his crime, he should be given strictest of the punishment. Immediate hearing should be done and he should be hanged," Shukla told ANI. In Focus Some women activists have also criticised the police for failing to curb such repeat incidents and arrest the habitual offenders.
Women activist Abha Singh told ANI, "It's a very shocking and scary situation that a serial rapist rapes and sexually assaults minor girls for more than years. What worrying is that in spite of such a strict law, such cases are happening, that too in the capital. That is a clear intelligence failure of the local police which could not find out that such a serial rapist was living in vicinity. What was surprising was that the police was caught sleeping." 
Rastogi was yesterday arrested by Delhi police after years of surveillance. He was evading the police for more than a decade. In the past 13 years, the accused had targeted nearly five hundred children. 
Rastogi reportedly committed his first crime in 2004 in Mayur Vihar. As the information of the crime became public, he and his family members were forced by the locals to leave the area. He has also been jailed in Rudrapur, Uttarakhand, for an offence in 2006 but was let-off after six months. 
Rastogi was out on bail in a Protection of Children Against Sexual Offences Act when he allegedly attacked the victims.
