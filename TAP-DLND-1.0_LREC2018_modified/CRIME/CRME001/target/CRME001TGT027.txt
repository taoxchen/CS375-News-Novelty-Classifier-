
Bollywood actor Salman Khan has been acquitted in the Arms Act case registered against him in Jodhpur in 1998. The Chief Judicial Magistrate gave a clean chit to Khan in the case, filed along with the black buck poaching case. The court rejected the prosecution’s claim that Khan possessed and used illegal arms, and gave him the “benefit of doubt” while exonerating him. The court also said that the prosecution did not produce evidence to prove its case beyond reasonable doubt.
