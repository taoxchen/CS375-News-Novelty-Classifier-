Moments after his acquittal in the 1998 Arms Act case registered against him in Jodhpur, Salman Khan expressed his gratitude to his fans. The 'Sultan' star took to Twitter and thanked his well-wishers with the message, "Thank you for all the support and good wishes".

A case was registered against Salman in October 1998 under the Arms Act for his alleged use and possession of arms with an expired license during the alleged poaching of two black bucks in Kankani village. Salman was allegedly in possession of 0.22 rifle and 0.32 revolver, which had expired licenses and cases were filed under Sections 3/25 and 3/27. 

However, Salman was acquitted of all charges in the case this morning at the Jodhpur Court.
