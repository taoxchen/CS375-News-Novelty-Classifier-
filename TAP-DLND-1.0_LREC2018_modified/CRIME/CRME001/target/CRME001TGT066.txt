Bollywood superstar Salman Khan has been acquitted of all charges against him in the Arms Act case in the alleged poaching on the two black bucks. The actor's lawyer said that the charges were dropped after the prosecution failed to provide conclusive evidence. The court gave Salman the benefit of doubt holding him not guilty in the 1998 case. A case was registered in October 1998 against the film star under the Arms Act for allegedly using and having possession of arms with expired licence while allegedly poaching two black bucks in Kankani village in Rajasthan. Salman arrived in Jodhpur yesterday with his sister Alveera and his lawyers. He was present in the court of Chief Judicial Magistrate (Jodhpur district) when the verdict was announced.
