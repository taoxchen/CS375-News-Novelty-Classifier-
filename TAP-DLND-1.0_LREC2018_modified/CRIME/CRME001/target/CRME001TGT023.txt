
Actor Salman Khan today reached Jodhpur where a local court is all set to pronounce its verdict in the Arms Act case against the superstar on Wednesday. If convicted, he may face a jail term of three to seven years. Surrounded by his fans outside the Jodhpur airport, Salman was driven straight to Hotel Taj Hari under heavy police security. CJM Dalpat Singh Rajpurohit had fixed January 18 as the date for verdict after final arguments from both the sides ended on January 9. A case was registered against him in October 1988 for alleged possession of arms without a licence during the “poaching” of black buck in Jodhpur.
