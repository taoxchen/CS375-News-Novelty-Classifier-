The Madurai bench of the Madras high court on Thursday stayed proceedings initiated by a judicial magistrate court in Melur on a case filed by a couple seeking maintenance from Tamil actor Dhanush claiming that he was their son. Dhanush filed a petition in the high court bench seeking to quash the proceedings in the Melur court. The actor stated that he was not the son of R Kathiresan (65) of Madurai and his wife Meenakshi (53) and that somebody was behind them to extract money from him. The actor appeared before the court on Tuesday as directed the court. Government doctors examined the actor to verify if he had a mole on the collar and a scar on the forearm, as claimed by the couple. The actor had earlier clarified that he had no such identification marks. On Thursday, when the Dhanush's petition came up for further hearing before Justice G Chockalingam, the couple's side demanded the DNA test of the actor and Kathiresan to prove paternity. "The actor said he is the son of film director Kasthuri Raja and Vijayalakshmi. He also produced fake transfer and birth certificates. Thus, a DNA test is necessary to prove the paternity. The test will prove the exact biological relationship, for which the petitioner is ready. Hence, the court may be pleased to order a DNA test on the actor and me (Kathiresan)," the couple's side said. Opposing it, the actor's side said that DNA test could not be ordered casually. "There should be a prima facie materials for it, based on which the court must come to conclusion," the actor's side said. The judge said a petition seeking a DNA test had been filed in the last moment. The judge asked the couple's side, "Why didn't you file a petition seeking DNA test at the beginning?" Then, the judge stayed the proceedings before the magistrate court until further orders. The case has been adjourned to March 9.
