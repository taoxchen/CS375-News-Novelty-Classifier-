
In a rather sad turn of events, filmmaker Mahesh Bhatt has received death threats over the phone. He filed a police complaint on Wednesday after he received death threats for daughter Alia Bhatt and wife Soni Razdan. 
Reports claim that an unidentified person called Mahesh Bhatt and demanded Rs 50 lakh from him. Apart from demanding money, the caller also threatened to kill the whole family, if he fails to fulfill their monetary demands. In November 2014, the Bhatts had received similar threats but Police later managed to arrest culprits. 

Reports further claim that the phone call was first made on February 26 and the man ordered Mahesh Bhatt to deposit the money in a Lucknow-based bank branch. India Today reported that when Bhatt first received the call, he chose to ignore it but later he received a Whatsapp message from the person who claimed to be a gang leader. The message read, “If you fail to do so, I will fire several rounds of bullets in your daughter Alia and wife Soni.” 
After this message, some more texts were sent to Mahesh Bhatt. Mahesh Bhatt’s statement has been recorded in the matter and a complaint has been filed in Juhu Police Station in Mumbai. 

Bhatt took to Twitter to thank the Police for their prompt call-to-action. A bolt from the blue in the form of an extortion call & threat to my family was nipped in the bud by the MH & UP police in tandem.Gratitude! 
 An India Today report quoted a Police Officer saying, “A case is registered under section 387 (causing a person to fear death or grievous hurt, in order to commit extortion).” 

The police investigation is going on in full-swing and the security for Mahesh, Alia and Soni has been beefed up.
