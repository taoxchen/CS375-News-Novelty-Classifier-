
Alia Bhatt calls her dad Mahesh Bhatt and police brave. She says she is not worried as the case has been solved now. For your update, we like to inform you that Mahesh Bhatt has recently received extortion call from an unknown caller, demanding Rs 50 lakhs rupees. 
The 23-year-old star, who is busy promoting her upcoming film ‘Badrinath Ki Dulhania’ these days, said that her father by her side has made her feel safe. 
"I think the police and my dad are brave they did not give me a chance to get worried. They just took over. The matter is sorted and solved and everybody is safe and happy. As a parent my father has made me feel safe. With him by my side I had nothing to worry about," Alia told reporters. The caller demanded Rs 50 lakhs to spare Alia and Soni and also informed Mahesh Bhatt through text messages where to deposit the money. The caller said that he will pump his daughter Alia Bhatt and wife Soni Razdan with bullets if he demand is not fulfilled. On Wednesday night, the filmmaker lodged complaint at the police station and within 24 hours, police was successful in arresting the culprit. 
Police took quick action and they were able to arrest the caller in no time after the accused sent his account details to the filmmaker. A senior official said on the condition of anonymity, "The accused messaged his account details. We managed to track him through his mobile phone number. He is in custody." 
Mahesh Bhatt also tweeted, "A bolt from the blue in the form of an extortion call & threat to my family was nipped in the bud by the MH & UP police in tandem.Gratitude!" 
The certain caller named Sandeep Sahu and is currently under police custody. 
FIR was lodged on Wednesday night and police recorded the statements of Mahesh Bhatt and his family members. 
Mumbai police sources said that Mahesh Bhatt received a phone call from an individual, who demanded 50 lakhs from the filmmaker. 
Initially, Bhatt thought it was a prank but when the caller later sent him texts and WhatsApp messages and told him that not to take the threats lightly, it raised alarm. 
He said if his instructions weren't followed, he would kill Bhatt, Alia and Soni. His exact words were: "If you fail to do so, I will fire several rounds of bullets in your daughter Alia and wife Soni." 
He continued to send a few more messages but later he stopped. 
Bhatt was asked to deposit the money in a Lucknow-based bank branch. This incident took place late on the night of February 26. 
Mahesh Bhatt immediately decided to inform the cops since the life of his wife and daughter were in danger. 
The case was registered by Juhu police and transferred to the Anti Extortion Cell for further investigation. 
A police officer told India Today exclusively, "A case is registered under section 387 (causing a person to fear death or grievous hurt, in order to commit extortion.)" After the threatening call, security of the Bhatts had been beefed up. Two years ago, the Bhatt’s family received death threats from a certain D gang.
