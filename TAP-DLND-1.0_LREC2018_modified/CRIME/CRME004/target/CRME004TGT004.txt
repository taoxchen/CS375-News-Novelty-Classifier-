
An unidentified individual called filmmaker Mahesh Bhatt and demanded a sum of Rs 50 lakh and if Bhatt fails to pay the ransom, the caller has threatened to kill his daughter Alia Bhatt and wife, Soni Razdan. 
Mumbai Police’s Anti Extortion Cell is investigating the matter along with other police departments, we have learnt. Reportedly, Mahesh Bhatt received a phone call from an individual claiming to be a gang leader. He initially thought it was a prank. However, when the caller started sending him texts and WhatsApp messages, and told him that not to take the threats lightly, it is then he immediately decided to inform the cops. An FIR has been registered and the case is now transferred to the Anti Extortion Cell. 
The noted filmmaker was asked to deposit the money in a Lucknow-based bank branch. The caller said if his instructions weren’t followed, he would kill Bhatt, Alia and Soni. His exact words were: “If you fail to do so, I will fire several rounds of bullets in your daughter Alia and wife Soni.”
In an interaction with a leading news portal, a police officer (on the condition of anonymity) said, “A case is registered under section 387 (causing a person to fear death or grievous hurt, in order to commit extortion.)” He also said that the case had been taken very seriously, and that security had been beefed up around the Bhatt residence in Juhu. 
This isn’t the first time Mahesh Bhatt has received death threats. In November 2014, over 13 people belonging to a certain D gang had plotted to eliminate Bhatt and his whole family, but were arrested later.
