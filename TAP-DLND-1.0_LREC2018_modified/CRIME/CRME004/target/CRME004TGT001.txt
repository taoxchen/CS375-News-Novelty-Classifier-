
Filmmaker Mahesh Bhatt received a death threat from an unidentified caller who reportedly demanded Rs 50 lakh. The person also threatened to kill Mahesh Bhatt's wife Soni Razdan and daughter Alia Bhatt if he failed to deposit the amount in a Lucknow-based bank branch.
Mahesh Bhatt filed a case in Juhu police station on Wednesday. The case has been registered under section 387 (causing a person to fear death or grievous hurt, in order to commit extortion). The police also recorded a statement from Bhatt's family.
Complaint filed in Juhu Police Station after Mahesh Bhatt receives extortion call,caller also threatened to kill Soni Razdan and Alia Bhatt.
2 March 2017 Mahesh Bhatt took to Twitter on Thursday and wrote, "A bolt from the blue in the form of an extortion call & threat to my family was nipped in the bud by the MH & UP police in tandem.Gratitude!"
