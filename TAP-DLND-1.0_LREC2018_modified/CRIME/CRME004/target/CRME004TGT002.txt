
Earlier in the morning, we told you that an unidentified individual called Mahesh Bhatt and demanded a sum of Rs 50 lakh. He threatened to kill Bhatt, his daughter Alia Bhatt and wife Soni Razdan, if the filmmaker failed to pay the money. Soon after the call, Mahesh filed an FIR with Juhu police, soon after which the case was then handed over to the Anti Extortion Cell for further investigation. 
The latest development is that the caller has been arrested by UP police. Mahesh took to Twitter and thanked the cops for arresting the accused. Here’s what he said: 
A bolt from the blue in the form of an extortion call & threat to my family was nipped in the bud by the MH & UP police in tandem.Gratitude! 
— Mahesh Bhatt (@MaheshNBhatt) March 2, 2017 
According to the reports, Mahesh received a phone call from this individual, who claimed to be a gang leader. who demanded 50 lakhs from the filmmaker. He initially thought it was a prank. However, when the caller started sending him texts and WhatsApp messages, and told him that not to take the threats lightly, it is then he immediately decided to inform the cops.
The noted filmmaker was asked to deposit the money in a Lucknow-based bank branch. The caller said if his instructions weren’t followed, he would kill Bhatt, Alia and Soni. His exact words were: “If you fail to do so, I will fire several rounds of bullets in your daughter Alia and wife Soni.” 
This isn’t the first time something like this has happened with the Bhatt family. Two years ago, over 13 people belonging to a certain D gang had plotted to eliminate the family was arrested in November 2014.
