Bengaluru: 4 detained for groping, robbing woman on street on January 1 Incident came to light after shocking CCTV footage surfaced soon after New Year eve mass molestation.
Bengaluru's Banaswadi police detained four persons for questioning in connection with a groping incident in Kammanahalli area. The incident occurred on January 1 and was captured on a CCTV camera installed at a house near the Kammanahalli 5th Main Road in east Bengaluru. 
The shocking footage surfaced soon after the mass molestation on New Year's Eve and shows a miscreant getting off his two-wheeler, walking towards the girl, molesting her and then dragging her towards his friend who is still on the scooter. When the woman tries to escape, the molester pushes her and rides away. 
Several people can be seen passing by at the end of the street, however none come forward to help the woman. The incident came to light soon after the mass molestation that occurred in Bengaluru on New Year's Eve.
