After the incident of mass molestation of women in Bengaluru on New Year’s eve, the nightmare doesn't seem to get over for women in the city as another such incident from the same day has been caught on the CCTV footage. A video from a security camera has surfaced showing a woman walking in a narrow and deserted street in the Kammanahalli area in East Bengaluru when two men on a scooter come from behind her and stop on the side of the street near her. As the two men get closer to the woman she breaks into a run but one of the men lunges at her. He gropes and molests her as she struggles to fight back, it can be seen in the nightmarish video. Seconds later the molester drags the woman towards his friend who sat on the scooter. She is thrown hard on the ground and both the molesters race away, the video shows. The video also captured the eyewitnesses who did nothing as the woman fought the two men or help the victim as she picked herself off the ground. The shameful incident comes after the state government and the police are under immense criticism for security failure during New Year celebrations in the heart of the city. The mass molestation on New Year’s eve was captured in pictures by a photojournalist showing frightened and panic-stricken women trying to escape their molesters on MG Road.
