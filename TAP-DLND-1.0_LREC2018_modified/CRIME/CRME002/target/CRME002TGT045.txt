 Moved by the episodes of molestation in the city + during the New Year eve and in Kammanahalli, a group of activists have announced to form a human chain and protest against the crimes against women in Bengaluru. A post saying 'Touch Me Not' went viral on Win Bangalore Back Facebook page on Wednesday, wherein, citizens have agreed to protest on January 7, in front of Vidhana Soudha from 11 am to 1 pm.
On New Year's eve in Bengaluru + women were witnesses of mass molestation as the revelry turned into a night of horror for many.
A post by a member is being shared with the announcement of the demonstration says,
"Bangalore, you have remained silent for far too long. Let us wake our CM and everybody sitting in Vidhana Soudha by raising our voice. Spare two hours of your Saturday to step out to protest. Together, we can raise our voice. Saturdays might be your day to relax but ask yourself one question. Are you safe in this city? And no, we will not go to Freedom Park to protest. Instead, we shall raise our slogans before the giant structure that promises to protect people.
To the government condemning incidents but doing nothing about it, we want to ask you -
If Nirbhaya's incident was not enough for you to act, then what is? If the pictures and videos are not your evidence, then what is? If a woman's horrifying testimony is not strong enough to call for action, then what is?"
The poster urges citizens to join them in the protest.
