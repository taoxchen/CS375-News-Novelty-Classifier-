
Days after shocking reports of mass molestation in the streets of Bengaluru on the eve of New Year, another horrifying video footage has emerged in which two men are seen molesting a woman on a street in the IT capital. 
The video, captured by a CCTV camera installed at a house off Kammanahalli 5th Main Road in East Bengaluru, shows that the woman who just got off an auto-rickshaw was intercepted by two scooter-borne men before she was groped and molested by one of them. 
As she resists, the footage shows, the man drags her towards his partner and pins her down. As she continues to fight them, the duo throw her on the road and escapes.
