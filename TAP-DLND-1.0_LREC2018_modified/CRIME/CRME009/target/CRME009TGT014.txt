Indrani and Peter Mukerjea have been charged with murder and conspiracy In the sensational murder of Sheena Bora, her mother Indrani Mukerjea and step-father Peter Mukerjea have been charged with murder and conspiracy. Indrani Mukerjea's former husband Sanjeev Khanna has also been charged by a special CBI court. Indrani's driver Shyamvar Rai, the fourth accused, had turned approver for the CBI earlier. The CBI has alleged that Sheena Bora was murdered by her mother over a property dispute. Peter Mukerjea, says the investigating agency, was aware of the murder plan throughout. Indrani Mukerjea is accused of strangling Sheena, 24, inside a car just outside Mumbai, with help from her husband and driver. The half-burnt body was found in 2015 from forests near Mumbai. In August that year, Indrani was arrested. The lawyers of both Indrani and Peter Mukherjea have argued that none of the charges can be proved by the CBI.
