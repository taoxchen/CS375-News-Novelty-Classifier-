
Almost four years after the murder of 24-year-old Sheena Bora, her mother Indrani Mukerjea was charged with murder, kidnapping and criminal conspiracy. 
On Tuesday, a special CBI court also framed charges against media executive Peter Mukerjea and Ms. Mukerjea's first husband Sanjeev Khanna for the murder. 
The three were charged with punishment for murder, kidnapping or abducting in order to murder, punishment for criminal conspiracy and acts done with common intention. 
Ms. Mukerjea and Mr. Khanna are charged with attempt to murder and punishment for criminal conspiracy for plotting to kill Mekhail, Sheena's younger brother along with her. 
Ms. Mukerjea, who is currently lodged at the Byculla jail, has also been charged for using a forged document and for cheating and forgery. 
All the three accused pleaded not guilty and Judge H.S. Mahajan adjourned the case to February 1. 
On April 24, 2012, Sheena Bora's partially burnt body was found in the forests of Maharashtra’s Raigad District.
