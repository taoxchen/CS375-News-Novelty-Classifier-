A special CBI court framed charges against Indrani Mukerjea, her husband Peter Mukerjea and former husband Sanjeev Khanna in the Sheena Bora murder case. 
Indrani is the prime accused in the murder of her daughter Sheena Bora. Her husband and former media baron Peter Mukerjea is also charged in the 2012 murder case. 
Sheena was murdered on April 24, 2012, but the crime came to light after the arrest of Rai in another case in August 2015.
