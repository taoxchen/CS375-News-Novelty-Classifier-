Footballer Cristiano Ronaldo of Portugal received FIFA’s best player award for the fourth time on Monday. Ronaldo’s 2016 global trophycompoments included the European Championship, Champions League, and Club World Cup, plus individual best player awards from UEFA and France Football magazine. Atletico Nacional win the FIFA Fair play award for their amazing gesture following the Chapecoense disaster in which 71 players were killed in a plane crash. They decided to award the title to Chapecoense. Borussia Dortmund and Liverpool supporters get the FIFA Fan award.
