Ronaldo saw off competition from Barcelona’s Lionel Messi and Atletico Madrid’s Antoine Griezmann to win the award, capping a fine year for the 31-year-old. American Carli Lloyd won the Best Women's Player award. The Real Madrid all-time record goalscorer helped his club side to the Champions League crown before lifting the European Championship trophy with Portugal in the summer. And those achievements, plus some astonishing stats for the year, saw him sweep the board in the major end of year award ceremonies, with a fourth Ballon d'Or being the highlight. 
Ronaldo and his fourth Ballon d'Or AFP FIFA's previous partnership with France Football saw their involvement with the Ballon d'Or come to an end in 2015, but the sport's governing body have honoured Ronaldo at their own ceremony in the Swiss capital at an event hosted by Eva Longoria and Marco Schreyl. Messi and the rest of the Barcelona players did not attend the ceremony, with the Argentine, Luis Suarez, Gerard Pique and Andres Iniesta all absent despite, being named in the FIFA World XI.
