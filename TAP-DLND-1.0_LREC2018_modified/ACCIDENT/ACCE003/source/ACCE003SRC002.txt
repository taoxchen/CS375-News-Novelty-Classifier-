 Fifteen young children were killed and twenty five others seriously injured this morning when their school bus crashed into a truck in Uttar Pradesh's Etah. The truck was speeding when the school bus, with more than 60 students, collided with it.The children on the bus were junior school students between seven and ten years old.Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed grief in tweets. "Anguished by the tragic accident in UP's Etah district. I share the pain of the bereaved families & condole passing away of young children," tweeted the Prime Minister.
