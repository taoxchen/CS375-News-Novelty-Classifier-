
More than 24 schoolchildren died and over 40 injured as a school bus collided with a lorry in Aliganj of Uttar Pradesh Etah district on Thursday. 
Uttar Pradesh Director General of Police Javeed Ahmad said the school was open against the order of the district administration to close schools due to cold. 
“Strict action will be taken against the school,” he said. 
The DGP said rescue operations are underway and the priority is to rescue trapped children. 
Meanwhile, Prime Minister Narendra Modi condoled the loss of innocent lives in the tragic incident. 
‘Anguished by the tragic accident in UP’s Etah district. I share the pain of the bereaved families & condole passing away of young children (sic),’ the PM tweeted. 
‘I pray that those injured in the accident in Etah recover at the earliest,’ he said in another tweet.
