
At least fifteen school children died and over 30 were injured after a bus collided with a truck in Uttar Pradesh's Etah today. 
According to reports, more than 50 school children of JS Vidya School were travelling when the bus collided with a truck. 
"Tragic road accident in Aliganj Etah. Over 15 school kids feared dead. Rescue of injured ongoing," UP DGP Javed Ahmed tweeted soon after the accident.
