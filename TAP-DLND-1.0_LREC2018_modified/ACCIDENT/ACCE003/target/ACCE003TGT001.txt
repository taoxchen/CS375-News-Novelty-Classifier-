In a major tragedy, at least 24 schoolchildren are feared to have died and over 30 seriously injured when a school bus collided with a truck in Aliganj in Etah district of Uttar Pradesh (UP), some 290 km from Lucknow Thursday morning. Local reports said some children were still trapped in the wreckage and the state’s director-general of police said the administration’s priority was to rescue those trapped. 
According to initial reports, bus belonging to J S Vidya Public School collided head-on with a truck while carrying over 50 children, ranging from kindergartners to class 7 students, to school. 
“At least 10 school children with grievous injuries have been taken to a super speciality hospital in Saifai while others with less serious injuries have been sent to hospitals in Aligarh and Agra districts,” the district magistrate of Etah said to media persons. 
Local sources said there was thick fog over Aliganj this morning and the district administration had ordered the closure of schools. 
“But the management of J S Vidya Public School decided to have classes in violation of the district magistrate’s orders,” said director-general of police (DGP) Javeed Ahmad. “Strict action will be taken against the school,” he stated. 
The district magistrate of Etah has directed the police to lodge an FIR against the school management for flouting the standing orders and initiated action to have the school’s recognition school cancelled. 
Chief minister Akhilesh Yadav has directed senior officials to visit the accident spot and ensure that hospitals render free treatment to the injured children. 
Meanwhile, Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Union home minister Rajnath Singh have tweeted their grief over the death of the victims. 
"Anguished by the tragic accident in UP’s Etah. I share the pain of the bereaved families and condole passing away of young children. I pray that those injured in the accident in Etah recover at the earliest," PM Modi said.
