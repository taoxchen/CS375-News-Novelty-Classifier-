 At least 24 young children have died after a truck loaded with sand collided with a school bus in India. 
The accident, in the northern Indian state of Uttar Pradesh, also injured at least two dozen other youngsters. 
The truck, said to be travelling at speed, collided head-on with the bus which then overturned, according to reports. 
The children, between the ages of three and 12, were studying at a school in the town of Etah. 
Police officer Daljeet Chaudhry said visibility was poor because of dense fog on Thursday. 
Some children were trapped in the wreckage but officials said everyone has now been rescued. 
Police are to investigate why the school had remained open when the state's education authorities announced all schools in the district were to close due to severe cold weather. 
Prime Minister Narendra Modi tweeted his condolences, saying: "Anguished by tragic accident in UP's Etah. I share the pain of the bereaved families & condole (the) passing away of young children." 
India has an appalling record for road accidents because of its poorly maintained roads, reckless driving and ageing vehicles.
