India school bus crash kills 15 pupils 
At least 15 Indian children have been killed and 45 others injured after a school bus they were travelling in collided with a truck in the northern state of Uttar Pradesh. The crash happened on Thursday morning in Etah, around 265km (164 miles) from the state's capital, Lucknow. A senior police officer told the BBC that 14 students "were in a critical condition".
