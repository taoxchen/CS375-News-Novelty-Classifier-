At least 25 schoolchildren have been killed and 30 other injured after a bus collided with a truck in Uttar Pradesh’s Etah on Thursday. The ghastly accident reportedly took place due to the poor visibility in the area. The truck is said to be speeding when it collided head-on with the bus which was ferrying around 60 children of JS Vidya School aged between 7 and 10. Many of the injured are said to be in critical condition and eyewitnesses told that it took about one hour to remove the bodies from the mangled school bus. Expressing grief over the accident, Prime Minister Narendra Modi wrote in his tweets, "Anguished by the tragic accident in UP's Etah district. I share the pain of the bereaved families & condole passing away of young children." "I pray that those injured in the accident in Etah recover at the earliest," PM Modi wrote in another tweet.
