ANI reported that at least 5 people have been killed in the accident which took place in Khatauli, a town which about 40 km away from Muzaffarnagar of Uttar Pradesh. The report says that at least six coaches went off track and over 30 people have been reported injured in the incident. The train was going to en-route to Puri with its arrival time scheduled at 9 PM. The incident took place at around 6:45 PM.
