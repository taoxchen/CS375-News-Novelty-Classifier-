The Railways authorities today terminated the services of 13 railway employees for gross negligence that led to the August 19 Puri-Haridwar Kalinga-Utkal Express train derailment at Khatauli in Uttar Pradesh in which more than 20 people were killed. 
Among those sacked are 11 gangmen, one ironsmith and a junior engineer, the railway ministry communi said. 
All of them were workering under the Kathauli section where the accident took place when 14 coaches of the train jumped off the tracks. 
The railways has terminated the services of 13 employees under Section 14 of the discipline and appeal rule, said Northern Railway spokesperson Neeraj Sharma. 
Under this section, the services of any rail staff found guilty of gross negligence can be terminated without an enquiry. 
The railway had earlier sent on leave, three top officials, including a secretary-level Railway Board officer, suspended four officers and transferred one in connection with the derailment. 
Even the Railways Minister Suresh Prabhu had offered to resign owing moral responsibility for the manmade tragedy while the Prime Minister Narendra Modi asked him to wait for some more time.
