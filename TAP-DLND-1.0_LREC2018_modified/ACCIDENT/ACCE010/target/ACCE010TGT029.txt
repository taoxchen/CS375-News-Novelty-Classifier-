
At least Five people were feared killed and over 50 others, including women and children, were injured, when Haridwar bound Utkal Express train derailed in Uttar Pradesh's Muzaffarnagar district, about 500 kilometres from here, on Saturday.

Eyewitness accounts claimed that five passengers were killed in the mishap. Additional director general of UP police, law and order, Anand Kumar told reporters here that a large number of passengers were injured in the mishap. He also said that some people had been killed but refused to specify the number of casualties.

Union minister Sanjiv Baliyan, who hailed from Muzaffarnagar, said that all the injured have been admitted to hospitals. Baliyan said that some passengers were killed in the mishap.

''There may be five..six...ten dead....passengers are still trapped in side the coaches,'', he added.

Police sources here said that the mishap occurred near Khatauli railway station, which 22 kilometres from the district headquarters, around six in the evening. The train was scheduled to reach Haridwar, a pilgrimage town in Uttarakhand around nine in the evening.

Sources said that six coaches of the train, which was on its way to Haridwar in Uttarakhand from Puri in Odisha, derailed near the outer cabin near Khatauli. One of the coaches also rammed into the houses, which were close to the railway tracks.

Officials said that medical teams had been rushed to the spot from Meerut, Saharanpur and other nearby districts. Ambulances have also been rushed to the spot, sources said.

The injured were admitted to the nearby hospitals in Meerut and Muzaffarnagar, the officials said adding that hospitals in the NCR region had been put on alert following the mishap. Some of the injured were stated to be in a critical condition.

Sources quoting eye witnesses said that some of the derailed coaches had climbed on other coaches. Gas cutters were being pressed into service to cut through the mangled coaches to pull out the passengers, who were trapped inside the derailed coaches.

Sources said that over one hundred passengers were pulled out of the derailed coaches so far. Locals were the first ones to reach the spot and rescue the passengers.

NDRF team had also rushed to the spot to assist in the relief and rescue operations, officials here said.

Officials here said that the Anti-Terrorist Squad (ATS) team of the UP police had also rushed to the spot to investigate if sabotage was behind the mishap.
