Several coaches of the Puri-Haridwar-Kalinga Utkal Express derailed in Muzaffarnagar's Khatauli on Saturday.
ADG law and order Anand Kumar has confirmed 10 deaths, and said that at least 40 people have been seriously injured in the accident.

"An inquiry has been ordered to ascertain the cause of the accident. Strict action will be taken against any lapse," railway minister Suresh Prabhu said.
"Medical vans have been rushed to the site. All efforts are being taken to ensure speedy relief and rescue operations," Prabhu tweeted from his official handle.

Calling it a sad incident, Uttar Pradesh chief minister Yogi Adityanath assured that the injured passengers will be given complete treatment. He added that directions have been issued for all possible assistance.
