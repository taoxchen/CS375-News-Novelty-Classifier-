In response, Prabhu said he was thankful to Nadda for offering the medical relief to the injured. Union Minister Rajyavardhan Singh Rathore also expressed grief on Twitter over the incident and urged all to join in praying for the families of the victims. In a major train accident, at least 23 passengers were killed and many others injured when 14 coaches of the Puri- Haridwar Utkal Express jumped the rails in Khatauli near Muzaffarnagar this evening, officials said.
