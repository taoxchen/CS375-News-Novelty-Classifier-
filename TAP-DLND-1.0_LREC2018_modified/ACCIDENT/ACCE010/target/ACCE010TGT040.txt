 Five coaches of the Puri-Haridwar Utkal Express derailed near Muzzafarnagar in western Uttar Pradesh this evening, injuring several people. The accident took place in Khatauli near Muzzafarnagar, about 115 kms from Delhi. Railway ministry officials said they are still awaiting details of the accident that took place around 5:45 pm. Aug 19, 2017 National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) teams have mobilised for rescue operations.  Medical vans have been rushed to the site.
