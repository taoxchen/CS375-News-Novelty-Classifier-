In an unfortunate incident, at least 5 coaches of Puri Haridwar Kalinga Utkal Express were derailed near Khatauli in Muzaffarnagar district of Uttar Pradesh. At least 5 persons have been reported dead, according to various media reports. However, official word on deaths is yet to come. Moreover, at least 34 people are reportedly injured in the train mishap. Rail minister Suresh Prabhu is personally monitoring the situation. Moreover, UP CM Yogi Adityanath has also expressed concern over the incident. Here are things to know about the unfortunate incident:-
5 coaches of Puri Haridwar Kalinga Utkal Express were derailed near Khatauli in Muzaffarnagar district of Uttar Pradesh.
The accident happened at 5.50 PM on Saturday.
The accident happened due to the derailment. However, the exact cause of the derailment is yet to be ascertained.
 At least 5 passengers have died so far.
At least 34 people were injured in the mishap.
The train was scheduled to reach Haridwar at 9 PM.
Moreover, media reports are claiming that the government is also probing terror angle (if any) in the mishap.
