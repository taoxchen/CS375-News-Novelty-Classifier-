
At least twenty-three passengers were killed and over 40 were injured when 12 bogies of the Haridwar-bound 18477 Kalinga Utkal Express (Puri-Haridwar) derailed near Khatauli Railways Station in Muzzafarnagar district of Uttar Pradesh on Saturday evening. 
The incident took place at 5.50 pm, about 200 metres from Khatauli Railway Station on Meerut-Saharapur section. A Railway spokesperson said that their main focus was on rescuing those trapped and rushing the injured to a local hospital. 
However, before the railways could reach, locals started the rescue operations by helping out trapped passengers. The impact of the accident was so severe that the tracks were uprooted at several places. The impact also resulted in the train’s S2 bogey jumping over other bogies and hitting a house near the tracks causing severe damage. Residents of the damaged house managed to escape, as one of the family members witnessed the derailment and called for everyone to run inside.  
An eyewitness told DNA that the tracks had recently been fixed, and new tracks demand trains travel at a desired speed limit to ensure safety. However, trains continued travelling at full speed. The tracks were recently repaired and we had requested the railways to put up a board directing the trains to slow down, as our houses were near the tracks. However, they didn’t pay heed, alleged Ratan Kumar, a local who lives nearby. 
Passengers who survived claimed that the driver of the train had applied the emergency break, when the train was travelling at a high speed for some unknown reasons. The SSP Muzffarnagar confirmed deaths of 20 passengers, adding that over 40 were injured. He said that 65 ambulances were pressed to take injured to the hospital. Local administration meanwhile had provided food and water to the stranded passengers and arranged buses to take them to their destination. 
“Only a few passengers remained trapped. Bogies are being cut and they would be pulled out soon,�? the SSP said, adding that the death toll could increase as several people sustained severe injuries. 
Although officials have not declared whether the attack had a terror angle to it, a team of the Uttar Pradesh Anti-Terrorism squad under SSP Anoop Singh was rushed to Khatauli from Noida to conduct probe. The National Disaster Relief Fund, railway relief team and medical vans were rushed to the spot. 
Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath has issued directives to the state home secretary and the director general of police to personally supervise the rescue and relief operations. The Inspector General of Railways has also been rushed to the spot. 
While speaking to ANI, Adityanath said that he had deputed his cabinet ministers Satish Mahana and Suresh Rana to ensure that the injured received the best possible medical attention, and those rescued have no trouble in reaching their destinations. The Adityanath government also announced a compensation of Rs 2 lakh to families of the deceased, and Rs 50,000 to each of injured. 
The Ministry of Railways also announced that the kin of the deceased will get Rs 3.5 lakh, while those with major injuries would receive Rs 50,000. The accident has resulted in the diversion of two dozen trains. This has affected the Meerut-Shahranpur-Ambala route.
