
Patna: The Bihar Police on Tuesday pointed to the involvement of Pakistan's notorious spy agency Inter Services Intelligence in the recent train mishaps including Ajmer-Sialdah Express derailment near Kanpur. 
According to reports, the Motihari unit of the Bihar Police, which was investigating a terror-related incident, recently arrested one person who has made startling disclosures confirming that Kanpur train tragedy was actually an act of terror, sponsored by the ISI.

Moti Paswan – the man in question – had revealed that he was paid by the ISI to plant bomb along the rail tracks to trigger derailment of trains. 
Paswan was arrested along with two others for planting an Improvised Explosive Device (IED) on tracks at the Ghorasahan Railway Station in East Champaran. 
Paswan had told interrogators that besides him, several others from Kanpur including Zubair and Zia-ul, who were nabbed from Delhi, were involved in the Ajmer-Sealdah Express train derailment near Rura railway station in Kanpur dehat district last month.

Confirming the development, East Champaran Superintendent of Police Jitendra Rana said, ''We have arrested three persons in connection with the IED recovered on Ghorasahan railway track on Oct 1 2016.'' 
Paswan further revealed that the ISI had hatched a conspiracy to blow the track at Ghorasahan Station for which its agent Brij Kishore Giri – the man who was later arrested from Nepal – had given Rs 3 lakh to Arun and Deepak Ram, both residents of Adapur in Bihar. 
Paswan further told that both Arun and Deepak Ram were later called to Nepal and murdered by Giri as ISI was upset with them for not triggering the IED device even after planted it on the track. 
Their bodies were thrown in the river, he claimed.

Based on its findings, the East Champaran Police intensified its probe and arrested Uma Shankar Prasad and Mukesh Yadav and confirmed their links with ISI. 
Arrested persons were given more targets and a probe is underway to find their links with Kanpur train accident, said the East Champaran SP. 
Meanwhile, Nepal Police had confirmed to Bihar Police that Pakistan's ISI had paid Rs 30 lakh to its agent Brij Kishore Giri for triggering blasts on rail tracks targeting popular trains in Bihar. 
Accused were paid to blow rail tracks at Ghorasahan. Their links in Nepal, Dubai and other places are being probed, SP Jitendra Rana added. 
All the accused have been interrogated by the Intelligence Bureau and the information extracted by them has been passed on to the RAW and military intelligence for further verification. 
At least least two people were killed and 43 injured when 15 coaches of the Ajmer-Sealdah Express train derailed near Rura railway station in Kanpur dehat district in December last year. 
Fourteen coaches of the train, 12987 Ajmer-Sealdah Express, derailed when it was crossing a bridge over a dry canal in the area. 
Earlier, a major train accident involving Indore-Patna Express took place which claimed the lives of at least 150 people at Pokhrayan near Kanpur
