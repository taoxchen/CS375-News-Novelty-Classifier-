 At least 28 people were killed Monday when a Turkish Airlines cargo plane crashed into a residential area close to the airport in Bishkek , capital of Kyrgyzstan , according to reports by local news agencies.
News agency AKIpress news said the crash involved a Boeing 747 cargo plane, which plunged into a residential area close to the airport, destroying at least 15 houses.
At least eight people have also been injured and taken to hospitals in the capital, said Health Minister Talantbek Batyraliyev, as cited by AKIpress.
