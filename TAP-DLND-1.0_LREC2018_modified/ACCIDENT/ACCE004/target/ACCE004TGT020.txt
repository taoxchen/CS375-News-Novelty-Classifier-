
A small plane hascrashed into a Riverside, Calif. home Monday afternoon. 
Firefighters were seen trying to put out the fire caused by the crash. 
Officials said the crash happened before 5 pm, according to KABC-T V . 
 Four people were reported missing and one was taken to a hospital in critical condition, fire officials told the station.  
A witness told the Daily News she saw ambulances carry three people away from the scene. 
Their conditions weren't immediately known.
