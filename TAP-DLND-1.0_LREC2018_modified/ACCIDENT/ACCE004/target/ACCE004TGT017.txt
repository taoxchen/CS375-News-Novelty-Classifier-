 
A small plane has crashed into homes in Riverside, California, USA, with at least four people reported missing. 

At least three homes were hit, with up to four people missing, fire officials told local media . It was not clear if they were referring to people on the ground or in the plane, which was reported to be a Cessna. 
Emergency services at the scene of the plane crash in California. 
One person was rushed to the hospital in critical condition. Firefighters were seen pulling a person from one of the damaged homes.

Nearby homes were being evacuated, local media reported. 
"The building I was in shook like a very short, small earthquake," H.L Reyes said on Twitter. No details on injuries or deaths were yet available.
