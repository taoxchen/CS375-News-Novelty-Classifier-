
A day after boat tragedy in Patna, a stampede has happened in Gangasagar in West Bengal. 
Six persons have been killed and 15 others have been injured in the stampede that took place near Kochuberia in Sagar Islands. 
Stampede took place on jetty number 5 while people were boarding the boat. 
Initial reports suggest that three people are seriously injured. 
But, the number of injured people could go up. More details are awaited.
