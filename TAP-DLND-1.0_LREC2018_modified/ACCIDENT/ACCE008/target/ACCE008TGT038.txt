At least five dead in stampede at Gangasagar fair Several were injured in the rush on a jetty in Kachuberia, South24 Parganas district.
At least five people died in a stampede at a fair in Gangasagar, which is in Kachuberia, West Bengal, on Sunday. However, reports said the toll is likely to rise. Several people were also injured in the rush on a jetty, News18 reported. Those injured have been moved to a local hospital. 
The incident comes a day after a boat capsized in the Ganga river in Bihar , killing at least 24 people.
