
At least six people died while 15 others were injured in a stampede that happened at a jetty at the Gangasagar Mela in West Bengal on Sunday. The death toll is expected to rise. All the six deceased were middle-aged women and have not been identified yet. 
The stampede happened around 6 pm when people were trying to get on a boat bound for Kolkata from Jetty Number 5 of the Kachuberia area in South 24 Parganas. All the six pilgrims died due to suffocation when a huge crowd of pilgrims were trying to board the boat after taking their holy dip. 
"Six aged women died at Kachuberia temporary hospital due to heart attack. They had fallen senseless due to suffocation. The mishap occurred as the pilgrims got impatient due to the long queue at jetty number five in Kachuberi and tried to outdo others in boarding the vessel," ," Manturam Pakhira, state Sundarbans development minister, told IANS. 
At least 10 people have been hospitalised, ANI reported. 
The officials said that naval divers have launched a search operation to find out anybody who might have fallen into the river during the stampede, the Indian Express reported. 
Prime Minister Narendra Modi expressed grief over the incident and also offered his condolences to the bereaved families. He also wished a speedy recovery for the injured. The Prime Minister's Office on Twitter announced an ex-gratia of Rs 2 lakh to the kin of the deceased and Rs 50,000 for those injured in the stampede. West Bengal Public Health Engineering and Rural Development Minister Subrata Mukherjee said that as many as 16 lakh pilgrims took their holy dip in two days on the occasion of Makar Sankranti at the Gangasagar fair. 
The event in also known as Ganga Snan and is held every year during Makar Sankranti at the Sagar Island or the Sagardwip in West Bengal. Hindu pilgrims come together to take a holy dip in River Ganga before she merges with the Bay of Bengal.
