A stampede in eastern India killed six women on Sunday as devotees returning from a religious festival rushed to get onto a jetty, a minister said. Some 1.5 million people had assembled at Gangasagar in West Bengal state to take a holy dip in the river on the occasion of the Hindu harvest festival of Makar Sakranti. The devotees were on their way back from the two-day festival when the stampede broke out, local minister Manturam Pakhira told AFP.
