Deaths at NTPC power station raise concerns over workers' safety
An explosion at an Indian coal-fired power plant has killed 18 people and injured up to 100 others in the northern state of Uttar Pradesh.
The government-run firm, National Thermal Power Corporation ( NTPC ), said the death toll had risen to 29 by late Thursday.
"Hot flue gases and steam let out by the blast severely injured several workers", Sanjay Kumar Khatri, the top government official of Rae Bareli district where the plant is located, told Reuters on Thursday.
