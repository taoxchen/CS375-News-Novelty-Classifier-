
At least nine bogies of a Bhubaneswar-bound express train derailed around 30 km from Rayagada station late on Saturday night. 
According to reports, the bogies of 18448 Jagdalpur-Bhubaneswar Hirakhand Express derailed near Kuneru station in Andhra Pradesh in which at least five passengers were feared dead. The casualty is likely to rise. 
Sources said that over 100 passengers were trapped beneath the coaches. The S7 and S8 coaches were badly affected. 
The derailment took place at around 11.20 pm. Two AC, four sleeper, two unreserved coaches, luggage van and the engine jumped the tracks. 
“The accident took place in Vizianagaram district of Andhra Pradesh. Nine bogies of the ill-fated train got affected. We don’t have confirmed number of casualties. However, casualties are feared. The cause of the accident is not yet known. Further investigation would reveal the cause of the mishap,�? a senior railway official said 
ECoR sources said four accident relief vans and officials were rushed from various locations to the spot for rescue operations. Medical relief vans were rushed from Visakhapatnam and Rayagada to provide first-aid to the injured. 
The grievously injured were rushed to Rayagada District Headquarters Hospital with the help of district administration officials, police and local residents.
