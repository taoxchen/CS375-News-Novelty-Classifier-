Train Derails In Indian State Andhra Pradesh; 2 Dozen Dead 
About two dozen people have been killed and ton others injured in India after derail of seven coaches of Jagdalpur-Bhubaneswar Hirakhand Express in Vizianagaram district of Andhra Pradesh state at around 11 p.m. local time on Saturday. 
Chief PRO of East Coast Railway JP Mishra said, the engine as well as luggage van, two general coaches, two sleeper coaches, one AC three tier coach and one AC two tier coach of 18448 Jagdalpur-Bhubaneswar Express derailed near Kuneru station. 
Mishra confirmed 23 people were killed. 
The wounded have been hospitalized at Parbatipuram and Rayagada.
